# F-0102 — Deliveries: one plan, one agent, one gate for several small items across the backlog

Epic: E-0002 — ASF 0.2 — self-improvement. Branch: `spec/F-0102`.

Every item pays the same fixed cost before its code. A Feature pays a spec session and a plan
session on the heavy model, then one coder session per Task, and each session loads the repo and
the brief, runs a gate and a harvest. The code itself is the cheap part. An S3 Bug pays worse than
that: `bug_rows` only emits a row for `S1`/`S2` (`asf/feeder/rows.py:218`), so an S3 Bug has no
lane at all and waits for a Feature to adopt it.

F-0086 already batches, but only *Tasks*, only *inside one Feature*, and it says why it stops
there: "two Features have two plans, two specs and two acceptance sets. Merging across them would
give one branch two owners" (`docs/specs/f-0086.md`, Out). This card is the operator's decision to
give that branch one owner on purpose.

A **Delivery** is several small decided items — small Features, S2/S3 Bugs, small Stories — that
share a footprint, carried by **one plan document, one branch, one coder session, one gate and one
landing**. The items skip their own spec and plan. The coder makes **one commit per item**, and it
is that commit which closes the item, through the evidence path the record already has
(`asf/record/ingest.py:369-380`, `asf/evidence/evidence.py:794-814`). An item the delivery could
not finish is left out of the branch, the rest land, and the groom releases it back into its own
lane the next day.

## Decisions

### In

- A **selection rule** — no model, no prose — over the record: `asf/groom/delivery.py`. It reads
  decided, open, unblocked, small items that declare a `writes:` footprint, groups them by
  overlapping writes or a shared area, and cuts each group to a budget of items, globs and
  estimated tokens.
- A fifth groom proposal section, `Deliveries proposed`, with a fifth verb, `deliver`, in the
  F-0086 proposal grammar (`asf/groom/groom.py:34`). `yes` forms the delivery; `no` declines it
  and suppresses that proposal.
- Two typed fields, written only by the groom's apply:
  - `delivers: [<id>, …]` on the **lead** — every member in build order, the lead first;
  - `delivered_by: <lead>` on every other member.
- Two feeder row kinds, emitted by a new `delivery_rows` over the leads:
  `DELIVERY → PLAN` (one heavy session, branch `plan/<lead>`) and `DELIVERY → CODE` (one light
  session, branch `worker/<lead>`). Every other member gets a non-launching
  `WAITS ON delivery <lead>` row, and neither `feature_rows` nor `bug_rows` speaks for a member
  again.
- The delivery's footprint is the **union** of its open members' `writes:`. It is checked against
  the running footprints like any Task's, and it holds that union while its session runs.
- Two brief kinds and two templates: `delivery-plan` (heavy) and `delivery-code` (light). The
  preamble renders one block per member — its type, title, `writes:`, `## Acceptance` and, for a
  Bug, its `## Fix` — and the brief's card digest folds every member's digest, so a member card
  that changes stales the brief (F-0090 D4).
- **No Task cards are minted.** A delivery's plan lists its items, not `### Task N:` headings, and
  neither brief kind needs a `BACKLOG_ID_RANGE`.
- `asf ingest` stages a delivery lead of **any type** off its own plan document, through the ladder
  a Feature already uses (`evidence.feature_stage` with no Tasks): `card` → `plan-draft` →
  `plan-review rN` → `plan-approved`. That stage is what tells the feeder whether to plan or to
  build.
- Harvest's lane refusal, for a delivery branch, accepts a commit subject that names **any** member
  of the delivery instead of the branch's one item, and names in its landing line which members got
  a commit and which did not. A member with no commit is not a refusal: the branch lands without
  it.
- A groom release rule: once a delivery has landed something, a member it landed without is
  released — `delivered_by:` cleared, the id dropped from the lead's `delivers:`, one History line
  on each card — and the feeder gives it its own row again on the next tick.
- `asf check` validates the two fields: every id in `delivers:` exists, points back with
  `delivered_by:`, and belongs to no second delivery; no card carries both fields.
- The rollup gains a `## Delivered vs single` section: landed items, sessions per landed item, USD
  per landed item, median hours to land and the failed-session share, for the delivered group and
  the single group side by side.
- Four conventions with documented defaults: `delivery_max_items` (4, and `0` turns deliveries
  off for a product), `delivery_max_globs` (6), `delivery_max_tokens` (60000), and the reuse of
  F-0086's `batch_max_globs` (2) as the size floor.

### Out

- **Minting a new item type.** A Delivery is not a card. `TYPES` (`asf/record/core.py:14-22`) has
  no free letter that reads as one, `metrics.ID_RE` (`asf/metrics/metrics.py:54`) accepts only
  `[EFSTBDR]`, and a session event whose `item` is not in `index.json` is refused outright
  (`metrics.py:236-237`). The lead item's id names the delivery, as F-0086's survivor names a
  merge (D7).
- **Minting Task cards for a delivery.** A Task hangs under a Feature or a Story
  (`core.py:28-33`), so a delivery spanning a Bug and a Feature has no legal parent for its Tasks;
  and a Task per item is exactly the per-item session this card exists to remove.
- **A spec per member, or a review per member.** The delivery plan is the spec-lite. It rides the
  plan lane and is approved the way every plan on this product is approved — the document reaches
  the trunk (B-0059, `asf/record/ingest.py:408-409`).
- **Deliveries formed without an answer.** The rule selects; the operator's one `yes` forms it.
  The feeder never writes the record (F-0086 D12), and the one place that writes it daily is the
  groom.
- **S1 Bugs, blocked items, items with `after:`, and items with no `writes:`.** An S1 has its own
  lane and must not queue behind three other cards; an item with no declared footprint cannot be
  placed against the running ones; an `after:` inside a delivery would order work the single coder
  session has no way to wait for.
- **Re-planning a delivery when a member is released.** The plan document keeps the section it
  wrote. The released item goes back to its own lane and gets its own spec or fix there.
- **A board column or a status table for deliveries.** The rows say it (`DELIVERY → PLAN`,
  `DELIVERY → CODE`, `WAITS ON delivery`) wherever rows are shown today, and the rollup measures
  it. No new view.
- **Measuring "gates saved" as its own number.** The delivered-vs-single table is the measurement
  the card asks for; a second derived metric on top of it can come later.

### Preconditions

  never existed does not hold the ladder — and returns `card` when there is no document at all.
- `Conventions.from_mapping` keeps unknown keys in `extra` (`asf/conventions.py:178-206`), so four

- The proposal grammar, its apply path and its decline field are in place from F-0086:

| D6 | The grouping test | overlapping `writes:`, **or** a shared area at `area_depth` — connected components of that graph | the same parent Feature; a title-word similarity | Both halves are already in the codebase (`footprint.overlaps`, `shape.area_of`), so nothing can disagree about them. A measured count needs the product checkout, which the groom does not have — it runs in the record clone. An estimate that only reads the record is honest about being an estimate, and its two constants are a property of the estimate, not of the product, so they stay in the module while the three ceilings are conventions. |
| D9 | Turning it off | `delivery_max_items: 0` | a `deliveries: on/off` flag | One knob, one meaning: a product that will not batch sets its ceiling to zero, and the rule reads the same field it already reads. |
| D10 | How the lane knows to plan or to build | `stage` on the lead, written by ingest from the lead's own plan document, whatever the lead's type | a typed `delivery_stage:` field the groom advances | The ladder already exists, is already derived from evidence rather than from anyone's claim, and already answers `plan-review r3` for the starvation and stalemate rows. A hand-maintained second stage would drift from the document within a day. |
| D11 | An item the delivery could not finish | it is simply not committed; the branch lands without it; the groom releases it the next day | hold the whole branch until every member is committed | "The rest still land" is the card's failure policy. ## The design

### `asf/groom/delivery.py` (new)

Pure functions over `canonical` (the record's parsed cards) and the conventions. It imports
`asf.feeder.footprint` and `asf.groom.shape` — never the other way round.

```python
TOKENS_PER_GLOB = 2000          # D8: the allowance an estimate makes for one glob of surface
CHARS_PER_TOKEN = 4

Member = namedtuple('Member', 'id type title writes rank tokens')

def is_small(rec, batch_max_globs): ...       # D5
def deliverable_items(canonical, batch_max_globs): ...   # [Member], sorted (rank, id)
def estimate_tokens(rec, writes): ...         # D8
def group(members, area_depth): ...           # connected components over overlap-or-area
def budget_cut(component, max_items, max_globs, max_tokens): ...  # the prefix that fits
def delivery_proposals(canonical, conv, declined=None): ...       # [shape.Proposal]
def deliveries(canonical): ...                # {lead id: [member ids]} from `delivers:`
def releasable(canonical): ...                # [(lead, member)] — D11/D12
```

**`deliverable_items`.** A card qualifies when all of these hold:

- `type` in `feature`, `story`, `bug`; `is_open`; typed `decided: true`; no `removed`;
- machine `state` is `New` and the card is not `blocked`;
- no `delivers:` and no `delivered_by:` already;
- no `after:` and no open `blockedBy:`;
- it declares `writes:` (D7);
- `is_small` (D5): `size: small` if the card says so, else `severity` in `S2`, `S3` for a Bug,
  else no children and `len(writes) <= batch_max_globs` for a Feature or a Story;
- a Feature: its stage is `card` (no spec yet). A Story: its Feature is not at `spec-approved` or
  beyond — past that point its Feature's own plan owns it.

**`group`.** An edge between two members when `footprint.overlaps(a.writes, b.writes)` is truthy
**or** `set(shape.areas(a.writes, depth)) & set(shape.areas(b.writes, depth))` is non-empty.
Components of size 1 are dropped.

**`budget_cut`.** Walk the component in `(rank, id)` order, keeping a member while the running
count is `< max_items`, the union of writes is `<= max_globs`, and
`sum(tokens) + TOKENS_PER_GLOB * len(union) <= max_tokens`. Fewer than two members fit: no
proposal. `max_items <= 0`: no proposal, ever (D9).

**`delivery_proposals`** returns `shape.Proposal('deliver', ids, (), why)` with

```
- [ ] F-0097 deliver F-0097+B-0034+S-0055 — 3 small items in asf/groom (2 overlap, 1 shares the area); 5 globs, ~21k tokens: one plan and one coder instead of 3 specs, 3 plans and 3 coders → answer: ____
```

and drops a proposal whose `proposal_key('deliver', ids)` any named card already declined.

**`releasable`** returns each `(lead, member)` where the lead carries `delivers:`, at least one
member is `Resolved` or `Closed`, and this member is still open with machine `state: New` and no
commit in its `evidence`. When the *lead* is the releasable one, every still-open member is
released with it and the lead's `delivers:` is dropped: the delivery is over.

### `asf/groom/groom.py`

- `PROPOSAL_RE` gains `deliver` in its verb alternation; `_parse_proposal` treats it like
  `merge`/`batch` (ids joined by `+`, the first equal to the line's id).
- `GROOM_SECTIONS` gains `('Deliveries proposed', 'deliver')` after `Split parts to confirm`, and
  `groom_shape_sections` returns its lines from `delivery.delivery_proposals`.
- `apply_proposal` gains a `deliver` branch:
  - `yes` → `form_delivery(canonical, ids, date, who)`. It re-checks that every id is still
    deliverable (F-0086 D8); one that is not skips the whole line with
    `groom: deliver <ids> skipped — <id> is <why>` and writes nothing. Otherwise it writes
    `delivers: ids` on `ids[0]` and `delivered_by: ids[0]` on each other id, with one History line
    per card — `- <date> groom: delivers B-0034, S-0055 (operator)` and
    `- <date> groom: delivered by F-0097 (operator)`. A lead that already carries exactly that
    list writes nothing, which keeps `--apply` idempotent.
  - `no`/`close` → `reshape_declined` gains `deliver <sorted ids>` on every named card (D13).
- A new rule in the same pass, needing no answer: for each `(lead, member)` of
  `delivery.releasable`, clear the member's `delivered_by:`, drop it from the lead's `delivers:`,
  and write on each card
  `- <date> delivery: released <member> from <lead> — the delivery landed without it`.
  The `cmd_groom` summary counts it like the other sections.

### `asf/feeder/rows.py`

- Two kinds: `DELIVERY_PLAN = 'DELIVERY → PLAN'` and `DELIVERY_CODE = 'DELIVERY → CODE'`, both in
  the module docstring's table and in `KIND_ORDER` after `RESHAPE`.
- `delivery_rows(items, product, busy, running, landed_shas=None)`, called from `candidates`
  **before** `feature_rows` so the union footprint is in `running` when Tasks are placed:

  ```
  for lead in every open item carrying `delivers:`, ordered (rank, id):
      open_members = [m for m in lead['delivers'] if m in items and is_open(items[m])]
      no open members                       → no row (the delivery is delivered)
      any open member blocked               → one WAITS ON <blocker> row, nothing launching
      lead in busy                          → no launching row
      stage 'card' | 'plan-draft' | 'plan-review rN'
                                            → DELIVERY → PLAN, brief `delivery-plan`,
                                              branch plan/<lead>, reason '<n> items, no plan yet'
                                              or '<stage>, no session'
      stage 'plan-approved' | 'building …'  → union = ordered union of the open members' writes
                                              first_conflict(union, running) → WAITS ON <other>
                                              else DELIVERY → CODE, brief `delivery-code`,
                                              branch worker/<lead>; running += [(lead, union)]
  plus, for every open member that is not the lead:
      Row(action='WAITS ON delivery <lead>', waits_on='delivery', brief_kind='task', …)
  ```

- `feature_rows` and `bug_rows` skip any card carrying `delivers:` or `delivered_by:`: the
  delivery rows speak for them. `task_rows` is untouched — a delivery has no Tasks.
- `running_footprints` also yields `(lead, union)` for a busy lead carrying `delivers:`, so a Task
  of an unrelated Feature waits on a running delivery exactly as it waits on a running Task.
- `hold_unlanded` reads a delivery row's members as well as its lead, so an unlanded `after:`
  anywhere in the delivery holds the row (selection excludes `after:`, but a member can gain one
  between the proposal and the launch).

### `asf/briefs`

- `KINDS` gains `delivery-plan` and `delivery-code`; `DEFAULT_MODELS` maps them to `HEAVY` and
  `LIGHT`; `ID_RANGE_KINDS` is **unchanged** — neither kind mints a card.
- `context()` gains two keys for every kind: `delivers` (`F-0097, B-0034, S-0055`, in build order,
  or `(none)`) and `delivery_count`.
- `preamble.collect` gains `members`: for each member, `(id, type, title, writes, acceptance, fix)`
  read with `card_sections`; `state_lines` renders, under the existing `Also delivers:` line's
  place,

  ```
  Delivery: 3 items, in this order — F-0097, B-0034, S-0055
  Writes (the footprint this job may touch): asf/groom/**, asf/feeder/rows.py, tests/test_groom.py
  ```

  and a new preamble section `## The items of this delivery`, one block per member, each carrying
  its title, `writes:`, its `## Acceptance` and (a Bug) its `## Fix`, clipped by `section_lines`
  and budgeted by the existing `fit(sections, limit)`.
- `card_digest` folds the members: for a card carrying `delivers:`, the digest lines include each
  member's own digest, so a member's acceptance changing stales the delivery's brief.
- `templates/delivery-plan.md` (new):

  ```
  ## Your job: write the delivery plan for {item_id} — {delivery_count} items in one branch

  Binding: the item blocks above, each one a decided card. There is no spec and there will be no
  second document: this plan IS the spec-lite for every item in it. Add no scope no card carries,
  and drop nothing a card's ## Acceptance asks for.

  DELIVERABLE: `{plan_path}` on branch `{branch}`, cut from `origin/{main}`. Create only that file.

  ONE SECTION PER ITEM, in this order — {delivers} — and nothing between them:

      ### <id> — <title>
      writes: <the globs this item's diff may touch — within the card's own>
      acceptance: <a fenced block that can be run, and that fails today>
      steps: <what to change, in order>

  The delivery's whole footprint is {writes}. A section that needs a file outside it is a section
  that does not belong in this delivery: leave the item out, name it under `left out`, and it goes
  back to its own lane while the rest land.

  No Task cards: one session builds every item and each item closes by its own commit, so this job
  mints no ids and needs no `BACKLOG_ID_RANGE`.

  End the plan with `delivers: <ids, in build order>` and `budget: <n> items, <m> globs`.

  Final message: the pushed sha, the plan path, the item count, the build order.
  ```

- `templates/delivery-code.md` (new):

  ```
  ## Your job: build the delivery {item_id} — {delivery_count} items, one branch, one gate

  Binding: `{plan_path}`, section by section, in this order: {delivers}. Do those items and
  nothing else — not the next card you notice, not a cleanup on the way.

  ONE COMMIT PER ITEM, and the subject names that item's id — `<kind>({id}): <what>`. A commit on
  the trunk naming the id is what closes the card: an item no commit names lands nothing, whatever
  its diff did. Never fold two items into one commit.

  THE BOUNDARY IS `writes:` — {writes}. That is the union of the items' footprints; a file outside
  it is a refusal, not a judgement call.

  An item you cannot finish: commit nothing for it — no half of it — name it under `left out`, and
  carry on with the rest. The branch lands without it and it returns to its own lane. A gate you
  cannot make green is the whole branch's problem: say so and stop.

  BEFORE THE PUSH: the plan's acceptance block for every item you committed, byte-identical and
  passing, and the product's gate once over the whole branch. Paste each last line in the report.

  Final message: the pushed sha, one line per item (committed or left out), the gate lines.
  ```

### `asf/harvest/harvest.py`

- `commits_name_items(repo, trunk, branch, ids)`: every commit subject on the branch names at
  least one id of `ids`; `commits_name_item` becomes the one-id call of it.
- `lane_refusal(repo, trunk, branch, item, members=())` takes the delivery's members; the merge
  refusal is unchanged; the naming refusal reads
  `commits do not name <item> or one of <members>`.
- `run_product_harvest` passes `members` from the card's `delivers:` (it already loads the record's
  items), passes the union as `item_writes` to `hold_with_correction` (so the foreign-red rule of
  F-0080 measures a delivery against its whole footprint), and prints the landing line as
  `landed worker/F-0097 → <sha>: delivered F-0097, B-0034 — no commit for S-0055`.

### `asf/record`

- `ingest.cmd_ingest`: after the Bug and Story loops, any card carrying `delivers:` that is **not**
  a Feature is matched with `match_feature` and staged with
  `evidence.feature_stage(spec_dict, plan_dict, [], False)` — no Tasks, so the ladder runs `card`
  → `plan-draft` → `plan-review rN` → `plan-approved` off the plan document alone. Its `state` is
  left to the type's own rule: a Bug is still `Resolved` when a commit names it.
- `check.py` gains `check_deliveries`: for each card with `delivers:` — every id exists, is not
  `removed`, and carries `delivered_by:` equal to this lead; the lead is `delivers[0]`; no card
  carries both fields; no id appears in two `delivers:` lists. One line per violation, in the
  existing shape.

### `asf/metrics/metrics.py`

- `delivered_vs_single(items, sessions, day, days=7)` → the two rows of the table. For each item
  that is `Closed` or `Resolved` and whose `stage_since` falls in the window:
  - its **owner** is its lead when it carries `delivered_by:`, itself otherwise; a card carrying
    `delivers:` is in the delivered group with its members;
  - its **sessions** are the owner's session events (`ev['item'] == owner`), divided by the number
    of landed items the owner delivered (D15); likewise its **USD**;
  - its **hours to land** are `parse_ts(stage_since) - ` the earliest session event of the owner,
    skipped when either is missing or the difference is negative;
  - a session event is **failed** when its `result` starts with `failed`.
- `render_daily` appends

  ```
  ## Delivered vs single (7 days)

  | Group | Landed | Sessions | Sessions/item | USD | USD/item | Median h to land | Failed sessions |
  |---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
  | delivered | 6 | 3 | 0.50 | 4.12 | 0.69 | 9.4 | 0 % |
  | single | 5 | 14 | 2.80 | 21.07 | 4.21 | 31.2 | 21 % |
  ```

  rendered with `—` in a cell no event supports, and omitted entirely when neither group has a
  landed item in the window.

### `asf/conventions.py` and `docs/products.example.yaml`

`DEFAULT_DELIVERY_MAX_ITEMS = 4`, `DEFAULT_DELIVERY_MAX_GLOBS = 6`,
`DEFAULT_DELIVERY_MAX_TOKENS = 60000`, each a field with a one-line doc comment beside
`area_depth` and `batch_max_globs`, and each listed commented-out at its default under

## Records

- **The groom file** `groom/<date>.md` gains `## Deliveries proposed`, rendered `(none)` when
  empty. Yesterday's answered line is the audit trail of who formed the delivery.
- **Typed fields on cards**, written only by `groom --apply`:
  - `delivers: [<id>, …]` on the lead — every member, build order, lead first;
  - `delivered_by: <lead>` on every other member;
  - `reshape_declined: [deliver <ids>]` on each card of a declined proposal.
- **History lines**, one per card per applied answer:
  `- <date> groom: delivers B-0034, S-0055 (operator)`,
  `- <date> groom: delivered by F-0097 (operator)`,
  `- <date> groom: deliver declined (operator)`,
  `- <date> delivery: released S-0055 from F-0097 — the delivery landed without it`.
- **Machine fields** written by ingest: `stage` on a delivery lead of any type, with its usual
  `- <stamp> ingest: stage card → plan-approved (…)` History line; every member's own `state` and
  `evidence` as before, closed by the commit that names it.
- **Feeder rows**: `DELIVERY → PLAN`, `DELIVERY → CODE` and `WAITS ON delivery <lead>`, wherever
  rows are shown today — the wave step's output, `asf next`, `asf status`. No new file.
- **The plan document** `docs/plans/<lead>.md`, one section per item, ending in its `delivers:` and
  `budget:` lines. It is the delivery's spec and its plan; no `docs/specs/<lead>.md` is written.
- **Harvest's printed lines**: `landed <branch> → <sha>: delivered <ids> — no commit for <ids>`,
  and the naming refusal quoting the members.
- **`asf check` lines** for a malformed delivery, in the existing one-line-per-violation shape.
- **The rollup**: `metrics/daily/<day>.md` gains `## Delivered vs single (7 days)`. No new event
  kind, no new stream, no new field on an event — the sessions the delivery ran are ordinary
  session events on the lead's id.
- **Config**: `conventions.delivery_max_items`, `delivery_max_globs`, `delivery_max_tokens`,
  optional, in `products/<name>.yaml`; `batch_max_globs` and `area_depth` are read, not changed.
