# F-0337 — a parked card nobody will ever look at again

The kernel's grooming has three stages and they all point the same way: **forward**. Intake decides
a new card (`asf/kernel/intake.py`), the Definition of Ready holds a thin one New and a
`groom-fill` session fills it (`asf/kernel/dor.py`), and the still-needed gate closes one whose
work already landed (`asf/kernel/needed.py`). Every one of them acts on a card the kernel is about
to *start*.

Nothing acts on a card the kernel has decided not to start. A parked item — `priority: later` on
it or anywhere in its lineage — is invisible by design (`model.py` `State.PARKED`: "no launch, no
PR upkeep, no mint, never Stuck, never counted as blocking"), and the invisibility is total. Five
parked Tasks from September produce **zero** actions, zero notes, no idle alarm and no LIMBO entry
(P2 below). They are not a queue. They are a pile, and the pile only grows: this product's record
carries **1135 parked Tasks and Bugs** today, 590 of them created in September, against 16 New ones
(P1). Every one of them was written by somebody who thought it was work, and not one of them will
ever be read again by anything.

That is the whole of this card. **A continuous sweep walks that pile, oldest first, on the seats
every other launch left idle, and gives each card one verdict: `retire`, `keep` or `revive`.** Three
at a tick, never ahead of a Ready launch, never while the seats are busy. Each verdict is a line
code applies — a retire goes through the same `supersede` writer a `groom-fill`'s `superseded`
verdict uses, a revive un-parks the card and hands it to the Definition of Ready, a keep leaves it
exactly as it is and marks it judged so it is never swept twice. And `asf kernel waits` grows the
one view that says how big the pile is and how fast it is shrinking: the parked-age histogram.

The grooming's fourth stage is the only one that points backwards, and it is the one that decides
whether the other three are grooming a backlog or a landfill.

### Out

* **Any card that is not a Task or a Bug.** 255 Stories, 197 Features and 1 Epic are parked too
  (P1), and a container's state is *derived* from the Tasks under it (`_state_of`,
  `decide.py:1473`) — retiring a Feature whose Tasks are alive would strand them, and retiring one
  whose Tasks are all retired is a no-op the derivation already performs. The sweep judges work
  cards; containers follow their children, as they do everywhere else in the kernel.
* **Any card with an open PR.** `flying` (`decide.py:1173`) takes an item with an open kernel PR
  out of the parked set exactly so finished work lands rather than rots, so such a card is never
  `PARKED` or `NEW` in the plan's states and never a candidate. Nothing extra is needed and nothing
  is added.
* **Changing what parks a card, or what `PARKED` means.** No new priority word, no auto-parking, no
  change to `_parked`, `_park`, `_visible` or `flying`. The sweep reads the parked set; it never
  computes a different one.
* **Changing the Definition of Ready, `groom-fill`, intake or the still-needed gate.** Not one line
  of `dor.missing`, `hold_unready`, `intake.plan` or `needed.judge` moves. A card the DoR holds
  with fills left is `groom-fill`'s and the sweep skips it (D4); the four stages never act on the
  same card in the same tick.
* **`FINISH_FIRST` and `_launches`.** The sweep launches outside the function every other class
  launches in, on the seats that function left (D3), so the most-tested ordering in the kernel
  keeps its exact shape.
* **A wait class, a target, a breach or a Stuck for an old card.** A card nobody will start is not
  waiting on anything the kernel can hurry, and an operator Stuck per parked card would be 1135
  questions. The sweep's output is a verdict on the card and a line in the tick; a card it cannot
  judge keeps its budget and its silence.
* **The `parked` row of the wait ledger, and the `biggest wait` line.** `is_wait('parked')` is True
  (`waits.py:87`), so 1135 parked items will always dominate `biggest wait` by item-hours. That is
  a true statement about where the item-hours are, it predates this card, and changing which
  classes count as waits would move numbers every other reader of that view depends on. The
  histogram is added beside it, and the `parked` row is left exactly as it is.
* **Retiring in bulk, or any verdict code reaches by itself.** Every retire is one session's
  judgement on one card with a named `superseded_by`. There is no "retire everything older than
  N", because the thing being decided is whether the work still matters, and that is not a date.
* **A second sweep of a card whose verdict applied.** One verdict per card, marked on the card
  (D8). A card whose verdict could not be applied keeps its `sweeps` budget and no marker.

| D7 | **`revive` = clear the card's own `priority: later` **and** clear its spent `dor` fills** | Un-park only | One meaning that works for both halves of the queue. For a parked card it is the un-park the card names; for a New card with spent fills — the other way a card dies quietly — it is the same sentence, "put it back in play", and the DoR then fills it on the next tick exactly as it does after an operator answer (precondition 9 of "Preconditions the work depends on"). ## The design

### 1. `asf/kernel/sweep.py` — the queue, the budget, the verdict, the histogram

A new pure module beside `dor.py`: arguments in, values out, no clock and no port.

```python
"""asf.kernel.sweep — the grooming sweep of old New and Parked cards (ASF 0.2, Stage 4).

Pure: reads nothing but its arguments. Intake, the Definition of Ready and the still-needed gate
all act on a card the kernel is about to start. This acts on the ones it never will: a card that
has been New or Parked for ``kernel.groom.min_age_d`` days is read, oldest first, by one
``groom-sweep`` session that returns one verdict (:data:`VERDICTS`) code applies:

- ``retire``: the work landed, another card covers it, or it no longer applies — the card is
  retired through ``superseded_by`` (:meth:`asf.kernel.ports.RealRecord.supersede`);
- ``keep``: still work, still not now — the card is left exactly as it is;
- ``revive``: work this product wants now — ``priority: later`` comes off the card and its spent
  Definition-of-Ready fills are given back, so the next tick judges it afresh.

The queue is New first then Parked (:func:`candidates`), at most ``kernel.groom.sweep_per_tick`` a
tick and ``kernel.groom.max_sweeps`` per card, on the seats every other launch left idle
(:func:`plan`, called after the launches and the intake). A card whose verdict applied carries
``kernel_swept`` and is never a candidate again.
"""

#: the session kind that judges an old card
KIND = 'groom-sweep'

#: the verdicts a groom-sweep session may return
VERDICTS = ('retire', 'keep', 'revive')

#: the line its verdict block opens with
HEAD = 'GROOM-SWEEP'

#: the keys that block carries
KEYS = ('verdict', 'superseded_by', 'risk_raise', 'reason')

#: the block a groom-sweep session ends with (its brief quotes it)
VERDICT_SCHEMA = """GROOM-SWEEP
verdict: retire | keep | revive
superseded_by: <id or sha>   # required for retire
risk_raise: none | high
reason: <one line>"""

#: the wait classes the sweep walks, in order: a card the kernel cannot start, then one it will
#: not (``State.NEW`` and ``State.PARKED`` in :attr:`asf.kernel.actions.Plan.states`)
CLASSES = ('new', 'parked')
```

`age_days` is the one reader of a card's age, and the one place an unknown date is decided:

```python
def age_days(created, now):
    """The days between ``created`` (a card's ``created:``, a date or an ISO time) and ``now``
    (``Facts.now``), or None when either is unreadable — a card the sweep cannot date is a card it
    never sweeps (F-0337 D13)."""
    from asf.kernel.settings import parse_time
    a, b = parse_time(created), parse_time(now)
    return None if a is None or b is None else (b - a).total_seconds() / 86400.0
```

`candidates` is the queue, and every exclusion in it is one line:

```python
def candidates(facts, config, states, parked, unready=()):
    """``[(item id, class, age in days)]``: the cards the sweep may judge, New first then Parked
    (:data:`CLASSES`), oldest first and ties by id. A card is a candidate when it is a Task or a
    Bug whose plan state is New or Parked, it is more than ``config.sweep_min_age_d`` days old, no
    live session holds it, it carries no ``kernel_swept`` marker, its ``kernel_sweeps`` are under
    ``config.sweep_max``, its intake decision is in, and it is not a card the Definition of Ready
    holds with fills left (``unready``: :func:`asf.kernel.decide.hold_unready`'s keys — that card
    is a groom-fill's, F-0337 D4). An item with an open PR is in neither state (``flying``) and so
    is never here."""
```

`plan` spends the budget:

```python
def plan(facts, config, states, parked, unready=(), free=0):
    """``(launches, record)`` of this tick's sweep: one :class:`asf.kernel.actions.Launch` of
    :data:`KIND` per card taken off :func:`candidates`, at most ``config.sweep_per_tick``, at most
    ``config.sweep_max_concurrent`` counting the live ones, and never more than ``free`` — the
```

## Records

The sweep's writes are small, and every one of them is on a card the kernel would otherwise never
touch again.

* **On a card a sweep is launched for** — `kernel_sweeps: <n>` in its machine block, one more than
  before (`apply.py`'s launch branch). Nothing else: not its state, not its priority, not its
  notes. A card whose session crashes carries only this.
* **On a card whose verdict applied** — `kernel_swept: <ISO-8601 date>` in its machine block,
  written once and never cleared by the kernel. It is the record that this card has had its one
  judgement, and the reason `asf kernel waits`' histogram can show the pile shrinking.
* **A `retire`** — the existing `supersede` write (`ports.py:576`), unchanged: `removed:` with
  `superseded by <id or sha> — <reason>` on the card, `superseded_by: <id>` beside it (or
  `landed: <sha>` for a trunk commit), `supersedes: [<id>]` on the winning card, and one History
  line on each: `- <date> groom-fill: retired, superseded by <id>`. Both cards go through the
  record stage's invariants, and a `superseded_by` that is not on the record is a `PortError` and a
  note.
* **A `revive`** — `priority: later` removed from the card's typed block through `set_typed`, with
  one History line (`- <date> groom-sweep: un-parked — <reason>`), and `kernel_dor_fills` cleared
  so the Definition of Ready gets its fills back. The card's state is not written: the next tick
  derives it.
* **A `keep`** — the marker and one note (`kernel_notes`): `groom-sweep: keep — <reason>`.
* **A note instead of a verdict** — `groom-sweep verdict rejected: <why>`, `groom-sweep <verdict>
  not written: <why>`, `groom-sweep revive refused: parked through <id>`, or `groom-sweep raised
  risk high: <reason>`, each appended once to `kernel_notes` as `apply.groom_fill` does.
* **In the plan and the tick** — `Plan.sweep` (`{'launched', 'waiting', 'oldest_d'}`), carried into
  the tick's summary and the saved plan file as `Plan.dor` is, and one line per tick:
  `sweep: N judged, M old cards left (oldest Xd)`. One apply-log line per launch and one per
  applied verdict.
* **In the view** — the histogram's rows, computed on each `asf kernel waits` run from the record
  and never stored.

No card is minted, no Bug filed, no branch created on origin (a `groom-sweep/<id>` branch is the
session's worktree name and is never pushed: the kind is in `NO_LANDING_KINDS`), no PR opened and
no wait-ledger class added.
