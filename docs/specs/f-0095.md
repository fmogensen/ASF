# F-0095 — the wave stops paying for sessions that have nothing to write

Eleven sessions ended `failed: empty branch: nothing to land`, and they were mostly the same five
Tasks over and over: T-0017 four times, T-0018 three, T-0019 twice, T-0020 twice, T-0021 once. A
coder was launched, opened a worktree, read the plan, found the change already on the trunk or the
surface it was told to build on missing, wrote nothing, and pushed an empty branch. Health judged
that `failed: empty branch` (B-0076), held it for correction, and the feeder sent the next session
to the same place to do the same nothing.

The loop is not wrong — a session that writes nothing *should* be sent back — it is just blind.
The correction loop assumes the session is the problem. In these eleven cases the session was
right: there was nothing to write. Nobody asked, before spending an Opus hour, whether the work
was already on `origin/main`, and nobody counted an end that wrote nothing as evidence about the
*Task* rather than about the session.

This spec adds the two things the card asks for, each in one place:

1. **A pre-launch check against the trunk.** The trunk already tells the truth — the harvest's
   naming gate (`harvest.commits_name_item`) refuses to land a branch whose commit subjects do
   not name their item, so every commit on `origin/main` names the item it belongs to. One `git
   log` per tick turns that into `{item id: sha}`, and a Task whose id is already there is not
   launched, nor is a Task held waiting on a predecessor that landed under a card the record has
   not yet closed.
2. **A park on the second empty end.** An empty end becomes a correction kind of its own, counted
   per item. The second one parks the Task with a reason instead of handing it back, flags the
   operator once, and `asf unpark` is the way out.

## 1. Decisions

### 1.1 In

- `asf/tick/step_wave.py`: `trunk_items(product)` — one fetch and one `git log` over
  `origin/<main>`, returning `{item id: (sha, subject)}`, handed to the feeder through
  `plan_inputs` as the fact `landed_shas`, exactly as `groom_state` is (P5: the feeder reads no
  git).
- `asf/feeder/rows.py`: that fact used in two gates —
  - a New Task whose **own** id is on the trunk gets a non-launching `ON TRUNK <sha>` row;
  - `after:` counts a predecessor as landed when the record says Resolved/Closed **or** the trunk
    names it, in both places the gate lives (`task_rows`, `hold_unlanded`).
- `asf/workers/lifecycle.py`: `EMPTY` as a correction kind beside `UNPUSHED`, `EMPTY_CAP = 2`,
  `empty_ends(path, item)`, and a `hold()` that parks instead of holding at the cap.
- `asf/workers/health.py`: the empty-branch branch of the reconcile passes `EMPTY` (it passes
  `UNPUSHED` today, which is why the two failures cannot be counted apart), and reports a
  `parked` finding when the hold parked.
- `asf/tick/step_health.py`: a `parked` finding raises the `parked` event and one
  `NEEDS OPERATOR` line, once.
- `asf/feeder/rows.py` again: a parked correction yields a non-launching `PARKED` row — no
  FIX → CORRECT, no STALEMATE → ADJUDICATE.
- `asf unpark <item>` — the operator's way out of a park, and the only thing that clears one.
- `conventions.empty_cap` (default 2) and `conventions.trunk_scan` (default 2000 commits).
- Tests in `tests/test_feeder.py`, `tests/test_lifecycle.py`, `tests/test_workers.py`,
  `tests/test_tick_steps.py` and `tests/test_cli.py`.

### 1.2 Out

- **Deciding "already done" by diffing the Task's `writes:` against the plan's intent.** The card
  offers two readings of "its change is already on main": a matching commit exists, or its writes
  are unchanged from the plan's intent. The second is not decidable — "the intent" is prose in
  `docs/plans/<f>.md`, and a file that is *unchanged* is equally consistent with "already done"
  and with "never started". Only the commit arm is implemented. §1.4 records the trade.
- **Touching the round cap, the adjudicate row, or the not-pushed loop.** `not pushed` is a
  different failure with a different cure (the work exists, it is not on origin) and keeps its
  three rounds. Only the empty-branch arm is capped at two.
- **Closing the Task whose work is already on the trunk.** The `ON TRUNK` row suppresses the
  launch and says why; reconciling the card with the trunk is the record's job (ingest, harvest)
  and a different card. The row is deliberately loud rather than self-healing: a Task the trunk
  already carries while the record says New is a record bug, and silently closing it would hide
  the count that proves it.
- **Any change to the coder brief.** A coder that correctly finds nothing to do should still end
  empty and say so; this spec makes that end cheap and finite, it does not ask sessions to behave
  differently.
- **Backfilling the eleven runs already in the ledger.** `empty_ends` counts what the ledger
  holds, so a Task with two empty ends behind it parks at the first tick after the change — that
  is the intended behaviour, not a migration.

|---|---|---|

| P5 | A run's `end_reason` for this failure is exactly `failed: empty branch: nothing to land` | `lifecycle.EMPTY_BRANCH`, `lifecycle.judge`; asserted in `tests/test_lifecycle.py:179` and `tests/test_workers.py:861` |
| P6 | `lifecycle.item_runs(path, item)` returns every run of an item, ended or not, over every job | `lifecycle.runs` / `item_runs`; `attempts()` and `rounds_of()` already fold the same way |
| P7 | A pending correction is cleared only by a later run on the item starting | `lifecycle.pending_correction` — which is why a park with no launch is permanent, and why `asf unpark` has to exist |

### 1.4 The choices

| Choice | Chosen | Against | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| What "already on main" means | a commit on `origin/<main>` whose subject names the Task's id | diffing the Task's `writes:` against the plan's intent | P1 makes the commit arm exact and free. The other arm cannot distinguish "done" from "never started" — an unchanged file is both. |
| Where the trunk fact is gathered | `step_wave.trunk_items`, one `git log` per tick | one `git log --grep` per candidate row; `git merge-base --is-ancestor` per Task branch | The feeder plans dozens of rows a tick; one log of 2000 subjects is a few milliseconds and is read once for every gate. Per-row calls scale with the backlog. |
| Whether to fetch first | `trunk_items` runs `git fetch -q origin <main>` itself | trust the fetch `health` does later in the tick | Step order is configurable (`asf tick --steps wave`), and a stale `origin/main` fails *open* — it would launch the very session this spec exists to prevent. One fetch is the price of the check being true. |
| Where the gate lives | the feeder (`rows.py`) | `workers/wave.py`, right before spawn | Every other launch gate — footprint, `after:`, reshape, split — is a feeder row with a reason. A gate in `wave.py` would be invisible to `asf next`, which is where an operator looks to ask why nothing started. |
| What a suppressed Task's row says | a non-launching row, `action = ON TRUNK <sha>` | dropping the row entirely | A dropped row is indistinguishable from "nothing to do". The action cell is the only string `asf next` and the wave print (`render.action_cell`), so it carries the sha. |
| Whether to close the Task automatically | no — suppress and say so | set it Resolved when the trunk names it | Two writers of a card's state is how a record stops being a record. The row is evidence for the ingest bug, not a workaround for it. |
| `after:` when record and trunk disagree | the trunk wins: trunk ∪ Resolved/Closed | the record alone (today) | A predecessor whose work is on main but whose card is still open blocks every successor for ever. The failure the card names — a launch whose dependency was "missing" — is this disagreement in the other direction. |
| Empty-branch cap | 2 ends, then park | the existing 3 rounds; 1 end | Four sessions for T-0017 is what 3 rounds costs. One end is too eager: a session that crashed early looks the same. Two is "it happened again", which is a fact about the Task. |
| What "parked" is | a property of the pending correction (`parked: true`), read in one place | a new `lifecycle` State constant; a new item state | A new State ripples into every view that switches on one. The feeder is already the only thing that decides whether a correction becomes a session. |
| Which kind the empty hold carries | a new kind, `EMPTY` | keep `UNPUSHED` (today) | The counter must tell the two apart. `UNPUSHED` for an empty branch also mislabels it in every line and event that prints the kind. |
| The way out of a park | `asf unpark <item>` | an operator editing `sessions.jsonl`; a timeout that un-parks itself | P7 makes a park permanent by construction, so the exit has to be explicit, recorded and runnable. A timeout would just restore the loop with a longer period. |
| Whether a park raises a hold in `approvals` | no — `operator_flagged` and one `NEEDS OPERATOR` line | a new approval class | An approval class is standing authority to *act*; a park is a request to look. `operator_flagged` is the existing "a human must read this" marker (`step_health`), and adding a class now touches the amendable set (F-0024). |

## 2. The design

### 2.1 `asf/tick/step_wave.py` — the trunk as a fact

```python
#: The trunk's subjects scanned for item ids each tick — `conventions.trunk_scan`.
TRUNK_SCAN = 2000
_ITEM_TOKEN = re.compile(r'(?<![\w-])([EFSTBDR]-\d{4})(?![\w])', re.I)


def trunk_items(product, limit=None):
    """``{item id: (sha, subject)}`` — every item id named by a commit subject on
    ``origin/<main>``, newest commit wins.

    The harvest's naming gate (``harvest.commits_name_item``) refuses to land a branch whose
    commit subjects do not name their item, so a subject naming ``T-0017`` *is* the record that
    T-0017's work reached the trunk — whatever the card says (P1). One ``git fetch`` and one
    ``git log`` per tick; the feeder reads this, never git (P3).
    """
```

It fetches `origin/<main>` once, runs
`git log -n<limit> --no-merges --format=%H %s origin/<main>`, walks the lines newest-first and
records the first sha per id. A repo that cannot be read (no `repo_dir`, a failed fetch, a git
that returns non-zero) yields `{}` — the gate then behaves exactly as today, which is the safe
direction for an *absent* fact: it launches. `plan_inputs` grows one key:

```python
def plan_inputs(product, root):
    return {'attempts': attempts(product), 'corrections': corrections(product),
            'busy': awaiting_harvest(product),
            'landed_shas': trunk_items(product),
            'groom_state': groom_state(product, root) if groom_policy.groom_auto(product) else None}
```

so the tick, `asf next` and the status cell all plan against the same trunk (P4).

### 2.2 `asf/feeder/rows.py` — the two gates

`candidates()` and `plan_rows()` take `landed_shas=None`. One helper carries it through:

```python
ON_TRUNK = 'ON TRUNK'


def landed_ids(items, landed_shas=None):
    """Ids that count as landed: the record's Resolved/Closed, plus every id the trunk names.

    The two disagree when a Task's work reached the trunk under a sibling's commit, or when the
    ingest has not caught up with a harvest. `after:` must not hold a successor behind a
    predecessor whose work is *already there* — a coder launched into that gap finds the surface
    present and writes nothing (B-0076, F-0095).
    """
    done = {i for i, v in items.items() if v.get('state') in DONE_STATES}
    return done | set(landed_shas or {})
```

**Gate one — the Task's own id.** In `task_rows`, before the `after:` check, a New Task whose id
is in `landed_shas`:

```python
sha, subject = landed_shas[t['id']]
out.append(Row(tier=2, kind=PLAN_CODE, item_id=t['id'], feature_id=feature['id'],
               action=f'{ON_TRUNK} {sha[:12]}', brief_kind='task',
               branch=branch_for(product, 'code', t['id']),
               reason=f'already on main: {sha[:12]} "{subject}" — the card is still '
                      f'{t.get("state", "New")}, the record has not caught up',
               waits_on='trunk'))
continue
```

No session, no slot, and the row is printed by `asf next` and by the wave. It is deliberately
*not* suppressed silently: a Task the trunk carries while the card says New is a record defect,
and the row is where it becomes visible.

**Gate two — `after:`.** `task_rows`'s `landed` becomes `landed_ids(items, landed_shas)`, and so
does `hold_unlanded`'s. Two bugs die with that one substitution: the predecessor whose card the
ingest has not closed, and — because `landed_ids` folds over *all* items rather than only the
Feature's children (`ix.feature_tasks`) — an `after:` that names a Task of another Feature, which
`task_rows` could never see as landed at all.

`correction_rows` reads the third:

```python
if c.get('parked'):
    out.append(Row(tier=tier, kind=FIX_CORRECT, item_id=iid, feature_id=fid,
                   action=f'PARKED {c.get("reason") or c["kind"]}', brief_kind='correct',
                   branch=branch, reason=c.get('reason') or 'parked', waits_on='operator'))
    continue
```

A parked correction launches nothing — not a correction session, not an adjudicate session. The
adjudicate row is what the round cap gives a *dispute*; a Task that wrote nothing twice is not in
dispute, it is waiting for a person (D-0049 informs the operator; here the operator is the only
one who can say which of "already done", "wrongly planned" and "blocked on a missing surface" it
is).

### 2.3 `asf/workers/lifecycle.py` — an empty end is its own kind, and it is counted

```python
EMPTY = 'empty'        #: a correction kind of its own: `UNPUSHED` is work not on origin, this is no work
EMPTY_CAP = 2          #: ends that wrote nothing before the item is parked (`conventions.empty_cap`)


def empty_ends(path, item):
    """How many runs of ``item`` ended ``failed: empty branch: nothing to land``."""
    if not item:
        return 0
    return sum(1 for r in item_runs(path, item)
               if r.get('end_reason') == f'failed: {EMPTY_BRANCH}')


def park_text(n):
    return (f'ended empty {n} times: nothing was written on any of them — the Task is parked. '
            f'Check whether its work is already on the trunk, then close it, reshape its plan, '
            f'or `asf unpark <item>` to let the wave try again')
```

`hold()` gains `empty_cap=EMPTY_CAP` and one branch, ahead of the round-cap branch:

```python
if kind == EMPTY and empty_ends(path, item) >= empty_cap:
    fields = {'correction': {'kind': kind, 'text': text, 'at': now,
                             'parked': True, 'reason': park_text(empty_ends(path, item))},
              'operator_flagged': 1}
    return fields, f'parked {branch}: {fields["correction"]["reason"]}'
```

No round is spent: a park is not a round, and the round counter stays what it means — corrections
the item has been through. The correction stays pending for ever (P7), which is the park; the
feeder reads `parked` and mints no launching row; `derive()` reports `HELD`, unchanged.

### 2.4 `asf/workers/health.py` and `asf/tick/step_health.py` — where the park is said

The empty-branch arm of `health()` passes the new kind and the product's cap, and reports what
happened:

```python
elif reason == f'failed: {lifecycle.EMPTY_BRANCH}':
    fields, line = lifecycle.hold(registry, s, lifecycle.EMPTY,
                                  lifecycle.empty_branch_text(), now,
                                  empty_cap=empty_cap(product))
    pool_mod.update_session(product, job, **fields)
    found.append((job, 'parked' if fields.get('correction', {}).get('parked') else 'held',
                  line.split(': ', 1)[1]))
```

`health()` writes the hold exactly once — at the moment it ends the run — so the event that
follows is raised once however many ticks see the park. `step_health` turns it into the two things
a person and the metrics need:

```python
for job, what, detail in found:
    if what == 'parked':
        s = sessions.get(job, {})
        ctx.event('parked', item=s.get('item'), job=job, kind='empty', reason=detail)
        out(f"NEEDS OPERATOR: {s.get('item') or job} parked — {detail}")
```

Every later tick still prints the feeder's `PARKED …` waits line, so the park is not a thing said
once and forgotten; only the event and the `NEEDS OPERATOR` line are once.

### 2.5 `asf unpark <item>` — the way out

```
asf unpark T-0017 [--why "the plan was wrong, T-0022 supersedes it"] [--product P]
```

Appends one line to `~/.ASF/state/<product>/sessions.jsonl` for the job that holds the pending
correction — `{'job': …, 'correction': None, 'unparked': <iso>, 'unpark_why': <text>}` — through
the same `pool_mod.update_session` every other writer uses. `pending_correction` then returns
`None`, the feeder's park row is gone, and the next wave may launch. It prints what it cleared and
refuses, with rc 1, an item that is not parked (nothing to undo is an operator error worth
hearing, not a silent no-op). The append-only ledger keeps both the park and its undo, so
`asf improve` can count parks that were released and parks that stood.

### 2.6 Config

`asf/conventions.py`, two optional keys, both with the shape `attempt_limit` already has:

| key | default | what it moves |
|---|---|---|
| `empty_cap` | `2` | empty ends before a Task is parked; `0` or absent → the default |
| `trunk_scan` | `2000` | commit subjects of `origin/<main>` read per tick |

## 4. Records

**The session ledger** — `~/.ASF/state/<product>/sessions.jsonl`, the existing `correction` field
with two new keys, and the existing `operator_flagged`:

```json
{"job": "task-t-0017", "correction": {"kind": "empty", "at": "2026-09-23T11:02:07Z",
 "text": "empty branch: nothing to land — commit and push what you have, or say why not in the report",
 "parked": true, "reason": "ended empty 2 times: nothing was written on any of them — the Task is parked. Check whether its work is already on the trunk, then close it, reshape its plan, or `asf unpark <item>` to let the wave try again"},
 "operator_flagged": 1}
{"job": "task-t-0017", "correction": null, "unparked": "2026-09-23T14:20:00Z",
 "unpark_why": "T-0022 supersedes it"}
```

Nothing is rewritten: the park and its undo are two appended lines, and the run's own
`end_reason` is untouched.

**Events** — `<record>/metrics/events/<day>.jsonl`, through `ctx.event`, schema-free (P9):

```json
{"kind": "parked", "item": "T-0017", "job": "task-t-0017", "kind_of_hold": "empty",
 "reason": "ended empty 2 times: …", "product": "asf", "ts": "2026-09-23T11:02:07Z"}
```

That one line is what 0.2 reads back: how many Tasks the factory parked rather than relaunched,
and — against the `launch` events that no longer happen — the sessions this card saved. There is
no `trunk-ahead` event: the `ON TRUNK` row is a per-tick fact of the plan, not an occurrence, and
`asf next --json` already carries it.

**Printed.** Per tick, from the wave step: one
`waits    -    T-0017     — ON TRUNK abc123def456` line per suppressed Task and one
`waits    -    T-0018     — PARKED ended empty 2 times: …` line per parked one. From the health
step, once per park: the `NEEDS OPERATOR` line naming the item and the reason. From `asf next`:
both rows in the table's Action column, with their tier and their kind.

**Files.** None. Nothing in this spec writes to the record's cards — that is §1.2's deliberate
refusal.

- S: A New Task whose own id is already on the trunk gets an `ON TRUNK <sha>` row and no session — acceptance: `tests.test_feeder.AlreadyOnTrunkTests` (§3.2)
- S: `after:` counts a predecessor the trunk names as landed, in `task_rows` and in `hold_unlanded`, whatever the record says — acceptance: `tests.test_feeder.AfterCountsTheTrunkTests` with the two existing `after:` classes as the regression (§3.3)
- S: An empty end is its own correction kind, counted per item, and the second one parks instead of spending a round — acceptance: `tests.test_lifecycle.EmptyEndsTests` (§3.4)
- S: Health parks on the second empty end, the feeder relaunches nothing for a parked correction, and the operator is told once — acceptance: `tests.test_workers.TestEmptyPark`, `tests.test_feeder.ParkedCorrectionTests`, `tests.test_tick_steps.ParkedEventTests` (§3.5)
- S: `asf unpark <item>` clears a park, is recorded beside it, and refuses an item that is not parked — acceptance: `tests.test_cli.UnparkTests` (§3.6)
