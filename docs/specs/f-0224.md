# F-0224 — the cheap half of the review bill is the half nobody asked for

The scorecard's reading: **198 `review` sessions cost $265.57 of $1,596.56 over 14 days (67.3 h),
a 0.166 share against a threshold of 0.15.** The card's Verify is stricter than its acceptance
line: the loop re-reads the same number over the two weeks after this lands and wants it **down by
20 %** — 0.166 → 0.133, which at an unchanged total is review spend down to **$212.85, a fall of
$52.72 over 14 days, $3.77 a day.** That is the bar this spec has to clear, and every number below
is measured against it.

**Where the bill comes from.** One place. `asf/feeder/rows.py:791-829`'s `lane_rows` is the **only** emitter of a review row in
the tree — `grep -n "brief_kind='review'" asf/feeder/rows.py` gives two hits, both inside it, and
one of them is the `WAITS ON landing` row that launches nothing. A review session exists when, and
only when, an item sits in `occupancy['review']` — the lane's `REVIEW` state — and nothing else
speaks for it:

```
PUSHED → REVIEW   {what} has no review of its head: round {rnd} ({why})
```

`rnd` is `review_reason`'s "round wanted" (`asf/harvest/lane.py:1041-1053`): the stored review's
round plus one. A CHANGES verdict sends the branch BACK (T5), a correcting session pushes a new
head, and the branch comes round to `REVIEW` again with `rnd` one higher. Nothing bounds that
count, and nothing distinguishes a second round from a second attempt at the first.

**What the trunk says about the rounds.** Every review the factory wrote before today's review store (`b10dacb7b`, #337) was committed to
its branch and is still readable here. **318 review files have ever been added under
`docs/reviews/`; 235 stand on the trunk now; not one of them names a Feature.** Every single one
is `<n>-t-…`, `<n>-b-…` or `<n>-pr-…` — a Task, a Bug or a foreign PR. Whatever route a Feature's
spec or plan review takes, it leaves no file on the trunk, so the distribution readable from this
worktree is the **code lane's alone**, and the code lane is what this spec changes.


The 19-file tail is one item, and it is not evidence of anything live: `T-0103` is the review
self-trigger loop that the review store closed today by binding an entry to the head it reviewed
(`asf/evidence/review_store.py:16-23`). Discount it and **216 reviews were written for 131
branches: 85 of them — 39 % — were round 2 or later.**

Two of every five reviews the factory pays for are re-readings. And the brief it hands them
(`asf/briefs/templates/review.md`) says, in the same file, both of these things:

> Read, in this order: the writer's report on `{branch}`, then the diff against `origin/{main}`,
> then the plan `{plan_path}` for the Task it claims to deliver.

> In a later round, anything already visible in round 1's diff is not a new C — record it under
> `### Missed in round 1` as an I. Rounds are expensive; a round that finds what the last one
> could have found is the failure mode this rule exists to stop.

So round 4 is told to read the whole diff against the trunk, and told that almost nothing it finds
there may block. **The factory pays the expensive rate to read material its own rule forbids acting
on.** The one thing the round actually has to judge — what the correcting session did about the
last round's C list — is the smallest part of what it is sent to read, and the brief never names
it, even though the fact is already in the brief: `stored_review_section`
(`asf/briefs/build.py:533-550`) quotes the previous round whole and prints **`of head
{stored['head'][:12]}`**. The sha that bounds the increment is already on the page.

**The pass that would have cut round 1 is already specced, and is not this card's.** Before
proposing anything, the obvious remedy was checked against the record. It exists.
`asf/precheck.py` — *"the mechanical pass that reads a code branch before the expensive review"* —
is landed, is 253 lines, has 38 tests in `tests/test_precheck.py`, and **has no caller anywhere in
the package** (`grep -rn 'asf.precheck\|precheck\.' asf tools plugin` → nothing outside the module
itself; `lane.precheck` at `asf/harvest/lane.py:2451` is the unrelated merge pre-gate, which
`docs/plans/f-0060.md` PD4 already disambiguates). It is Story 1 and 2 of **F-0060 — "A mechanical
correctness pass before the human-standard review"**, approved, planned into six Tasks
(`docs/plans/f-0060.md`), of which Task 1 landed as `T-0270` and Task 2 is `T-0271`: a live branch,
three commits, a round-1 *changes requested*, 314 commits behind the trunk.

F-0060 attacks the **cost of round 1**. This card takes what F-0060 leaves alone: the cost of
round 2 and later, the two ends of the loop, and the accounting that decides whether either card's
number is allowed to fall for the wrong reason.

### In

* A later review round's brief reads the **increment** — the diff since the head its previous
  round reviewed — instead of the whole diff against the trunk again.
* A floor on the loop: a head that has already had `attempt_limit` review sessions come back with
  no review filed gets no further reviewer, and says so.
* A ceiling on the loop: a code branch asking for round `stalemate_round` or higher gets the
  adjudicate row the document lane already gets, not another round.
* One entry in `REPAIR_PREFIXES`, so spend that moves into a mechanical pass in front of the
  review is still counted as repair when this card's own before/after is read.

### Out

* **Re-speccing F-0060.** D1 — the decision the rest of this spec is cut around. No `PRECHECK`
  state, no precheck row, no precheck brief, no `asf precheck`, no change to `asf/precheck.py`,
  no change to `RECORD_KINDS` or `metrics.KINDS` (F-0060's Task 6 owns that line).
* **Widening `review_waived`.** D7. Dropping its `small_task` guard is the single largest count of
  removable sessions in the tree and it is refused: P16 is an approved spec calling today's waiver
  a defect, and this card will not buy its number by widening a hole another card is open to close.
* Any change to a model label or an effort level (D8).
* Any change to the `kind:review` cause key, its title, its threshold or its scope. The key is
  read twice — at filing and at verify — and a card that renames its own class scores a false
  victory. Nothing here touches it.
* The verdict grammar in `asf/reviews.py`, the checklist rows, the `covers`/`missing` coverage
  rule, and the review store's layout.
* Doc (`spec`/`plan`) review rounds. The trunk holds no evidence about them (they leave no file),
  and `DEFAULT_LANE_REVIEW` is `{'docs': 'none', 'code': 'required'}`. S-36504's ceiling is written
  against the code lane's `round`, which is the one this card measured.

### How this card's own acceptance can honestly be met

The bar is a 20 % fall in `kind:review`'s share over the two weeks after landing — about $53 of
the $265.57, measured by `diagnose.metric` (P13) over the same arithmetic that filed the card.

* **S-36502 is the Story with the money in it.** 39 % of the reviews this repo can account for are
  round ≥ 2, and each one is currently sent to read the entire branch diff against the trunk. If a
  round's cost is dominated by that reading, halving it on 39 % of the bill is a 20 % fall — the
  bar, from this Story alone.
* **S-36503 and S-36504 are bounds, not probabilities.** They do not make a round cheaper; they
  stop the factory buying rounds that will not converge and reviewers that file nothing. Round 5
  and later cost 4 reviews of the 216 the trunk records (plus the correction beside each); the
  dead-attempt count is not readable from this worktree at all (the session registry lives outside
  it). Both are worth having whatever their size, because both replace an unbounded number with a
  bounded one.
* **S-36505 moves the number by nothing.** It is the guard that stops it moving for the wrong
  reason (D6).

**What this spec does not claim.** The assumption under S-36502's arithmetic is that a review
session's cost is dominated by reading the diff. If instead it is dominated by the session's fixed
cost — the preamble, the gate commands, the tests re-run — the fall is smaller than 20 % and the
card is correctly reopened with both numbers. That is the right outcome and it is left in place;
this spec does not add a second lever to make the metric move regardless.

## The design

### 1. A later round reads the increment — `asf/briefs`

**`asf/briefs/preamble.py`**, in `collect` (P9), one more fact beside `round`/`next_round`, from
the store entry `facts.py` and `build.py` already open (D3):

```python
'prior_head': prior_head,   # the head the previous review round read (review_store), '' when none
```

`prior_head` is the `head` of `review_store.newest(review_store.root(product), slug, branch)` —
the same entry whose text `stored_review_section` quotes — and `''` for a first round, for a brief
with no branch, and for a product whose store holds nothing for this branch. **`build.context`**
passes it through as `{prior_head}`, beside the `{next_round}` it already passes.

**`asf/briefs/templates/review.md`**'s read order becomes two, chosen by the round:

```
Read, in this order — {read_order}
```

where `read_order` is, for round 1 (`prior_head` empty), exactly today's sentence:

> the writer's report on `{branch}`, then the diff against `origin/{main}`, then the plan
> `{plan_path}` for the Task it claims to deliver.

and for a later round:

> the newest review quoted at the end of this brief — its C list is what this round is for — then
> **`git diff {prior_head}...HEAD`**, which is everything the correcting session did about it,
> then `git diff --name-only origin/{main}...HEAD` for the scope row. Do **not** re-read the whole
> diff against `origin/{main}`: round {round} already read it, and this brief's rounds rule below
> says what you may do with anything you would find there.

The rounds rule, the verdict table and every row of it are unchanged (D2). The table is still
answered against the current head: the two rows that name runs — *those tests were run and are
green*, *the Gate commands are green* — are answered by running them, as they are today.

A `prior_head` the branch no longer contains (a rebased or recut branch) makes
`git diff {prior_head}...HEAD` fail. The template says so in one clause: *if that sha is not on
this branch, the branch was recut — read the whole diff as round 1 does, and say so in the
review's evidence.* No code decides this; the session that runs the command sees the failure.

### 2. No second reviewer for a head that filed no review — `asf/workers/lifecycle.py`, `asf/feeder/rows.py`

**`lifecycle`** gains one fold beside `by_branch` (P6):

```python
def review_attempts(path, lanes):
    """``{branch: n}`` — ended review runs on that branch started at or after its lane record's
    ``head_at`` (P5): how many reviewers this head has already had. Pure over the registry."""
```

A run counts when its `kind` is `review`, its `branch` is the key, it has an `ended`, and its
`started` is at or after `lanes[branch]['head_at']`. A branch whose record carries no `head_at`
(a record written before the key existed) counts nothing — the guard opens rather than holds.

`occupancy` puts the count on the entry it already builds, so the feeder reads one structure and
not two:

```python
out['review'][item] = {'branch': branch, 'round': …, 'pr': …, 'why': …,
                       'attempts': attempts.get(branch, 0)}
```

**`lane_rows`** (P1) turns the row over at the cap:

```python
rnd, tries = int(h.get('round') or 1), int(h.get('attempts') or 0)
if tries >= attempt_limit(product):
    out.append(Row(tier=review_tier(item), kind=PUSHED_REVIEW, item_id=iid, feature_id=fid,
                   action=f'{HOLD}: {tries} reviewers filed nothing',
                   brief_kind='review', branch=branch, waits_on='hold',
                   reason=f'{tries} review sessions on this head ended with no review filed: '
                          f'no further reviewer until the head moves'))
    continue
```

`attempt_limit` is `conventions.attempt_limit` (default 3) — the number that already means
*attempts before the factory stops trying* (`asf/feeder/rows.py:333-336`). The row launches
nothing (`action` is a `WAITS`), takes no slot, and is visible in `asf status`. When the branch's
head moves, `head_at` moves with it (P5), the count is zero again, and round `rnd` is asked for
normally — no operator action, no correction, no state to clear.

### 3. A review loop has the ceiling the document lane already has — `asf/feeder/rows.py`

In the same `lane_rows` branch, above §2's guard, the mirror of the document rule at `:1086-1091`
(D5):

```python
limit = stalemate_round(product)
if rnd >= limit:
    out.append(Row(tier=review_tier(item), kind=STALEMATE, item_id=iid, feature_id=fid,
                   action=LAUNCH, brief_kind='adjudicate', branch=branch,
                   reason=f'review round {rnd} >= r{limit}: adjudicate, no further round'))
    continue
```

Order matters and is deliberate: the ceiling is tested **before** the floor, because a branch that
has reached round `limit` has had real reviews with real verdicts and there is something for an
adjudicator to rule on; the floor's condition (reviewers filing nothing) is the case where there
is not (D4). A foreign PR — an item no card made — reaches the ceiling like any other; its
adjudicate row is for the same reason, an unconverging loop.

### 4. A mechanical pass in front of the round is repair — `asf/scorecard/score.py`

One entry (D6):

```python
REPAIR_PREFIXES = ('correct', 'adjudicate', 'review', 'rereview', 'prereview', 'precheck',
                   'rebase', 'remerge', 'relaunch', 'bounce', 'revise', 'hotfix')
```

Nothing else in the scorecard changes: no key is renamed, no cause is regrouped, no threshold
moves, `causes` and `metric` read exactly as they do now. `kind:review`'s share is arithmetically
untouched (P13), which is what makes this safe to land inside this card's own measurement window.
The effect is entirely in the future: the first `precheck-…` session the factory ever runs is
counted as repair, can become its own `kind:precheck` cause, and cannot be mistaken for a saving
in this card's verify.

## Acceptance tests

Every block runs from the repo root and is the whole of what its Story claims.

**S-36502 — a later round reads the increment, not the whole diff again**

```bash
python3 -m unittest -v tests.test_brief_facts.PriorHeadTests tests.test_briefs.LaterRoundReadOrderTests
```

Asserts: `collect` carries `prior_head` equal to the `head` of the store entry

no branch, and for a first round; a round-1 review brief's read order is byte-identical to today's
sentence; a round-2 brief names `git diff <prior_head>...HEAD` with the real sha and does **not**
contain the whole-diff sentence; the verdict table, its six rows and the rounds rule are
byte-identical in both; and the recut-branch clause is present in the later-round brief.

**S-36503 — no second reviewer for a head that filed no review**

```bash
python3 -m unittest -v tests.test_lifecycle.ReviewAttemptsTests tests.test_feeder.ReviewAttemptFloorTests
```

Asserts: `review_attempts` counts only ended runs whose kind is `review`, on that branch, started
at or after the record's `head_at` — a run started before it, a run of another kind, a live run and
a run on another branch each count nothing; a record with no `head_at` yields zero; `occupancy`
carries the count on its `review` entry; the feeder emits the launching `PUSHED → REVIEW` row below
`attempt_limit` and a non-launching `WAITS` row naming the count at it; and that moving the head
(a new `head_at`) restores the launching row with no other change.

**S-36504 — the review loop has a ceiling**

```bash
python3 -m unittest -v tests.test_feeder.ReviewRoundCeilingTests
```

Asserts: a review round below `stalemate_round` emits `PUSHED → REVIEW` as today; at it and above,
one `STALEMATE → ADJUDICATE` row on the same branch with `brief_kind='adjudicate'` and no review
row beside it; the limit is read from `conventions.stalemate_round` and a product that sets it
