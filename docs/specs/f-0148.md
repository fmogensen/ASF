# F-0148 — one line that says how the whole factory did this week, and whether that is better than last

The scorecard loop measures one product at a time. `asf scorecard` prints that product's weeks, its
Features, where its money went and the causes over threshold; the daily step upserts that product's
current ISO week into `state/<product>/scorecard.jsonl`. An operator running two or three products
has two or three of those and no way to add them up, and every number in every one of them is an
absolute with nothing beside it: `$41.20/feature all-in` is not a reading until you know whether last
week was `$35` or `$60`.

F-0044 shipped the per-product, per-week half. This card is the rest of it, and it is three things:

- **a total row** across every configured product — landed and on prod summed, all-in $ over *all*
  products' landings;
- **the week-over-week delta** beside each headline number — this week against the stored snapshot of
  last week;
- **that line first in `metrics/daily/<day>.md`**, so the weekly retro reads the whole factory's
  direction before it reads one product's tables.

The third is what makes the first two worth building. The rollup is the document a retro opens, and
today its first line is `generated: <day> — from metrics/ci (3), metrics/sessions (4), …`.

### In

- **`score.total_row(rows)`** — pure. A list of stored weekly rows (P1) → one total: the summing keys
  added, the three ratios recomputed over the sums, `own_usd_per_feature` as the landed-weighted mean
  (P3), `clutter` summed, the three medians `None` (D4), and `products` = how many rows it summed.
- **`score.delta_row(now, prev, keys)`** — pure. `now[k] - prev[k]` for each key both carry as a
  number; a key missing or `None` on either side is absent from the result.
- **`score.delta(v, money=False, unit='')`** — the one signed-delta renderer, moved down from the view
  (D9). `views.scorecard._delta_cell` is rewritten over it and keeps its output byte for byte.
- **`score.total_line(totals)`** — the one line, beside `headline_line` and `lanes_line` (P17).
- **`loop.totals(as_of=None, products=None)`** — the reader. Every configured product's stored row for
  the ISO week containing `as_of` and for the week before it, each product's delta, the total, the
  previous total and the total's delta. Reads `state/<p>/scorecard.jsonl` and nothing else: no record,
  no repo, no forge, no registry (D2).
- **`asf scorecard --all [--json]`** — one row per product plus a **total** row, each cell
  `<this week> (last <last week>, <Δ>)`.
- **The rollup's first line.** `render_daily` grows one keyword argument, `totals=None`, and emits
  `score.total_line(totals)` as the first body line, immediately under the H1 and above `generated:`
  (D5). `cmd_rollup` computes it for the day it is rendering (D3), guarded so an unreadable state
  directory never loses the rollup.
- **`docs/guide/operating.md`** — `asf scorecard --all`, the rollup's first line, and what the delta is
  measured against.

### Out

- **Computing anything from a record in `--all`.** `--all` is a reader of what each product's own daily
  step wrote. It never loads facts, never opens another product's repo, and never runs its forge. See
  D2.
- **Naming the other products in a product's record.** The rollup's line carries the total and a
  **count** of products; the per-product rows stay in the command's output, which prints on the
  operator's own console (D6).
- **A median in the total row.** `median_lead_days`, `median_prod_days` and `median_task_days` are
  `None` on the total and the line does not print them (D4). They stay on the per-product rows.
- **`--all --weeks n`.** `--all` is exactly two weeks: this one and the stored one before it, which is
  what a delta is. `--weeks` keeps its meaning on the default view and is ignored with `--all`.
- **Reordering the daily step's parts.** `rollup` stays before `scorecard` (P11); see D3's accepted
  cost.
- **Backfilling snapshots.** A product whose daily step has never run has no rows and reads as `—`, not
  as zero (D8). No command is added to reconstruct old weeks.
- **A new state file, a new config key, a new module, a new command.** The total is derived on every
  read from rows that already exist (P1), and every edit lands in a file that already exists (P14, P19).
- **Any change to what `asf scorecard` prints without `--all`**, to `asf status`'s `Value` row, to
  `--by-lane`, or to what `loop.daily` writes.

| # | Chosen | Against | Why |

| D1 | Both consumers — `asf scorecard --all` and the rollup line — are fed by **one** reader, `loop.totals`, over **one** pure total, `score.total_row` | the view computes its table and `render_daily` computes its line | the acceptance is literally *"the daily rollup's first line carries the same numbers"*. Two computations of one number is the defect that acceptance is written to catch; one function makes it true by construction rather than by a test that has to keep being true |
| D2 | `--all` reads each product's **stored** `scorecard.jsonl` for both weeks, including the product it is run in | recompute this week live from facts for the current product and read the snapshot only for last week; or load facts for every configured product | the card says the delta is *"this week vs the stored snapshot of last week"* — the stored row is already the designated source for one side, and mixing a live number against a stored one makes the delta an artefact of when you ran the command. A stored-row reader needs no repo at all and works for a product whose clone is not on this machine |

## The design

### `asf/scorecard/score.py` — the total, the delta, the line

Pure, at the bottom beside `headline_line` and `lanes_line` (P17). The key lists are module
constants because the plan, the view and the line all read the same three sets:

```python
#: A stored weekly row's keys that add across products (:func:`total_row`). A median does not
#: add and a ratio is recomputed over the sums — see D4, D7.
TOTAL_SUMS = ('landed', 'on_prod', 'tasks_landed', 'sessions', 'tokens', 'usd', 'ci_min',
              'repair_sessions', 'send_backs', 'bugs', 's1', 'dead_sessions', 'dead_usd')
#: The medians a total cannot carry: `None` on every total row, printed by no total line.
TOTAL_MEDIANS = ('median_lead_days', 'median_prod_days', 'median_task_days')
#: A week's clutter block, summed over the products that have a reading (`None` where none does).
CLUTTER_SUMS = ('open_prs', 'stale_prs', 'branches')
#: The numbers the total line prints, each beside its week-over-week delta, in print order.
DELTA_KEYS = ('on_prod', 'landed', 'usd_per_feature', 'own_usd_per_feature',
              'repair_per_feature', 'usd')


def total_row(rows):
    """One row across several products' stored weekly rows (:func:`asf.scorecard.loop.snapshot`):
    :data:`TOTAL_SUMS` added, the three ratios recomputed over the sums — so ``usd_per_feature``
    is all-in $ over *every* product's landings, not a mean of means — ``own_usd_per_feature`` the
    landed-weighted mean (exact: a week's own mean times its landings is that week's own spend),
    the medians ``None`` (D4), and ``products`` how many rows were summed.

    A product with no row for the week is simply not in ``rows`` (D8): the total says what it
    covers and never reads an unmeasured product as a zero."""
    rows = list(rows)
    out = {k: round(sum(_num(r.get(k)) for r in rows), 2) for k in TOTAL_SUMS}
    out.update({k: int(out[k]) for k in ('landed', 'on_prod', 'tasks_landed', 'sessions',
                                         'tokens', 'send_backs', 'bugs', 's1', 'dead_sessions')})
    n = out['landed']
    own = sum(_num(r.get('own_usd_per_feature')) * _num(r.get('landed')) for r in rows)
    out.update({k: None for k in TOTAL_MEDIANS})
    out.update(
        product='total', products=len(rows),
        week=rows[0].get('week') if rows and len({r.get('week') for r in rows}) == 1 else None,
        usd_per_feature=round(out['usd'] / n, 2) if n else None,
        own_usd_per_feature=round(own / n, 2) if n else None,
        repair_per_feature=round(out['repair_sessions'] / n, 1) if n else None,
        clutter=_clutter_total(rows))
    return out
```

`_clutter_total` sums each of `CLUTTER_SUMS` over the rows whose `clutter` carries a number for it,
and is `None` for a key no product could read — the forge being down for everyone stays `—`, never `0`.

```python
def delta_row(now, prev, keys=DELTA_KEYS):
    """``{key: now[key] - prev[key]}`` for every key both rows carry as a number. A key missing or
    ``None`` on either side is absent — a delta against no reading is not zero."""


def delta(v, money=False, unit=''):
    """``+3`` · ``-$6.10`` · ``+2.5 d`` · ``0`` · ``—`` — one signed change, rendered once (D9).
    ``views.scorecard._delta_cell`` is this function keyed on :data:`PAIR_METRICS`."""
    if v is None:
        return '—'
    sign = '+' if v > 0 else ('-' if v < 0 else '')
    body = f'${abs(v):,.2f}' if money else format(abs(v), 'g')
    return f'{sign}{body}' + (f' {unit}' if unit else '')
```

and the line itself, the third of this module's one-line renderers:

```python
def total_line(totals):
    """The daily rollup's first line and the last row of ``asf scorecard --all``: the whole
    factory's week, each headline number beside its change against the stored week before it.

    ``totals`` is :func:`asf.scorecard.loop.totals`, or ``None`` when nothing has been measured
    yet (D12). No product is named — the count is (D6)."""
```

With three products measured on the Wednesday of the week beginning 2026-09-21:

```
scorecard week 2026-09-21 (day 3/7, 3 products): 7 on prod (+2) · 11 landed (+3) · $38.40/feature all-in (-$6.10) · $21.00/feature own (-$1.40) · 2.1 repair sessions/feature (-0.4) · $422.40 all-in (+$40.00)
```

With a week nobody has a stored row for, every delta is `—`:

```
scorecard week 2026-09-21 (day 1/7, 2 products): 0 on prod (—) · 1 landed (—) · $12.00/feature all-in (—) · $12.00/feature own (—) · 0 repair sessions/feature (—) · $12.00 all-in (—)
```

and with nothing measured at all:

```
scorecard: no weekly snapshot yet — the daily step writes the first one
```

`_money` and `format(v, 'g')` are the existing renderers (P17); nothing about `headline_line`,
`lanes_line`, `window_row`, `weekly` or `snapshot` moves.

### `asf/scorecard/loop.py` — the reader

Beside `snapshot`/`snapshots`, which it is the third of:

```python
def totals(as_of=None, products=None, read=snapshots):
    """Every configured product's stored row for the ISO week containing ``as_of`` and for the
```

`week` is `score.week_start(to_dt(as_of)).date().isoformat()` — the same key the rows carry (P4) —

## Records

- **Nothing new is persisted.** No state file, no stream, no config key, no record card, no event.
  The total is derived on every read from `state/<product>/scorecard.jsonl`, which
  `loop.snapshot` already writes once a day per product (P1), and whose row schema is unchanged.
- **One line is written into the record**, every day, by `cmd_rollup`: the first body line of
  `metrics/daily/<day>.md`, under the H1 and above `generated:` —
  `scorecard week <Monday> (day <n>/7, <k> products): <n> on prod (Δ) · <n> landed (Δ) ·
  $<x>/feature all-in (Δ) · $<x>/feature own (Δ) · <x> repair sessions/feature (Δ) · $<x> all-in (Δ)`,
  or `scorecard: no weekly snapshot yet — the daily step writes the first one`. It names a **count**
  of products and never a product (D6). The tick commits it with the rest of the rollup.
- **That file churns for at most the rest of its week.** A past day's rollup is rewritten once a day
  while this week's snapshot keeps being upserted, and is stable from the Monday after (D3).
  `write_if_changed` means a day whose total has not moved is not rewritten at all.
- **`$ASF_HOME/state/<product>/` is created for every configured product** the first time `--all` or a
  rollup runs, because `env.state_dir` makes the directory it resolves (P6, D14). The directory is
  empty and is the operator's own; nothing in the record is touched.
- **`asf scorecard --all`** writes nothing. Like every other `asf scorecard` view, it computes on each
  run and prints.
- **`docs/guide/operating.md`** gains the flag, the rollup's first line, and what the delta is
  measured against.
- **A product whose daily step has never run is a visible `never measured`**, in the table and in the
  total's product count — never a zero that moves the total (D8).
- **No other product's record is read or written.** `--all` reads only the operator's own
  `$ASF_HOME/state/*/scorecard.jsonl`, which each product's tick wrote for itself; the rule that no
  tick writes another product's record is untouched.
