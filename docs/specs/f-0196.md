# F-0196 — the noise is gone; the record does not say so, and nothing holds it gone

The card asks four things of the groom digest's **For you**, after a first customer install read
`379 for you`: a near-duplicate Task decided by rule; deciding a card crossing no approval class;
housekeeping in its own section; and no derived Backlinks on a removed card.

**All four are on the trunk.** Items 1–3 landed on 2026-09-24 in `3cc3741b8` — *"fix(groom): For
you holds only human-now approval actions"* — and item 4 landed the same day, earlier, in
`b191575fd`. The card's own acceptance sentence is a test file that exists and passes:
`tests/test_groom_for_you.py`, three methods, `Ran 3 tests … OK` in this checkout.

Nothing closed F-0196. `3cc3741b8`'s trailer is `Fixes:
groom-digest-for-you-fills-with-rule-decidable-noise-379-on-the-first-customer-only-human-now-actions-belong-there`
— the card's **slug**, not its id — and no commit subject on `main` carries the token `F-0196`. So
the card sits at `New`, stage `card`, while its four sentences are live code. This spec is the
residual one: it does not re-mint the policy, the bar or the section.

What survives is three seams.

**The retirement rule is held by nothing.** `is_retired` and the branch in `expected_body`
(`asf/record/core.py:252-255`, `:294-306`) give a card with `removed:` or `moved_to:` an empty
`## Children` and an empty `## Backlinks`. It is reachable and enforced through both readers of
that function — probed here, on a card retired while still carrying its sections:

```
features/F-0002.md:20: ## Children section is stale (run `asf index`)
features/F-0002.md:24: ## Backlinks section is stale (run `asf index`)
  -> after `asf index`, both sections are empty
```

No test in `tests/` names `is_retired` or `expected_body`, and no test retires a card carrying
derived sections. The one line that makes the card's item 4 true is four words inside an `if`,
with no regression around it.

**The card's acceptance is proven at the seam, not through the command.**
`tests/test_groom_for_you.py:58-68` calls `build_groom_sections`, `run_policy_pass` and
`render_digest` directly, with `attempts=2, cap=2` hard-coded — the **past-the-cap** branch, the
only one in which `## Housekeeping` renders at all. So three things the card asks for are not
asserted anywhere: that For you is `0` on the same fixture **under** the cap (the ordinary day);
that `close_duplicate_task`'s answer is actually **applied** — the younger Task `removed:` in the
record with its History line — rather than merely rendered as a groom line; and that any of it
survives a real gated `asf groom`, which is what an operator runs.

**The troubleshooting page still documents the line the fix removed.**
`docs/guide/troubleshooting.md:89` says a bare `NEEDS OPERATOR: <item> — <why>` in the groom
digest means *"the adjudicate sessions for today are spent and the question is still open"*. Since
`3cc3741b8` a spent cap sends that question to **Spoken for** (`the next GROOM → ADJUDICATE`) or to
**Housekeeping** — never to For you. The only bare `NEEDS OPERATOR:` line For you can hold is an
applied answers file's own escalation. And the **Housekeeping** section, which item 3 added, is
named in no page of `docs/` at all: an operator who finds it in a digest has nothing to read.

### In

1. **The retirement rule gets its first test.** A card retired while carrying derived sections is
   reported stale by `asf check` on both headings and stripped by `asf index`; a card retired
   before they were ever written stays byte-identical; `moved_to:` behaves exactly as `removed:`;
   and the two things the rule deliberately does *not* change are pinned beside it — a **live**
   card's sections still carry every live child and backlink, and `compute_derived` still returns
   a retired card's graph (P13).
2. **The card's acceptance runs end to end.** One new test file drives a gated `asf groom` over a
   379-shaped fixture — templated near-duplicate Tasks, keyword-laden parity Stories, a Feature
   with no Stories — and asserts, on both sides of the adjudicate cap, that the digest reads
   `0 for you`; that the duplicate Task is `removed:` **in the record** with its
   `close_duplicate_task` History line; that no groom line reads `barred`; that past the cap the
   housekeeping is under `## Housekeeping` and the summary carries the fifth count; and that a
   real human-now action — a new Epic — still reaches For you as one line.
3. **The operator-facing page describes the digest that exists.** The two groom-digest rows move
   out of `### The record` into a `### The groom digest` subsection of
   `docs/guide/troubleshooting.md`, led by one sentence saying what For you holds (only actions the
   approvals matrix holds at `human-now`, typically 0–5 a day) and gaining two rows for the lines
   that replaced the one being corrected: the **Spoken for** line a spent cap now writes, and the
   **Housekeeping** line. A test in `tests/test_readme.py` holds the page to it.
4. **F-0196's History records why its spec is a residual one** — the two shas its ask landed at,
   and that neither named the card.

### Out

- **Re-specifying items 1–4.** They are live code with passing tests (P1–P6, P8). Nothing in this
  spec changes `asf/groom/policy.py`, `asf/groom/groom.py`, `asf/groom/digest.py` or
  `asf/record/core.py`. If a Task finds it cannot make an acceptance pass without touching one of
  them, that is a finding to report under `needs writes:`, not a change to make.
- **Sending housekeeping to `## Housekeeping` under the cap as well.** P10: under the cap it is
  genuinely spoken for by a session that is briefed to answer it, and For you is `0` either way.
  Moving it would take a question away from the adjudicate session that can answer it and hand it
  to the next day's groom instead. Rejected below.
- **Emptying a retired card's `index.json` entry.** P14: `children` there has a reader that needs
  it (cost roll-up over a retired Feature's subtree), and `backlinks` there has no reader at all,
  so emptying only the half with no reader buys an inconsistency and a one-time rewrite of every
  record's `index.json`. The divergence is deliberate and is pinned as such by S-37850's test, so
  a later reader does not "fix" it. Rejected below.
- **Removing the dead `'Inbox cards with a question'` entry from `HOUSEKEEPING_SECTIONS`** (P19).
  It cannot put a line in For you, which is the card's whole subject, and deleting it means
  writing `asf/groom/digest.py` — the file this spec is at pains not to touch. Worth a card of its
  own; not this one.
- **`docs/guide/operating.md`.** Its `:307` sentence is loose (P16) but not false, and F-0128's
  Task 4 already claims that file (P18). The bound belongs on the page that documents digest lines
  line by line, which is `troubleshooting.md`.
- **`asf/views/status.py`** (P17), **the approvals matrix and its classes**, **the
  `area:`-declared bound** (F-0128's, P18), and **closing F-0196 by asserting `3cc3741b8`**: the
  landings are recorded in the card's History as preconditions, not as a `landed:` sha, because the
  trunk commit that closes this card is the one that lands the three Stories.

## The design

### 1. The retirement rule, pinned — `tests/test_retired_derived.py`

A new file. One `RetiredDerivedSectionsTests` over a `tests.test_groom.make_repo` record, in the
idiom of the probe in the preamble: write an Epic, a live Feature that mentions a second Feature,
that second Feature carrying both derived sections **and** `removed:`, and a Story parented to the
retired one. Then, with no code change anywhere:

- `record_findings(root)` reports `## Children section is stale (run `asf index`)` and
  `## Backlinks section is stale (run `asf index`)` against the retired card's path, and reports
  neither against the live one;
- `refresh(root, create_index=True)` leaves the retired card's two sections empty and the live
  card's `## Backlinks` carrying the retired card's id — the record still records that the link
  existed, on the side that is not retired;
- the same, with `moved_to: other:F-0049` in place of `removed:`;
- a card retired *before* its sections were ever written is byte-identical after `refresh` — the
  rule removes, it does not add a finding of its own;
- `compute_derived(canonical)['F-0002']` still carries `{'children': ['S-0001'], 'backlinks':
  ['F-0001']}`, and `build_index_data(...)['items']['F-0002']` still carries both — asserted
  positively, with the reason in the test's own docstring (P13, P14), so the divergence reads as
  the rule's boundary rather than as a second defect.

### 2. The card's acceptance, end to end — `tests/test_for_you_end_to_end.py`

A new file, one `ForYouEndToEndTests(GroomAutoTestCase)` imported from `tests.test_groom_policy`.
The fixture is `tests/test_groom_for_you.py`'s, rebuilt in the record the gated case owns: a
decided Epic; a `plan-approved` Feature *Parity with the old app*; three Stories whose titles read
as money, legal and credentials; four `Parity: the sign-up form shows the … plan` Tasks of which
`T-0004` repeats `T-0001`'s parent **and** `writes:` footprint; and a second Feature with no
Stories. `write_product(approvals={'groom': 'auto', 'spend_money': 'human-now', 'touch_security':
'human-now', 'touch_legal': 'human-now'})`, `run(['index'], self.root)`, then
`self.run_groom(['--date', DATE])`.

Under the cap — the ordinary day, no session line:

- the digest's summary line contains `· 0 for you` and its `## For you` section is `(none)`;
- `tasks/T-0004.md` carries `removed:` and a `## History` line
  `- <date> groom: removed → … (controller, close_duplicate_task)`, and `T-0002`/`T-0003` carry
  neither — the answer applied, not merely rendered;
- the day's `groom/<date>.md` contains no `barred` anywhere;
- the housekeeping question is present, under **Spoken for**, labelled `GROOM → ADJUDICATE` (P10).

Past the cap — `groom_cfg={'adjudicate_attempts': 1}` and one launch line
`{"job": "groom-<date>", "kind": "groom", "started": …, "pid": …}` appended to
`sessions.jsonl` under the temp `ASF_HOME` (P12), on a second date so the first run's writes do
not change the question set:

- still `· 0 for you`, and the summary now ends `· <n> housekeeping`;
- `## Housekeeping` holds the `no Stories` line, naming its section in parentheses;
- `## For you` is still `(none)`.

And the counter-case, so the test cannot pass by emptying the section: a new Epic in the record
with `approvals={'groom': 'auto', 'new_epic': 'human-now'}` gives `· 1 for you` and a
`## For you` holding exactly `NEEDS OPERATOR: E-0010 — …; approvals.new_epic is not auto.`

### 3. The page that says what For you holds — `docs/guide/troubleshooting.md`

The two groom-digest rows leave `### The record` (`:88-89`); a new subsection takes their place
after it, before `## The redaction hooks`:

> ### The groom digest
>
> `groom/<date>-digest.md`'s **For you** holds only actions the approvals matrix holds at
> `human-now` — typically none to five a day. A question a rule can answer is answered, a question
> an adjudicate session can answer is queued to one under **Spoken for**, and the groom's own
> bookkeeping goes to **Housekeeping**. None of those is yours.
>
> | line | meaning | fix |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | `NEEDS OPERATOR: <item> — <why>; approvals.<key> is not auto.` (**For you**) | a groom policy would have decided it, but the action class the answer crosses is not `auto`. The only line a rule can put in **For you** | answer it in the groom (`/asf:groom`), or map `approvals.<key>` to `auto` |
> | `NEEDS OPERATOR: <what> — <the question>` (**For you**) | an adjudicate session ruled the question was not its to answer — money, credentials, or an action that cannot be undone — and escalated it with a recommendation | answer it in the groom (`/asf:groom`) |
> | `- <item> <why> — (spoken for: the next GROOM → ADJUDICATE)` (**Spoken for**) | today's adjudicate sessions are spent; the question is queued for tomorrow's, not parked on you | nothing |
> | `- <item> <why> (<section>)` (**Housekeeping**) | the groom's own bookkeeping past the day's adjudicate cap: an inbox card still undecided, a Feature with no Stories, a Story with no Tasks after plan-approved. Not an operator decision | nothing — the next groom asks again |

A `GroomDigestProseTests` in `tests/test_readme.py`, in `ProductResolutionProseTests`' idiom
(`_read` plus the `_section` slice, anchored to `### The groom digest` rather than to a line
number): the subsection exists, it names `human-now`, `Spoken for` and `Housekeeping`, it still
carries the `approvals.<key> is not auto.` row, and it no longer contains the string
`the adjudicate sessions for today are spent and the question is still open`.

### What it looks like when it is there

Nothing an operator sees changes: `asf groom` prints what it prints today, For you holds what it
holds today, and a removed card's derived sections are as empty as they already are. What changes
is that all three are now *held*. A future edit to `expected_body`, to `BARRED`, to
`task_duplicate_of` or to `HOUSEKEEPING_SECTIONS` that put the 379 back — or that returned derived
Backlinks to a removed card — fails a test that names the customer complaint, and an operator who
meets a `## Housekeeping` line has a page that tells them it is not theirs.
