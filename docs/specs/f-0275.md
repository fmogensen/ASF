# F-0275 — nothing changed, so nothing should be relaunched

A correction is the factory's way of saying *the work is wrong, go and fix it*. Two things that are
not the work being wrong were saying it anyway.

A review session that dies without a report judged nothing: the branch's head is exactly what
it was. A lane whose own reword bails on trunk-copy commits found nothing wrong with the
content either — that is the lane's mechanics, its own `repair_naming`/`drop_copies`, not a
finding. In both cases the lane went BACK (`asf/harvest/lane.py:1451-1456`,
`correction_turns_back` at `:222-239`) and the feeder launched `correct-<item>` on the same
head. A correct session was asked to change a branch nobody had found a defect in, and a
priority-requested PR sat out of its batch for one to two more CI cycles while it did.

Seen 2026-10-07 08:02-08:07Z on a product: T-0091 (#1070) was BACK after its review session died at
head `845549bf8`; T-0659 (#1122) took a naming/copies hold after the lane's reword bailed. Both
relaunched correct sessions on unchanged branches. And there was no supported way to end either of
them: no `asf stop`, `asf correct --drop` exits 1 once the session is live, and `asf park` kills
nothing.

Most of that is already answered in the trunk (P1-P5 below). This card is what is left: the hold the
answer put in, which quietly times out and throws the branch away by a different door (P6), and the
command the answer added without putting it on the record or on the surface (P7, P8).

### In

1. **The hold has its own clock, and it never ends in STALE** — `held` on the lane record, a pure
   predicate over it, and the PUSHED stale clock reading that predicate. A branch the lane is
   holding by its own mechanics is never declared stale for not moving: it was never a session's to
   move.
2. **The hold asks, once** — past `lane.stale_after` the lane raises one `NEEDS OPERATOR`
   line with a read-only command, marks the record as asked, and goes quiet again. P7's
   promise, made true.
3. **`asf stop` on the record and on the surface** — `--why TEXT` required as `asf correct --drop`
   requires it, recorded as `stop_why` beside `stopped_by: operator`; one row in
   `asf/surface.py`; the command's own test asserting that row (P11).
4. **The 2026-10-07 replay** — both incidents, end to end, ending in no `correct-<item>`
   launch and a branch still on its way to landing.

### Out

- **The two halves already in the trunk are not re-specified.** P1-P4's code and its tests stand as
  they are. This card pins them as preconditions and adds the regression that replays the incidents
  over them; it does not re-decide `REFUSAL_KINDS`, `refused_on_head` or `REVIEW_DEATHS_MAX`.
- **`died` keeps turning a landing wait BACK** (P1). A *coder* that dies may well have left the
  branch half-written, and that is a real correction. The review case is answered where it belongs,
  before any correction is written (P4) — not by widening `REFUSAL_KINDS`.
- **An operator ruling is never held** (P2). `asf correct` at the cap turns the branch back whatever
  the kind, and this card does not touch that.
- **`asf park` still kills nothing, and `asf correct --drop` still exits 1 on a live session.** They
  are a hold and a release of a *correction*; ending a *session* is `asf stop`, which now exists.
  Making either of them kill would give two commands the same job.
- **No change to what the lane repairs.** `repair_naming`, `drop_copies`, `rebase_onto_trunk`,
  `repair_signoff`, the transplant (T13t) and `flags.mechanical` are all still reachable from the
  PUSHED the hold leaves the branch at — the hold is a hold, not a terminal state, and this card
  keeps it that way rather than teaching the lane a new repair.
- **No new stale clock.** The question is due on the `lane.stale_after` the lane already reads; no
  second duration, no new convention key.
- **Table/parser parity is not restored.** F-0084 took the parser out of `tests/test_surface.py` on
  purpose (P10) and nothing in `asf/` reads `surface.COMMANDS` any more. This card adds the missing
  row and the command's own assertion of it (P11); a parity check, and whether `asf --help` should
  render from the table again as its docstring still claims, is a separate card's.
- **The CI cost is not addressed directly.** "One to two more CI cycles" was the price of the
  relaunch; with nothing relaunched there is nothing to pay. No change to `asf/ci_queue.py`, to
  priority, or to how a batch is cut.
- **`asf stop` is not given a relaunch policy.** A stopped run is not counted by the cap (P5) and
  the item goes back to its own row. Whether an operator should also be able to stop *and* hold is
  `asf park`'s question, already answered.

## The design

### 1. The hold's own clock — `asf/harvest/lane.py`

Beside `REFUSAL_KINDS`:

```python
#: A lane record's ``held``: the lane is holding this branch by its own mechanics, not waiting on a
#: session (F-0275). ``refusal`` — a naming or copies refusal a session already answered on this
#: head (:func:`refused_on_head`); ``operator`` — the same hold, past ``lane.stale_after``,
#: with its question raised. Either way the branch is PUSHED and the lane's repairs still reach it.
HELD_REFUSAL = 'refusal'
HELD_OPERATOR = 'operator'
HELD = (HELD_REFUSAL, HELD_OPERATOR)


def held_by_mechanics(rec):
    """True when ``rec`` is a lane record the lane is holding itself (:data:`HELD`): its branch did
    not move because nothing was asked of a session, so it is never STALE for being unmoved."""
    return (rec or {}).get('held') in HELD


def hold_overdue(rec, now, stale_after):
    """True when a hold (:func:`held_by_mechanics`) has stood past ``stale_after`` with its question
    not yet raised — ``held`` still :data:`HELD_REFUSAL`. The deadline that used to declare the
    branch STALE (C6), read off the same ``rec['at']``."""
```

`next_state`'s `s == PUSHED` branch gains one condition, and nothing else moves:

```python
     if s == PUSHED:
         at = _parse_at(rec.get('at'))
-        if at is not None and f.get('now') and f.get('stale_after') \
+        if not held_by_mechanics(rec) and at is not None and f.get('now') and f.get('stale_after') \
                 and f['now'] - at > f['stale_after']:
             return STALE, 'unmoved in PUSHED past lane.stale_after'
         if f.get('refusal'):
             return BACK, f"kind={f['refusal'][0]}"
```

A held branch therefore falls through to the refusal and back into the hold, every pass, for as long
as the refusal stands — and the moment the lane's reword or rebuild lands, the next `set` writes a
record without `held` (C2) and the ordinary clock is back.

The transition table gains the exception where T12 states the rule:

```
- T12 any open → STALE         PR closed unmerged, branch gone, item superseded, PUSHED unmoved
                               past ``lane.stale_after`` — except a branch the lane is holding by
                               its own mechanics (``held``, :func:`held_by_mechanics`): it was
                               never a session's to move, so it is raised, not abandoned (F-0275)
```

### 2. The question, once — `Lane.enter_back`

The F-0275 branch at `asf/harvest/lane.py:3314-3328` keeps its shape and gains the overdue arm:

```python

         and refused_on_head(self.path, f.get('item'), kind, f['head']):
     why = (f'{kind} refused again at {f["head"][:9]}: a session already answered it on '
            f'this head and moved nothing — not sent back again')
+    if hold_overdue(f.get('prev'), f.get('now'), f.get('stale_after')):
+        self.out(f'lane: {b} NEEDS OPERATOR: {why}, and the lane has not reworded it in '
+                 f'{conv.lane_stale_after} — reword its subjects or drop its copies by hand, '
+                 f'or `asf stop` the item and recut it — '
+                 f'`git log --format=\'%h %s\' origin/{self.trunk}..origin/{b}`')
+        if not self.dry_run:
+            self.set(f, PUSHED, f'{why} (operator asked)', held=HELD_OPERATOR)
+        self.results[b] = 'waiting'
+        return None
     if (f.get('prev') or {}).get('reason') != why:
         self.out(f'lane: {b} {why}')
         if not self.dry_run:
-            self.set(f, PUSHED, why)
+            self.set(f, PUSHED, why, held=HELD_REFUSAL)
     self.results[b] = 'waiting'
     return None
```

The `(NEEDS OPERATOR if the lane cannot reword it)` tail comes off `why`: it is now a line the lane
prints when that is true, not a hint inside a reason. Because the overdue arm writes
`held: operator` and a reason of its own, the next pass's `hold_overdue` is False and its
`prev['reason'] != why` is True but unreachable — the question is asked exactly once per hold,
and a hold re-placed on a *new* head starts over with `held: refusal`.

## Records

- **The branch's lane record**, on its run's `sessions.jsonl` line: a new field `held`, written
  `refusal` when the lane places the hold and `operator` when it raises its question, absent from
  every other transition (C2). `reason` loses its `(NEEDS OPERATOR if the lane cannot reword it)`
  tail and gains `(operator asked)` on the raised one.
- **The tick's own output**: one `lane: <branch> naming refused again at <sha9>: … — not sent
  back again` when the hold is placed, and one `lane: <branch> NEEDS OPERATOR: …` with the
  read-only `git log` command when it goes overdue. Nothing between, nothing after.
- **The run's registry line** for a stop: `ended: stopped`, `end_reason: stopped`,
  `stopped_by: operator` and the new `stop_why: <text>` — the ledger now says why the run
  ended, not only that an operator ended it.
- **`asf/surface.py`**: one row, `stop`, with the `HOST` effect — the declared command surface
  carries the command the factory has.
- **What is no longer written** — a `STALE` lane row reading `unmoved in PUSHED past
  lane.stale_after` for a branch the lane is holding itself, and the terminal state and closed PR
  that follow it; a `stopped_by: operator` line with no reason beside it.
- **No new file, no new state file, no new convention key, no new event kind.** The hold is a field
  on a record the lane already writes, the question is a line the tick already prints, and the
  deadline is the `lane.stale_after` `asf check` already validates (C6).
