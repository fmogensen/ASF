# F-0319 — the ceiling stayed on the feeder's half of the fact

`capacity.ci` is read two ways, and the two were never reconciled.

On 2026-09-28, `25a9e74ed` replaced the feeder's CI-free formula — the ceiling less the runs in
flight — with a reading of the runners themselves (`capacity.runner_ci_free`): per class, the idle
online runners over what one run asks of that class. Its own commit message says why: *"a run with
only a long tail job left counted as a whole run, so CI free read 0 … while runners sat idle,
halving the feeder's auto Feature cap."* But it left the old ceiling standing over the new figure —
`min(free, capacity.ci)` — "capped at the CI ceiling", as that commit put it.

Three days later, on 2026-10-01, `fcf1439dc` gave the CI start queue the rule that settles the
question: `ci_queue.ceiling_gate` applies `capacity.ci` **only when the runners could not be read**.
A batch start is admitted by the free capacity of the classes it needs, against its per-run need;
the ceiling is the stand-in for a host that cannot answer. `asf status` prints that law in its own
Capacity row — `ci 6 runs in flight (batch admitted by free runners; ceiling 4 only when they are
unreadable)` (`asf/views/status.py:277-280`).

The feeder's half of the same fact was never brought along. `asf/capacity.py:336` still clamps.
So a product whose ceiling sits below its pool publishes a CI-free figure that is not the runners'
word and not the gate's either: on a product with 23 runners beside `capacity.ci: 4`, `asf capacity`
read `ci 4 … free 0 (overrides ci.pool 23)` while most of the pool was idle, and the operator's
only lever was to raise `capacity.ci` to 23 — a ceiling meant for the queue's fallback, raised to
unclamp a feeder fact, which also raised the fallback itself from 4 runs to 23.

This card finishes the 09-28 change: the feeder reads CI free from whatever actually gates this
product's CI starts, and the figure says which half of the law spoke.

### In

1. **One law for the feeder's CI-free fact** — `asf/capacity.py:bandwidth` reads `ci_free` from
   whatever actually gates this product's CI starts: the runners' own figure when the queue admits
   by their fit and they read; the ceiling less the runs in flight when they do not, and when the
   queue is off and the ceiling itself is the gate. The clamp (`min(free, capacity.ci)`) goes.
2. **The figure says which half spoke** — `bandwidth()` gains `ci_free_from`, and the `auto` cap's
   `why` line renders it: `CI free 9 (runners)`, `CI free 0 (ceiling 4 less 4 in flight)`,
   `CI free ?` unchanged when the fact is absent.
3. **The regression**, end to end: the product shape the card describes — a pool bigger than the
   ceiling, runs in flight at or above the ceiling, the runners readable — no longer halves the
   Features in build; and the no-read branch still does, pinned so the fallback cannot drift.
4. **The law written down where the other half is** — the two docstrings that state the clamp
   (`asf/capacity.py`'s module docstring and `bandwidth`'s), and one paragraph in
   `docs/guide/ci-runner-pool.md` beside the queue's own statement of the same rule.

### Out

- **The no-read branch is not touched.** `max(ceiling - inflight, 0)` stays exactly as it is. With
  no runner read the ceiling less the runs in flight is the only measure there is, and it is
  precisely what `ceiling_gate`/`ceiling_held` then hold a batch start by (P8, P9). The halving the
  card reports comes from this branch (P7); it is the law, not the defect.
- **`capacity.ci`'s own meaning is unchanged**, and so are `ceiling_gate`, `ceiling_held`,
  `shortfall`, the backfill rule, the tick's `batch` gate for a queue-off product, `product_ci`'s
  precedence and the `ci from` column. This card changes one reader of the ceiling, not the ceiling.
- **`runner_ci_free`'s arithmetic is unchanged** — the per-class idle count, the phantom rule, the
  `ci.reserve` rule, the need capped at what the class has online, the bottleneck class deciding.
  The figure it returns is now believed; it is not recomputed.
- **The halving rule itself is unchanged**: `ci_free <= 0` still divides the `auto` cap by two, and
  `MIN_FEATURES_IN_BUILD`, `features_per_session`, the quota-stop share and the fair-share clause
  are all left alone. This card fixes the input, not the formula.
- **No per-class breakdown in the feeder's figure.** `ci_free_from` says `runners`, not which class
  is the bottleneck. `asf ci queue` already names the class and its shortfall, and the status row is
  long enough.
- **No new config key, no new state file, no new command, no operator question.** Nothing in
  `~/.ASF` has to change for this fix to take effect, and no product file is edited by it.
- **No operator edit is recommended or required.** A product that raised `capacity.ci` to its pool
  size to work around the clamp keeps working unchanged; the ceiling it chose simply goes back to
  meaning what the queue means by it. Whether to lower it again is the operator's call, outside this
  card.
- **`asf capacity`'s `ci free` column is not this fact.** That column is `max(0, ceiling - inflight)`
  by design — the ceiling's own headroom, which is what the `(overrides ci.pool 23)` row was
  reporting. It is left alone; the figure this card fixes is the feeder's, published in the `auto`
  cap's `why` line.

## The design

### 1. The law — `asf/capacity.py:bandwidth`

The branch becomes a three-way read of one question: *what gates this product's CI starts?*

```python
        # the ceiling governs exactly where it gates the starts: with the queue on and the runners
        # readable a batch start is admitted by their fit alone (asf.ci_queue.ceiling_gate), so the
        # runners' own figure is the fact — the ceiling stands in only when they cannot be read,
        # and for a queue-off product, whose batch step the ceiling itself holds (F-0319)
        from asf import ci_queue
        by_runners = runner_ci_free(product) if ci_queue.mode(product) != 'off' else None
        if by_runners is not None:
            out['ci_free'], out['ci_free_from'] = by_runners[0], 'runners'
        elif r.ci is not None and r.ci_inflight is not None:
            out['ci_free'] = max(int(r.ci) - int(r.ci_inflight), 0)
            out['ci_free_from'] = f'ceiling {int(r.ci)} less {int(r.ci_inflight)} in flight'
```

`out` gains `'ci_free_from': None` beside `'ci_free': None` in its initial dict, so a fact that
cannot be read has no source either, and the whole block stays inside the existing `try` (P17).

`bandwidth`'s docstring loses "capped at the CI ceiling" and gains the law:

```
    ``ci_free`` — the runs this product's CI can take now, read from whatever gates its starts:
    the idle self-hosted runners' own figure (:func:`runner_ci_free`) while the CI start queue
    admits a batch by their fit (``ci_queue.mode`` not ``off``) and they read; the CI ceiling less
    the runs in flight when they do not, and for a queue-off product, whose ``batch`` step the
    ceiling itself holds. ``None`` with no CI cap and no count. ``ci_free_from`` names which of
    the two spoke — ``runners``, ``ceiling <n> less <m> in flight``, or ``None`` with no figure.
```

The module docstring's CI law (`asf/capacity.py:36-40`) loses the same clause and gains one
sentence: that the ceiling bounds the *ceiling*, never the runners' figure, and that the feeder's
CI-free fact and the queue's admission are now the same rule read twice.

### 2. The source, in the line that publishes the figure — `asf/feeder/rows.py:features_cap`

```python
    src = bw.get('ci_free_from')
    return cap, (f"auto: sessions {sessions}, quota-stopped {said(stopped)}"
                 f"{'' if accounts is None else f'/{accounts}'}, CI free {said(ci_free)}"
                 f"{f' ({src})' if src else ''}")
```

and the docstring's `halved while CI has no free slot` keeps its wording — the rule is unchanged —
with the inputs list gaining `ci_free_from` beside `ci_free`.

Three assertions move with it (P13): `tests/test_views.py:248` and `:461`, and
`tests/test_feeder.py:3190`, each to a pattern that accepts the clause and still refuses a missing
figure — `CI free (?:\?|\d+ \([^)]+\))\)`. Nothing else in the tests moves: every hand-built
bandwidth dict carries no `ci_free_from`, so it renders exactly as it does today (C5).

### 3. The guide — `docs/guide/ci-runner-pool.md`

One paragraph at the end of **When a run starts**, beside the queue's own statement of the rule:

> **What the feeder reads as CI free.** The same law decides the figure that sizes
> `feeder.max_features_in_build: auto`: the idle runners' own count of the runs they can take,
> while the queue admits a batch by their fit; the ceiling less the runs in flight only when the
> runners cannot be read, or when the queue is off and the ceiling is itself the gate. So a ceiling
> set below the pool's size never halves the Features in build while runners sit idle. The figure
> names which of the two spoke: `CI free 9 (runners)`, `CI free 0 (ceiling 4 less 4 in flight)`.

### 4. What it looks like when it is there

The product the card describes — 23 runners, `capacity.ci: 4`, 6 runs in flight, the runners
readable and the queue on — and the figure it publishes under `asf next`:

```
before   Features in build 4 / 16 (auto: sessions 16, quota-stopped 0/2, CI free 4)
after    Features in build 4 / 32 (auto: sessions 16, quota-stopped 0/2, CI free 9 (runners))
```

The `before` line's cap is unhalved at a ceiling of 4 (P7) and its figure is still wrong: 4 is
neither the runners' word (9) nor the gate's headroom (0). At `capacity.ci: 0`, with the same read,
`before` is `CI free 0` and a cap of 16 — halved while the queue starts runs (P5, P8).

With the runners unreadable, nothing moves but the words:

```
Features in build 4 / 16 (auto: sessions 16, quota-stopped 0/2, CI free 0 (ceiling 4 less 4 in flight))
```

— the same 0 and the same halved cap as today (P3), now saying where it came from.
