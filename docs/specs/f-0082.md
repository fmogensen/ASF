# F-0082 — Quota: a cooldown band before the stop

The card, verbatim: *"Instead of a hard limit at 90 % for 7 days, just do a cooldown and only
allow one new job at a time until we hit 95 %. Then stop new runners."*

Today the quota guard is one number per window (`asf/workers/quota.py`): an account whose 5h, 7d
or per-model 7d usage reaches its threshold is simply not picked, and a wave of rows all wait on
one `NEEDS OPERATOR: no account under quota — …` line. There is no middle. An account at 86 % of
its 7d window is treated exactly like one at 99 %: it launches nothing, and the factory stops
hours before the window is actually spent.

This spec puts a band between "free" and "stopped". Each window gets two thresholds instead of
one: at or above the **cooldown** threshold an account takes **one job at a time**; at or above
the **stop** threshold it takes none. The defaults are the card's numbers — cooldown 90, stop 95,
for every window.

## 1. Decisions

### In

- `asf/workers/quota.py` reports one of three bands for an account — `free`, `cooldown`, `stop` —
  instead of the boolean `under_guard`. `under_guard` is removed, not kept beside it.
- Two thresholds per window, read from `config.yaml quota_guards`. Defaults: **stop 95**,
  **cooldown 90**, for `five_h`, `seven_d` and `seven_d_model` alike.
- The existing config forms keep working and mean the **stop**: a flat `quota_guards: {five_h: 92}`
  and the older `worker_pool.quota_guard: {max_5h, max_7d}` (fractions) both set the stop
  threshold, and that window's cooldown is derived as `stop − 5`.
- The pick rule (`Pool.pick_account`) gates on the band: a `free` account is picked as today; a
  `cooldown` account is picked **only when it carries no live session**; a `stop` account is never
  picked. A free account is always preferred over a cooling one, however loaded it is.
- A wave that could only launch onto a cooling account which is already busy waits with
  `quota cooldown — one job at a time` — an ordinary wait line, **not** a `NEEDS OPERATOR` line.
  The `NEEDS OPERATOR` line stays for the case it was written for: every candidate at or above its
  stop, or unreadable.
- `asf workers quota` names the band per account, with the window and the number that decided it.
- The status view's `Quota 5h/7d` cell names the band when it is not `free`, and an account whose
  quota cannot be read reads as `stop`, not as free.
- `docs/config.example.yaml` documents the band and stops advertising the one-number form.

### Out

- **Nothing is killed.** The band gates *launches* only. An account that crosses either threshold
  with three sessions running keeps all three; it simply starts nothing new until its load drops
  to zero (cooldown) or the window resets (stop).
- **No new quota source, no new window.** `five_h_pct`, `seven_d_pct`, `seven_d_model_pct` from the
  same `worker_pool.quota_command` line, read the same way.
- **No time in the rule.** "Cooldown" here is a usage band, not a timer: nothing sleeps, nothing is
  scheduled, no cooldown-until stamp is written. The band is re-read from the source on every pick,
  so it opens and closes on its own as the window moves.
- **`asf workers spawn --account <name>` still bypasses the quota entirely**, as it does today. An
  operator naming an account is an override, and this card does not take it away.
- **The count stays per product.** "One job at a time" counts the live sessions in *this* product's
  ledger — the same accounting `account.cap` already uses (`Pool.live` is `live_sessions(product)`).
  An operator running two products gets one cooling job per product. Making the pool's session
  count cross-product is one change to `Pool.live` that moves `cap` too; it is another card.
- **The `metrics/ticks` `quota` field stays `{}`.** The stream has the field (`asf/metrics/metrics.py`)
  and the tick has always written it empty; filling it means a quota read per account per tick, a
  subprocess the tick does not spend today. Not this card.
- **S1 gets no exemption.** The band applies to every row, `BUG → FIX` included. The S1 reserve
  decides *who* gets a slot; the quota decides whether there is one at all, and an S1 fix launched
  past the stop is the same overrun as any other.

### Preconditions

- `QuotaSource.read(account)` returns `{"five_h_pct": .., "seven_d_pct": ..}`, optionally
  `seven_d_model_pct`, or `None` when the account cannot be read. Unchanged, already true.
- `Pool.usage(account)` memoises that read per account for the life of the pool, so a wave reads
  each account's quota at most once. Already true.
- `Pool.take()` appends to `self.live` as a wave launches, so `Pool.load(account)` counts a session
  the same wave just started. Already true — this is what makes "one at a time" hold *within* a
  wave and not only between ticks.
- `under_guard` has exactly two callers, `Pool.pick_account` (`asf/workers/pool.py`) and
  `cmd_quota` (`asf/workers/__init__.py`): `grep -rn under_guard asf/` finds those two plus the
  definition. `quota_cell` (`asf/views/status.py`) reads the source directly and never judged the
  guard; it is the third place this spec touches, not a third caller.

### Choices

| chosen | against | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two thresholds per window (`stop`, `cooldown`), same three windows | One band on the 7d window only, the one the card names | The card's complaint is about a cliff, and the 5h window has the same cliff. One rule for every window is less to explain than a rule with an exception, and an operator who wants the band on one window only sets the others' cooldown equal to their stop. |
| Defaults: stop 95, cooldown 90, every window | Keeping today's 92 / 85 / 90 as the stops and only adding a cooldown below them | The card gives both numbers outright — "one new job at a time until we hit 95 %", "then stop". Uniform defaults are the card. This raises the 5h stop 92 → 95 and the 7d stop 85 → 95; both are deliberate, and both are still guarded, now by the cooldown at 90. |
| `cooldown = stop − 5` when the cooldown is not named | Cooldown always 90 regardless of the stop; or no cooldown at all unless named | An operator who has set `quota_guards: {five_h: 80}` in the old form wants a lower ceiling, not a band that opens above it. Deriving keeps the band the same width wherever the ceiling sits, and with the default stop of 95 it produces exactly the card's 90. |
| The cooldown is clamped to at most its own stop | Refusing the config; or letting a cooldown above the stop sit unreachable | There is no config-validation path in `quota.py` and a `ConfigError` from a pick would take down a wave. Clamping makes an inverted pair mean "no band", which is the honest reading of it. |
| `band(usage, guards) -> (state, why)` replaces `under_guard` | Keeping `under_guard` as a two-state shim over `band` | Three states cannot be told through a boolean, and a shim would have to choose whether `cooldown` is "under" — either answer is a lie at one call site. Two callers (`pick_account`, `cmd_quota`), both in this package, both updated. |
| A cooling account is eligible only at load **0** | Letting it take one *more* job on top of what it has | "One new job at a time" is a ceiling of one, not an increment. An account that entered the band with two sessions running is already over; giving it a third is the cliff again. |
| `free` accounts are preferred over `cooling` ones, whatever their load | The existing single sort — lowest load wins across both | A cooling account picked ahead of a free one burns exactly the account the band is trying to protect. The band's whole point is to shift work off it. Among free accounts the old rule is untouched: lowest load, ties by name. |
| Nothing launchable + at least one account held only by the cooldown → `quota cooldown — one job at a time`, precedence over the `NEEDS OPERATOR` line | One reason for every quota wait | `NEEDS OPERATOR` means a human is needed. A cooldown clears itself when the running job ends; paging an operator for it trains them to ignore the line. The stop still pages, because it needs a window reset or another account. |
| The wait reason is a constant, naming no account or window | Naming the account and its number in the reason | It is a property of the whole candidate set, not of one account — the same idiom as `pool full` and `reserved for S1`. `asf workers quota` is the view that names which account and which window, and the wait line points nowhere else. |
| `stop` is judged over every window before `cooldown` is | One pass, first window that trips anything decides | With 5h at 91 and 7d at 97, a single pass in window order would report `cooldown` and launch a job onto an account that is past its stop. Two passes mean the worst band always wins, and `why` still names a real window. |
| An unreadable account is `stop` | `cooldown` (half-trust), or `free` | Unchanged from today's rule — unknown ≠ free. A source that has started failing is the moment to launch nothing, not the moment to launch one more. |
| The band gates launches only; running sessions are untouched | Ending sessions on an account that crosses the stop | Killing a worker mid-job destroys the work and still spends what it spent. The factory's own rule elsewhere (`health`, `stall`) is that a live session ends on its own terms. |

## 2. The design

### `asf/workers/quota.py` — the band

```python
DEFAULT_STOP = {'five_h': 95, 'seven_d': 95, 'seven_d_model': 95}
BAND = 5                       # how far below the stop the cooldown opens, when unnamed
WINDOW_KEYS = {'five_h': 'five_h_pct', 'seven_d': 'seven_d_pct',
               'seven_d_model': 'seven_d_model_pct'}          # unchanged; its order is `why`'s
FREE, COOLDOWN, STOP = 'free', 'cooldown', 'stop'
```

`guards_from_config(cfg)` returns `{'stop': {window: pct}, 'cooldown': {window: pct}}`, built in
this order so the most specific form wins:

1. `stop` starts as `DEFAULT_STOP`; no cooldown is named yet.
2. `worker_pool.quota_guard: {max_5h, max_7d}` (fractions) → `stop['five_h' | 'seven_d'] = v * 100`.
3. `quota_guards: {five_h: .., seven_d: .., seven_d_model: ..}` (the flat form) → `stop[window]`.
4. `quota_guards: {stop: {..}, cooldown: {..}}` (the new form) → `stop[window]`, `cooldown[window]`.
5. Every window with no cooldown named: `cooldown[w] = max(0, stop[w] - BAND)`.
   Then every window: `cooldown[w] = min(cooldown[w], stop[w])`.

`band(usage, guards) -> (state, why)`:

- `usage is None` → `(STOP, 'quota unreadable')`.
- Every window in `WINDOW_KEYS` order whose value is not `None` and `>= guards['stop'][w]` →
  `(STOP, f'{uk} {v:g} ≥ {stop:g}')`, the first one found.
- Then the same pass against `guards['cooldown']` → `(COOLDOWN, f'{uk} {v:g} ≥ {cooldown:g}')`.
- Otherwise `(FREE, '')`.

`under_guard` is deleted. `QuotaSource`, `FakeQuotaSource`, `CommandQuotaSource`, `NoQuotaSource`
and `source_from_config` are untouched.

### `asf/workers/pool.py` — the pick rule

`Pool.guards` now holds the nested dict; the default is `quota_mod.guards_from_config({})`.

```python
REASON_COOLDOWN = 'quota cooldown — one job at a time'
```

`Pool.band(account)` → `quota_mod.band(self.usage(account), self.guards)`.

`pick_account` keeps its shape — lane filter, caps, S1 reserve — and replaces its one guard line
with the band split, which runs last, exactly where the guard ran:

```python
free, cooling, held = [], [], False
for a in room:
    state, _why = self.band(a)
    if state == quota_mod.FREE:
        free.append(a)
    elif state == quota_mod.COOLDOWN:
        if self.load(a) == 0:
            cooling.append(a)
        else:
            held = True                         # cooling and already carrying its one job
if free:
    free.sort(key=lambda a: (self.load(a), a.name))
    return free[0], ''
if cooling:
    cooling.sort(key=lambda a: a.name)          # every one of them is at load 0
    return cooling[0], ''
return None, (REASON_COOLDOWN if held else REASON_NO_QUOTA)
```

`REASON_NO_QUOTA`'s text is unchanged, so the operator's existing line still reads
`NEEDS OPERATOR: no account under quota — wait for a window to reset, or add an account …`.

Nothing else in `pool.py` moves: `load`, `under_caps`, `lane_load`, `take` and the ledger are as
they are.

### What a wave looks like

One account, cap 3, 7d at 93 %, three rows ready:

```
launched spec-f-0031             F-0031     → acct-a (opus) pid 4242
waits    plan-f-0030             F-0030     — quota cooldown — one job at a time
waits    spec-f-0033             F-0033     — quota cooldown — one job at a time
```

The same account at 96 %:

```
waits    spec-f-0031             F-0031     — NEEDS OPERATOR: no account under quota — wait for a window to reset, or add an account under worker_pool.accounts (see: asf workers quota)
```

Two accounts, `acct-a` at 92 % and idle, `acct-b` free with two of three slots taken: every row
goes to `acct-b` until it is full, and only then does `acct-a` take one.

### `asf workers quota`

The last column becomes `Band`:

```
| Account | Lane | Load / cap | 5h % | 7d % | Band |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| acct-a | local | 0 / 3 | 10 | 91 | cooldown — seven_d_pct 91 ≥ 90 |
| acct-b | local | 2 / 3 | 44 | 12 | free |
| acct-c | cloud | 0 / 2 | 10 | 96 | stop — seven_d_pct 96 ≥ 95 |
| acct-d | cloud | 0 / 2 | ? | ? | stop — quota unreadable |
```

### `asf/views/status.py`

`quota_cell` keeps its shape and appends the band when it is not free; an unreadable account is
`?%/?% stop` rather than a silent `?%/?%`:

```
| Quota 5h/7d | acct-a 10%/91% cooldown, acct-b 44%/12%, acct-d ?%/?% stop |
```

The `except` around `source.read` keeps the failure to a cell (it is a status table), but the
value it falls back to is `None` — which the band reads as `stop` — not `{}`.

### `docs/config.example.yaml`

The `worker_pool.quota_guard` block is replaced by a documented top-level `quota_guards`, where
`guards_from_config` actually reads it:

```yaml
# quota band (percent of each window, not a fraction). An account at or above `cooldown` in any
# window takes one job at a time; at or above `stop` it takes none. Running sessions are never
# stopped — the band gates new launches only. The older `worker_pool.quota_guard: {max_5h, max_7d}`
# is still read, as the `stop` for those two windows.
quota_guards:
  stop:     {five_h: 95, seven_d: 95, seven_d_model: 95}
  cooldown: {five_h: 90, seven_d: 90, seven_d_model: 90}
```

## 4. Records

What the change writes, and where:

| what | where | when | who reads it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `waits <job> <item> — quota cooldown — one job at a time` | the wave's stdout → the tick log | a row that could only have gone to a cooling account that is busy | the operator |
| `waits <job> <item> — NEEDS OPERATOR: no account under quota — …` | the wave's stdout → the tick log | every candidate at or above its stop, or unreadable | the operator, who must act |
| the `Band` column: `free` / `cooldown — <window> <v> ≥ <t>` / `stop — …` | `asf workers quota` stdout | on demand | the operator |
| the band in the `Quota 5h/7d` cell | the status view's table | whenever the status page renders | the operator |

Nothing new is persisted. No file, no stamp, no row in the session ledger, no event in
`metrics/events`, no field in `metrics/ticks`. The band is derived from the quota source on every
pick and from the ledger's live sessions; there is no state to go stale and nothing to migrate.
A launch that happens while an account is cooling is an ordinary launch line in
`~/.ASF/state/<product>/sessions.jsonl`, indistinguishable from any other — which is correct: the
band decided *whether*, not *what*.

Config that an operator may now write (`~/.ASF/config.yaml`), all optional:

| key | means | default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quota_guards.stop.{five_h,seven_d,seven_d_model}` | at or above: no new job on that account | 95 |
| `quota_guards.cooldown.{five_h,seven_d,seven_d_model}` | at or above: one job at a time | its stop − 5 |
| `quota_guards.{five_h,seven_d,seven_d_model}` (flat, existing) | that window's stop | — |
| `worker_pool.quota_guard.{max_5h,max_7d}` (fractions, existing) | that window's stop | — |
