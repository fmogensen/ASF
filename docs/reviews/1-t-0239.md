- **C1** `asf/conventions.py:1038-1049` (the `('feeder', FEEDER_KEYS)` arm of the new block loop in `Conventions.from_mapping`) — extracting `max_specs_in_flight` out of the `feeder:` block into a dedicated field removes that key from `extra['feeder']` entirely (`c.extra == {}` after `from_mapping({'feeder': {'max_specs_in_flight': 4}})`, asserted by the new test itself at `tests/test_conventions.py:450-452`). `asf/feeder/rows.py:372-378` (`max_specs_in_flight(product)`, a pre-existing, currently-shipping reader used by `finish_first` at `asf/feeder/rows.py:2709,2725`) reads the override the old way: `_conventions(product).get('feeder').get('max_specs_in_flight')`. That path now always returns `None`, so any product that sets `conventions.feeder.max_specs_in_flight` silently falls back to the hard-coded default of 2 instead of its configured cap. Confirmed: `python3 -m unittest tests.test_feeder -v` fails 4 tests on this head and passes clean on `origin/main` with the identical command. Fix: either (a) have `asf/feeder/rows.py:377` read `_conventions(product).max_specs_in_flight` (the new field) instead of digging into the `feeder` extra dict, or (b) stop stripping `max_specs_in_flight` out of `extra['feeder']` in `from_mapping` (keep it in both places) until Task 6 — whichever the plan intends for its reader-task boundary — but either way this diff cannot ship as-is: it breaks an existing, shipped behavior today, not merely leaves a future reader unwritten.
