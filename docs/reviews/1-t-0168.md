  and its only caller, `handle_dead` (`asf/tick/step_health.py:81-82`), catches
  `(OSError, KeyError)`, not `spawn_mod.SpawnError`. A dead session whose kind's resolved label
  has no `worker_pool.models` entry now raises out of `handle_dead` and aborts the rest of that
  tick's health step (`asf/tick/step_health.py:169-172`'s loop, plus

  fails 4 tests with exactly this traceback, and it is why `python3 tools/run_tests.py` (the
  Task's own Gate) is red. Fix inside this Task's footprint: catch `spawn_mod.SpawnError` in
  `correct_once` itself (the same pattern already used two lines down for
  `runtime_mod.AuthEnvError`, `asf/workers/stall.py:239-241` — print and `return False`), so an
  unmapped label holds the one session instead of the whole step.
