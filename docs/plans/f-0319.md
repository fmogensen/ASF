# Plan F-0319 — one law for the feeder's CI-free fact, and the figure that says which half spoke

Epic: E-0004 — Control plane — the factory's dashboard, served from the record. Spec:
`docs/specs/f-0319.md` (approved). Branch: `plan/F-0319`.

**One Task.** The spec's four design sections are one sentence of behaviour read in four places:
§1 drops the clamp from `asf/capacity.py:bandwidth` and sets the source in the *same two lines*,
§2 renders that source in `asf/feeder/rows.py:features_cap`, §3 writes the law into the two
docstrings and the guide, and §4 is the replay that proves the end the card exists for. Two of the
three Stories' acceptance fences cross `asf/capacity.py` and `asf/feeder/rows.py` **together** —
S-99356's names a class in `tests/test_capacity.py` *and* one in `tests/test_feeder.py`,
S-99357's one in `tests/test_capacity.py` *and* one in `tests/test_views.py` — so no cut puts a
whole fence inside one Task except the trivial one (**PD1**). The whole diff is ~15 lines of
production code, one guide paragraph and three test modules.

Wave order: **1**. `after: none` — it starts at once and nothing waits on it.

What is fixed the moment this Task lands, stated exactly: a product whose `capacity.ci` sits
below its runner pool stops publishing a CI-free figure that is neither the runners' word nor the
gate's headroom — at every ceiling — and stops halving the Features-in-build cap **at a ceiling of
0**, which is the one ceiling at which the clamp could halve it (**PD3**, measured; the halving
the card's description reports at a ceiling of 4 comes from the no-read branch, which is the
queue's own law and stays). Both published lines then say which half spoke. Nothing about this
change is staged — there is no inert half, no backfill, and no tick to wait for.
