# F-0131 re-planned — the classification alone

The groom's reshape decision on F-0131 is **cut it to the classification alone**. The spec says in
its own words what the two halves are (`docs/specs/f-0131.md:50-54`):

> This card is therefore two halves of one fact. **Every cancelled job carries the cause of its
> cancel** — `timeout`, `runner-loss`, or `failure` for a cancel that followed a verdict somewhere
> else — computed at import from evidence that is free and durable, and **every job whose runs come
> close to its limit is listed** in the scorecard, with the limit and the distance, before it starts
> timing out.

The first half is the classification and stays whole: computed at import, written on the row,
named in the waste table, acted on by the diagnosis. The second half — the near-limit list — is
the cut, and it is cut everywhere it reaches, not just where it is named.

One Task of this Feature has landed and is kept exactly as it is: `T-0644`, the spec's §2, which
put `DEFAULT_JOB_TIMEOUT_MIN`, `parse_timeouts`, `Backend.timeouts()`, `GitHubBackend.timeouts()`
and the `_workflow_texts()` walk into `asf/ci_pool.py`. The limit is **not** near-limit work: arm 1
of the ladder — *it ran to its own limit* — is the whole of the `timeout` class, so the
classification cannot be built without it. Verified in this checkout at `7d2ee49c6`, below.

Three open Tasks, all three rewritten, none dropped, none added. The decision removes work from one
of them and leaves the other two re-anchored.
