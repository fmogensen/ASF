# Replan F-0185 — one connector kind, carried end to end

The reshape decision, and the whole of the scope: **cut it to one connector kind end to end.**

`kind:` is a field of the card's own model with a closed vocabulary of exactly three values —
`cli | token | oauth` (`docs/specs/f-0185.md`, the `## Records` block). *One kind* names one of
those three. *End to end* keeps the chain the card's title names — declaration → `asf connect` →
doctor → approvals → sessions → status — and narrows the kind that travels down it.

The kind that survives is **`cli`**: it is the only one all six shipped catalogue entries are, it is
the only one the card's own "Today" paragraph has evidence for, and it is the one whose carriers
already exist in this checkout. `token` and `oauth` are refused by name, so an operator who declares
one is told what this release carries rather than loading a connector whose other half nothing
implements.

Against the plan `docs/plans/f-0185.md`, which this replan re-cuts: its PD1, PD3, PD5, PD6, PD7,
PD8, PD9, PD10, PD12, PD13, PD14, PD16 and PD17 all stand and are carried into the Tasks below by
number. **PD2, PD4 and PD15 leave with the half they are about** (RD2). Where this replan corrects
the plan, it says so as an `RD`.

The six open-at-plan-time pieces become **six Tasks again**, because T-0551 is a merge of five of
them (its `writes:` is the union of the plan's Tasks 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6, twenty globs over nine
packages) and a merged card is what the reshape row was raised against. The union is un-merged, the
`cli` cut shrinks what each piece has to do, and one of them — the variable carrier — disappears
altogether.
