### Task T-0671: a read the pass already made costs no second process, and anything that writes empties the cache
stories: S-52904, S-52905
writes: asf/harvest/harvest.py, asf/harvest/lane.py, asf/gitpush.py, asf/ci_queue.py, tests/test_harvest.py, tests/test_lane.py

The spec's §*The design* §1, §2 and §3 in full, merged into one card by the record's own groom
(2026-10-03, 2026-10-04): `READ_SUBCOMMANDS`, `read_cache()`, `drop_reads()`, `read_stats()`,
`sh(cache=…)`, `_gh` served from and stored in the store, the drop at both `mutation_guard`
chokepoints this process reaches (`gitpush.push`, `ci_queue.GitHubSource.gh_try`), the
`cache=True` marks at the lane's eleven per-branch call sites, the two windows — and
`PREFETCH_WIDTH`, `harvest.prefetch`, `Lane.prefetch_facts` and `Lane.prefetch_checks`, the
per-branch `git` reads and the gated PRs' checks issued eight wide *before* the sequential loop
reads them back out of the store. §4 (`reads_line`, the `lane: reads` line) is **not** part of
this card — it was cut by the 2026-10-03 replan and stays cut; no Acceptance line below names it,
and `read_stats()` returns `{'git', 'gh', 'hits'}`, never a fourth `process_s` field (nothing
reads it once the line is gone).

The store pays first and everything else rests on it: a hit returns what the miss returned, every
write empties the store before it runs, and the prefetch that follows is never load-bearing — it
produces nothing anyone reads, swallows every ordinary failure, and skipping it entirely leaves
the pass computing exactly what it computes today, at today's speed.

Read **RC1** (why `_gh`'s wrapper is simpler now, not harder — no retry-argv concern), **RC2**
(the only other read-only git client in the tree, `asf.gitops`, and why none of the twelve marks
below are on it), **RC3** (a fifth `pr_checks` caller the warm already covers) and **RC4**
(`defer_pushes` and the gate window's boundary, both unmoved) above before starting.
