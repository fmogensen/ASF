## Why no `stories:` line

`parse` reads a missing field as `None` and a present one as a list, and `_apply` distinguishes
them: `if t['stories'] is not None: updates['stories'] = [s for s in t['stories'] if s in
canonical]` (`asf/record/replan.py:275-276`). A line reading `S-37850` matches
`re.findall(r'\bS-\d{4}\b', …)` **not at all** — the trailing `\b` cannot hold between `5` and `0`
— so it parses to `[]`, which is *not* `None`, and the record would write an empty list over
whatever the card holds. Omitting the line leaves T-0771's own `stories:` untouched, which is the
only safe choice while the ids are out of range. The Story the Task covers is named in prose
instead, above and in the Task's opening line, so coverage is still readable by a human.
