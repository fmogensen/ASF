# Replan F-0130 — backfill becomes one `git log` and the record; the open pull requests leave

The reshape decision, in the groom's words: *cut it to what the repository can actually be made to
say.*

**What the repository can be made to say.** A merge commit on the trunk, and five things about it
that are in the repository itself: its sha, its committer date, its subject — and so the head branch
name the subject carries and the pull-request number the subject carries — and its diff against its
first parent. That set, resolved against the record's own `legacy_id` rules and the product's
declared `legacy_branch` regex, is the whole of what this card now reads. Nothing else. Measured in
this worktree at `7d2ee49c6`:


The record already holds the answer this evidence becomes (`closing.RULES[0]`, the `reconciled`
rule), and P5's defect is still exactly a defect: measured here, nothing in `asf` passes `landed=`
to `closing.Ev`, and `asf/record/trunk_check.py` does not exist. The repository can be made to say
what landed; the record can be made to answer; the wire between them is missing. That is the card.

**What the repository cannot be made to say, and which therefore leaves.** Whether an open pull
request merges clean against today's trunk. Whether every file it touches is already identical on
the trunk. What verdict word an old review file carried. What a squash merge's head branch was
called. The first three are the host's answers about work that **is not in the repository at all** —
an open pull request is not on the trunk — and the fourth is the host's answer about a commit whose
own subject does not say it. Measured: `match_event(items, title='Add the free plan (#820)',
pr=820)` → `([], 'no rule matched (pr, title)')`.

### Drop T-0749: every open pre-ASF pull request gets exactly one disposition, and the lane still lands it

The reshape decision — *cut it to what the repository can actually be made to say* — cuts this Task
whole, and it is the one Task the decision names rather than narrows.

Not one of its five dispositions is a reading of the repository. `on_trunk` is `already_on_trunk` /
`review.same_code` over a branch that is **not on the trunk**; `adopt` requires `merges_clean`, a
merge trial of an open pull request against today's trunk; `conflicting_stale` is that same trial
plus the head's last-moved date from the host; `report_only` reads the host's file list for an
unmerged head; and `legacy_review` reads an old review file's verdict word on a branch the record
has never seen. Every one is an answer about work that exists only as host state, and the repository
cannot be made to say any of them.

Three further reasons the drop is the right shape rather than a deferral:

- **It is a third implementation of a question two other cards already own.**
  `docs/specs/f-0125.md` and `docs/plans/f-0125.md` carry `asf adopt-pr`,
  `conventions.legacy_review`, the lane's adopted run, the `Adopted` status row and the
  `steps.batch` doctor row; `docs/plans/replans/f-0120-53be601e3f22.md` records the groom closing
  that scope into F-0120 and leaving it *"uncut"* there, pending a spec revision and a ruling on
  F-0125's card. This Task would have been the third pass over the same lane.
- **Its own design said the lane already does it.** D7 is *"backfill decides; the lane lands"* and
  its whole argument is that P8 and P9 are *"a complete second implementation of that already, with
  the archive pushes, the PR closes, the stale rules and the conflict handling that took B-0057,
  B-0065 and B-0066 to get right"*. The Task's content was two calls into `Lane` and a predicate
  table in front of them; dropping the predicate table loses no landing path.
- **It is the only irreversible half of the card.** The three closing dispositions close pull
  requests on the host, and its own plan text says it: *"A PR closed in error is the one outcome
  this pass cannot undo."* Everything that remains in F-0130 writes a typed field in a git-tracked
  record and is revertible by a commit.

What goes with it: `asf/backfill/prs.py` whole (no Task writes that path now), the five disposition
constants, `OpenPr`, `disposition`, the three reason lines, the `Lane.close_pr` and `Lane.adopt`
hand-offs, and the spec's D7, D11, D12, D14, P9, P10, P12, PD10 and PD12. `conventions.legacy_review`
goes with it out of T-0745, being its only reader (D14). `tests.test_lane` and `asf/harvest/lane.py`
are left exactly as they are, which they would have been anyway: the Task added no line to the lane,
it called two of its methods.

**S-39154 is left uncovered by this drop** — it is §6's Story, and §6 is the cut. No Story is
reparented and no id is minted here; that is the operator's call on the Feature's `stories:` line,
and nothing above depends on it.
