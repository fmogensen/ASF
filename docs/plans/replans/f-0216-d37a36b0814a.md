# Replan F-0216 — cut to the defect the card's own title names

Spec: `docs/specs/f-0216.md` (approved). The plan this replaces: `docs/plans/f-0216.md`. Branch:
`plan/F-0216-replan`.

**One open Task, rewritten. One third of the spec cut, nothing added, no Task minted and none
dropped.** T-0699 landed and is kept exactly as it is, with every commit on its branch.

Every measurement below was re-taken in this worktree at `7d2ee49c6` — nine commits past the plan's

## Reading the decision

The card's own title names three clauses:

> **cloud is the default executor** — **local sessions only by exception**; **the host guard covers
> local only**

The spec's title (`docs/specs/f-0216.md:4`) names **four**:

> the allow-list of eleven kinds becomes a closed exception list of three · `cloud.default` is true
> unless the config writes false · **every local session names the one reason it stayed on the
> host** · the host guard — which now covers the local lane alone — keeps the S1 load bypass it
> loses the moment the cloud lane opens

The third of those four is in no clause of the card's title. It is the spec's §2 — `LOCAL_CAUSES`,
`local_cause()`, a cause on the row, a new session-ledger key, a suffix on the `launched` line and
two new fields on the `launch` event — a reporting surface the spec grew on top of the defect, and
the one thing the card never asked for. That is what the cut removes. The spec had already refused
the *reading* of that surface (C14: no table, no doctor row, no scorecard cause — "a reading is
worth building once somebody has seen a week of the numbers"); the decision goes one step further
and refuses the field itself.

Everything the card's title does name stays: the exception list (landed, T-0699), the default that
makes the host the exception (C5), and the host guard with the S1 bypass it loses the moment the
lane opens (C11, C12, C13).

**The reading rejected.** "The defect" could be read as the host-guard clause alone, dropping the
default flip too. Against: the card's title *opens* on `cloud is the default executor`, and the
binding phrase is "the defect **its own title** names" — the title is the boundary, and it names
the default in its first clause. Cutting that would leave the card titled after work it does not
do, and would make the decision a split rather than a cut. §2 is the only surface the title is
silent on, and so it is the whole of the cut.
