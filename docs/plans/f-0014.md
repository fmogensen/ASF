# Plan F-0014 — one table shape, one stamp, and the three views the console still lacks

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0014.md` (approved).
Branch: `plan/F-0014`.

Eight Tasks, cut the way the spec's own §1.3 cuts the change — "the first line, the last line, and
three new commands" — with the registry those three all stand on pulled out in front, and the
plugin, the doctor row and `asf init` behind.

Task 1 is `asf/views/header.py` and nothing else: the registry, `head()` and `command_key()`,
stdlib only, with no caller (§2.1). Task 2 moves the seventeen existing first lines onto it and
corrects the two stale docstrings (§2.1, §2.6). Task 3 is the stamp, printed once by the
dispatcher, and deletes the doctor's own (§2.2). Task 4 is `asf ci` (§2.3), Task 5 is
`asf health` and `asf performance` (§2.4, §2.5), Task 6 is the `roles` doctor row (§2.6, D8),
Task 7 is the plugin's fifteen skills plus §3.1's completeness and source guards (§2.6), and
Task 8 is `asf init`'s console hand-over (§2.6, D9).

Three files force most of the order. `asf/cli.py` is written by Task 3 (the stamp tail, the
docstring) and Task 5 (the two new commands), so they queue. `asf/doctor.py` is written by Task 2
(`== DOCTOR`, `== SCHEDULER`), Task 3 (deleting `print(stamp(…))`) and Task 6 (the `roles` row),
so those three queue. `asf/ci_pool.py` is written by Task 2 (`== CI RECONCILE`, `== CI RESERVE`)
and Task 4 (`required=False` and the view's parser), so those two queue. Everything else is
disjoint: Tasks 3 and 4 share nothing and run together, and so do Tasks 5 and 6.

Wave order: **1 → 2 → 3 ‖ 4 → 5 ‖ 6 → 7 → 8**.

§3.9's gate binds every Task, so every Gate below runs the four commands beside its own fence.

7. No `asf/views/__init__.py` change here — Task 2 owns that file's docstring.
