# Plan F-0300 — the rule pass rules, and the row it ruled does not come back

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0300.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0300`.

**Four Tasks.** The spec's `## The design` names four product files and three test modules, and
the seams between them are real ones: the table (`asf/evidence/rulepass.py`) is pure and reads
only landed readers; the feeder's stand-down is a membership test on one new kwarg and needs
nothing of the table; the wave's check and the `waivers` map need both of them landed; and the
status cell needs only the event name and the flag. Three of the four `writes:` sets are
disjoint, so the queue costs nothing the `after:` chain was not already paying.

Wave order: **1 → (2, 4) → 3**. Task 1 is `after: none` and starts at once. Task 2 (the feeder)
and Task 4 (the cell) wait on Task 1 only because their own Acceptance fences name
`tests.test_rulepass` and the flag (**PD2**). Task 3 waits on both: its `plan_inputs` key would
be a `TypeError` against a `plan_rows` that does not yet take `waived` (**PD2**).

Nothing in `## Out` is touched. `asf/evidence/rulings.py` is read and not changed — P4 and P5 are
re-measured here against the exact line this spec specifies (**PD9**), and they hold. `file_rulings`
is not changed: the pass writes its own line in that function's shape, from `screen()`.
`asf/workers/lifecycle.py` is read — `finding_of`, `repeats`, `corrections`, `correction_of`,
`REVIEW` — and not changed; the waiver is matched in `waivers`, outside the feeder (C8, C11).
`bug_rows` at the attempt limit and `_capped` at and above it launch exactly as today, and
`rulepass.dispute` answers `None` for both by construction (**PD11**). No lane state is written, no
correction is cleared, no branch moves, no PR is opened, and `precedent.brief_section` is still
not wired into the adjudicate brief — that is F-0262's residue and its own card.

Four things in the spec do not survive contact with the tree at this head, and all four are
corrections this plan makes rather than scope it adds: the `Screened` kinds tuple the spec rewrites
(**PD3**), the `Ruling` fields its own `screen()` snippet reads (**PD4**), the record root the ruling
is filed in on the wave-clock path (**PD5**), and C9's claim that the flag needs no registered key
(**PD14**). Two more are names the spec uses that do not exist: `WAITS_GATE` (**PD12**) and
`precedent.history_note` as a citation formatter (**PD7**).

## What this plan leaves where the spec left it

The two attempts-exhausted adjudicate rows launch as today: `bug_rows` at `n == limit` and
`_capped` at and above it are about three sessions having failed, not about a finding, and
`rulepass.dispute` answers `None` for both by construction (**PD11**). The code-pattern precedent

of the 93, not all of them. No lexical or semantic guess at a decision match is made: the
`precedent` rule fires only when the finding's own text *names* a `D-nnnn` or a path under
`decisions.DOCS_DIR` and the register confirms it with a live status (**PD15**).
