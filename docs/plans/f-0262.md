# Plan F-0262 — the register is supplied, the search is demanded, and the citation rides the park and the card

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0262.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0262`.

**Four Tasks, two waves.** The spec's change is a new reader and four places that consume it. The
reader is one module whose own test module exercises every one of its six functions; the four
consumers are four files in four packages with four separate test modules and no overlap. So:

1. **the register and the citation's grammar** — `asf/evidence/precedent.py` whole, and the one
   `precedent:` field in `asf/workers/report.py` that its grammar is read out of. Nothing in the
   factory behaves differently when it lands: no brief prints the field, no park reads it, no
   card carries it. That is why it goes first, and **PD1** is why the field is in this Task and
   not the next one.
2. **the brief** — the `TAIL` line, `PRECEDENT_KINDS`, `precedent_section`, the one term in
   `build()`'s part 3, the template's `BEFORE ANY NEEDS OPERATOR` block, the 19 goldens the
   `TAIL` line moves, and the one skill file that carries `TAIL`'s fenced block byte for byte
   (**PD2** — the spec's `## In` does not name it).
3. **the park** — `blocked_park_text`/`blocked_park`'s `precedent=` parameter, the one `health`
   call site that fills it for an adjudicate run, and the operating guide's paragraph.
4. **the card** — the clause `file_rulings` appends to the `## History` line it already writes.

They run **1 → (2 | 3 | 4)**. Tasks 2, 3 and 4 need Task 1's module and nothing of each other,
and their `writes:` are pairwise disjoint — `asf/briefs/*` + the goldens + the skill, against
`asf/workers/lifecycle.py` + `asf/workers/health.py` + the guide, against `asf/tick/step_health.py`
— so the feeder runs all three together.

**Read PD1, PD2, PD3, PD4, PD5, PD6 and PD9 before writing a line.** PD2 and PD3 are two readers
the spec's `## In` and `## Stories` do not name, and a Task that misses either lands red. PD4 is
the one acceptance test in the spec that cannot run as written against the spec's own fixtures,
and the fixture that makes it run. PD5 and PD6 are the two places the spec's design snippets name
a local variable that does not exist. PD9 is five cases that are **already red on the trunk**, in
a module Task 3's gate runs.

Everything below was measured in this checkout at `e4f8509`, with the design applied and then
reverted — not quoted from the spec. The spec measured itself at `d4e58e87b`; **PD12** records

1. `asf/evidence/precedent.py`'s constants and regexes, exactly as the spec's §1 writes them, with

## What this plan does not do

* **No new scope.** No `conventions.precedent_dir`, no new state file, no new event stream, no new
  metric, no new `asf` verb, no new record writer, no entry in `asf/surface.py` (C15, P14). The
  register is computed from tracked files and the index in the same pass that builds the brief, and
  is stored nowhere.
* **No narrowing of the four classes.** Licence, money, security and anything that changes what the
  customer sees stay the operator's, in the template's existing words (C1, Out (a)).
* **No automatic ruling.** Nothing here reads a decision and decides with it. The register is
  supplied; the judgement is the session's (C8, Out (b)).
* **No send-back, hold, relaunch or release** for a missing `precedent:` line (C12, Out (c)). The
  enforcement is the supply, the demand in the brief, and the visibility of the gap.
* **No change to any other kind's brief or park.** `precedent_section` and `park_note` return `''`
  off `adjudicate`, so every other kind's brief gains exactly the one shared `TAIL` line and its
  park is byte for byte what it is today (Out (d)).
* **No change to `asf.record.decisions`.** `docs_ids` keeps its signature and its return type, and
  its two existing callers are untouched (Out (e), C2). A public `decisions.file_id(name, head)`
  would be the tidier home for PD8's precedence, and the spec's `## In` does not carry
  `asf/record/decisions.py` — so this plan reads the three regexes where they are rather than
  widen the footprint, and names the refactor here as a card of its own, not as scope.
* **No Bug filed for PD9's five `TestSpawn` failures.** They are red on the trunk, one root cause,
  and nothing in F-0262's footprint reaches `spawn`. Recording them is this plan's job; fixing them
  is another card's.
