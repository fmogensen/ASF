# Plan F-0301 — the push is the gate: the door, the wire, the rubric, the number

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0301.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0301`.

Four Tasks, in the spec's own order of dependence. Task 1 is the **door**: the
`conventions.pre_push_checks` key and its validator, the whole new `asf/prepush.py`, the
`asf pre-push` command with both of its refusals, the one call in the shim, and the ledger the
metric will later count. Task 2 is the **wire** that arms the door's second refusal: `ASF_WRITES`,
computed in `briefs.build` where `items` is already in hand, carried onto `pool.Row` as
`card_digest` is, and set in `spawn`'s env. Task 3 is the **rubric**: one block in
`asf/briefs/build.py` over the landed `asf/precheck.py` and `asf/security/paths.py`, printed by the
five code templates. Task 4 is the **number**: `facts.landings`, `score.first_push_green_row` on
`window_row`, and `prepush_refused` written at health time.

The cut follows the spec's own files paragraph ("Five files gain surface and one is new"), with one
deliberate departure the spec's §3 forces and PD1 explains: the door ships **whole** in Task 1,
footprint refusal included, because step 2 of §3 is inert without `ASF_WRITES` ("With an empty
boundary nothing is tested"). Shipping the command once, fail-open, beats writing it twice. So
S-77505 sits on two Tasks — Task 1 owns its five door bullets, Task 2 its three wire bullets — and
the plan says on each Task which.

Wave order: **1 → (2, 4) → 3**.

Task 2 and Task 4 are genuinely parallel: Task 2 writes `asf/briefs/build.py`,
`asf/tick/step_wave.py`, `asf/workers/pool.py` and `asf/workers/spawn.py`; Task 4 writes
`asf/scorecard/*`, `asf/improve/measure.py` and `asf/workers/health.py`; their `writes:` are
disjoint on both sides, source and test. Task 3 intersects Task 2 on `asf/briefs/build.py` and
`tests/test_briefs.py`, so it is queued behind it whatever its `after:` said (PD1), and `after:
Task 2` makes the spec's ordering the record's. Task 4 needs Task 1 only for `prepush.caught` and
the ledger Task 1 writes.
