Epic: E-0004 — Control plane — the factory's dashboard, served from the record. Spec:
`docs/specs/f-0134.md` (approved). Branch: `plan/F-0134`.

Two Tasks, cut so that **no document says a thing the trunk has not yet started doing**:

1. **the parse, the two refusals, and the evidence** — `parse_inbox_file`'s first-line header test,
   `shape.derive`'s no-title guard, `question_lines`' file-name fallback, `apply_answer`'s insertion
   point, `_typed`'s rewritten sentence with its `NEEDS` table, the unknown-type sentence, and the

2. **the guide paragraph** — `docs/guide/operating.md`'s inbox paragraph and the prose test that
   holds it. Disjoint in footprint from Task 1 and provable on its own.

They run **1 → 2**, one at a time. The order is not a footprint collision — the two write no file in
common — it is the card itself: the paragraph Task 2 writes states that a declared type the card is
not shaped like is *refused*, and until Task 1 lands, it is not. Landing Task 2 first would put a
false sentence about intake in the customer-facing guide, which is precisely the defect this card
exists to remove (P7), with the sign flipped. One Task changes what intake does; one tells the
customer what it does, after it does it (PD13).

**Read PD1, PD2 and PD3 before writing a line of Task 1.** Three of the spec's S-39251 fixtures do
not reach the sentence they assert unless the card is shaped to get past two earlier questions, the
spec's own case list is one short of its own count, and the guard of C3 stops something worse than
the misleading question C3 names — it stops a mint. All three are measured below.

The fences of §*The fences, and the suite* bind both Tasks, so every Gate and Acceptance block below
