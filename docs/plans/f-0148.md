# Plan F-0148 — the pure total, the reader and its command, the rollup's first line, the guide

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0148.md` (approved). Branch: `plan/F-0148`.

Four Tasks, cut exactly the way the spec's `## Stories` are cut and the way `## The design` is
sectioned: the **pure half** (the total, the two deltas, the line, and the one signed-delta renderer
the `--by-lane` view stops owning), the **reader and its command** (`loop.totals`,
`asf scorecard --all`, the flag), the **rollup's first line** (`render_daily`'s new argument and
`cmd_rollup`'s guarded read), and the **guide**.

Task 1 owns `asf/scorecard/score.py` whole and the `_delta_cell` delegation in
`asf/views/scorecard.py`. Task 2 owns `asf/scorecard/loop.py`, the rest of `asf/views/scorecard.py`
and the one flag in `asf/cli.py` — plus one line of `asf/cli.py`'s dispatch the spec does not know it
needs (PD1). Task 3 owns `asf/metrics/metrics.py` and `tests/test_metrics.py`. Task 4 owns
`docs/guide/operating.md` and `tests/test_readme.py`.

Wave order: **1 → 2 → {3, 4}** — in `after:` terms, 1 with `none`, 2 after 1, 3 after 1 and 2, 4
after 2. Tasks 3 and 4 share no file with each other and run together. Tasks 1 and 2 do share two
files (PD2), which costs nothing because 2 waits on 1 regardless: it calls `score.total_row`,
`score.delta_row` and `score.total_line`, none of which exist until Task 1 lands.

§Acceptance tests' fences `tests.test_scorecard tests.test_direct_lane tests.test_cli` and the
whole-suite-plus-fences one bind every Task, so every Gate and every Acceptance block below carries
them beside its own.

**The one thing this plan adds to the spec's design, and why the card's own acceptance is false
without it:** `asf/cli.py:539-541` resolves a record — and therefore a product — for every view
command *before* `cmd_scorecard` is ever entered. PD1 has the measurement and the five-line fix.
Read it before Task 2.

### Task 1: the pure half — one total, one delta, one renderer the view stops owning
stories: S-34950
writes: asf/scorecard/score.py, asf/views/scorecard.py, tests/test_scorecard.py
after: none

The spec's first three **In** bullets and `## The design` §`asf/scorecard/score.py`, plus the
four-line `_delta_cell` delegation from §`asf/views/scorecard.py` (D9 — it is part of this Story,
whose acceptance names `tests.test_direct_lane`). Everything here is pure: no file, no clock, no
product. Nothing calls `total_row`, `delta_row` or `total_line` yet — Task 2 and Task 3 do — so on
this Task alone the only observable change in the whole tree is that `--by-lane`'s delta cells are
rendered by a function one module down, byte for byte identically. That is the point: the pure half
lands provable first.

**Files**

- `asf/scorecard/score.py` — `TOTAL_SUMS`, `TOTAL_MEDIANS`, `CLUTTER_SUMS`, `DELTA_KEYS`;
  `total_row`, `_clutter_total`, `delta_row`, `delta`, `total_line`
- `asf/views/scorecard.py` — `_delta_cell` (`:165-176`) rewritten over `score.delta`
- `tests/test_scorecard.py` — `TotalRowTests`, `TotalLineTests`

**Steps**

1. `asf/scorecard/score.py`, at the bottom beside `headline_line` and `lanes_line` (P17, verified at
   `:392-428`): the four module constants with the spec's `#:` comment on each, byte for byte —
   `TOTAL_SUMS` (thirteen keys), `TOTAL_MEDIANS` (three), `CLUTTER_SUMS`
   (`open_prs`, `stale_prs`, `branches`), `DELTA_KEYS` (six, in print order). They are constants
   because the total, the view's table and the line all read the same sets.
2. `total_row(rows)` with the spec's docstring and body byte for byte. Do not add
   `repair_sessions` to the int-coercion tuple — PD10 is why. The three ratios are recomputed over
   the sums and are `None` when `landed` is `0`, which is the "a week where nothing landed" case of
   the S-34950 fence and the reason there is no division by zero.
3. `_clutter_total(rows)` to PD8's contract, which is the spec's one sentence made exact: each of
   `CLUTTER_SUMS` summed over the rows whose `clutter` block carries it as a number, and `None` for
   a key no row could read. Reuse `_num`'s own test (`:65-66`) for "is a number" rather than writing
   a second one — `isinstance(v, (int, float))` and not `bool`. A row whose `clutter` is absent,
   `None` or not a dict contributes to no key and raises nothing.
4. `delta_row(now, prev, keys=DELTA_KEYS)` with the spec's docstring byte for byte: `now[k] - prev[k]`
   for every key **both** rows carry as a number; a key missing or `None` on either side is absent
   from the result, never `0`. `now` or `prev` being `None` or empty yields `{}`.
5. `delta(v, money=False, unit='')` with the spec's docstring and body byte for byte. Its output must
   equal today's `_delta_cell` for all three keyings, which is what step 6 and the Gate's first line
   prove: `money=True, unit=''` is the `cost` form, `money=False, unit='d'` the `lead_days` form,
   and `money=False, unit=''` every other. `format(abs(v), 'g')` and `f'{abs(v):g}'` are the same
   call — do not change which one is written, the spec's body uses `format`.
6. `asf/views/scorecard.py`: replace `_delta_cell`'s five-line body (`:166-176`) with the spec's one
   line, `return score.delta(v, money=(key == 'cost'), unit='d' if key == 'lead_days' else '')`.
   Keep the function's name, its signature and its position; `score` is already imported at `:13`.
   `PAIR_METRICS` keying stays in the view (D9). Nothing else in that file moves in this Task.
7. `total_line(totals)` with the spec's docstring byte for byte, rendering the spec's three worked
   examples exactly:
   - measured, with deltas —
     `scorecard week 2026-09-21 (day 3/7, 3 products): 7 on prod (+2) · 11 landed (+3) · $38.40/feature all-in (-$6.10) · $21.00/feature own (-$1.40) · 2.1 repair sessions/feature (-0.4) · $422.40 all-in (+$40.00)`
   - measured, nothing to compare against — the same shape with every delta `—`;
   - nothing measured — `scorecard: no weekly snapshot yet — the daily step writes the first one`.

   The separator is ` · `, the six numbers are `DELTA_KEYS` in that order, each delta is `delta(…)`
   in parentheses, money through `_money` and the two counts through `format(v, 'g')` — the existing
   renderers at `:407-412`, never a new one. The line names the product **count** and no product
   name (D6); a coder must not add one, and the Gate's `check_generic.sh` is the second net under
   that. No median is printed (D4).
8. The no-snapshot guard covers **both** inputs (PD9): `totals is None`, and an envelope whose
   `total` is missing or carries a falsy `products`. Both produce the same sentence.
9. `TotalRowTests(unittest.TestCase)` in `tests/test_scorecard.py`, one case per clause of the
   spec's first fence: the thirteen summing keys added; `usd_per_feature` is `Σusd / Σlanded` over
   every product and is asserted **unequal** to the mean of the per-product values on rows chosen so
   the two differ; `own_usd_per_feature` is the landed-weighted mean, asserted exact against the own
   spend it was built from (P3's identity, checked arithmetically and not to a tolerance);
   `TOTAL_MEDIANS` are all `None`; clutter summed, with a key no row read staying `None` and a key
   every row read as `0` staying `0`; a week where `landed` is `0` giving three `None` ratios and no
   `ZeroDivisionError`; and `total_row([])` giving `products: 0`, `landed: 0`, `week: None`. Build
   the rows as literal dicts in the test — the spec's fence is over stored rows, so nothing here
   needs `Facts`, a file or a clock.
10. `TotalLineTests(unittest.TestCase)`, one case per clause of the second fence: `delta_row` skips
    a key missing on either side and a key `None` on either side, and does **not** read either as
    zero; `delta` renders `+3`, `-$6.10`, `+2.5 d`, `0` and `—` for the five inputs the spec names;
    the line carries all six headline numbers each with its change, names the week, the day of the
    week and the product count, and contains **no** product name (assert the names of the products
    in the fixture are absent from the string, not merely that a count is present);
    `total_line(None)` is the no-snapshot sentence; and — PD9 — an envelope with `products: 0` is
    the same sentence.
