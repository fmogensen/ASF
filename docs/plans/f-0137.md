# Plan F-0137 — three Tasks, three waves: the record, the doors, the readers

Epic: E-0002 — ASF 0.2 — self-improvement. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0137.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0137`, cut from `origin/main` at `50c653ba7`.

**Three Tasks, three waves, strictly sequential — and the sequence is not a preference, it is the
shape of the spec's own acceptance.** The card is one predicate with five readers, so the split is
the obvious one: mint the predicate, then close the two doors, then make the three readers say it.

1. **Task 1 — §1, the record, the predicate and the two verbs.** New `asf/pause.py`
   (`PAUSE_FILE`, `pause_path`, `read`, `held`, `text`, `hold_reason`, `pause`, `resume`,
   `register`, `cmd_pause`, `cmd_resume`), the two parsers wired into `asf/cli.py`, and
   `tests/test_pause.py` with `TheRecord` and `TheCommands`. `after: none` — it reads nothing this
   card writes.
2. **Task 2 — §2 and §3, the two doors.** The wave's hold at the launch decision and its
   `wanted = 0` demand write in `asf/tick/step_wave.py`; the cold retry's hold in
   `asf/tick/step_health.py`; `TheWaveHolds` and `TheOtherDoor` in `tests/test_pause.py`.
   `after: Task 1` — both doors call `pause.held`, which does not exist until Task 1 lands.
3. **Task 3 — §4, the three readers.** `dry_run._wave_rows`, `status.paused_cell`,
   `doctor.scheduler_rows`; `TheSlotsAreLent` and `TheOperatorCanSee` in `tests/test_pause.py`.
   `after: Task 2` — `TheSlotsAreLent` asserts the `wanted = 0` line **Task 2** writes (PD1), and
   all three readers call `pause.held`.

Wave order: **1**, then **2**, then **3**. One seat per wave. PD1 measures why there is no parallel
wave here and declines to manufacture one.

Read **PD4 before writing `held`** — an `isinstance(product, env.Product)` resolution, the one
`env.state_dir` uses, silently reads the *operator's own* product pause for the
`types.SimpleNamespace` stand-in three shipped suites hand the tick. Read **PD15 before writing
`pause_path`** — routing it through `env.state_dir` turns a predicate read on every wave row into a
`mkdir`, and `tests/test_dry_run.py:71` checksums that directory before and after the rehearsal.
Read **PD5 before touching `doctor`** — the spec's cited line sits *below* a `return`, and below a
RED guard the row must not disturb. Read **PD6 before touching `step_wave`** — the block has four
neighbours and only one gap satisfies all of them.
