# Plan F-0141 — two Tasks, two waves: the recogniser with both seams and its own self-check, then the doctor row that says what it checks

Epic: E-0002 — ASF 0.2 — self-improvement. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0141.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0141`.

**Two Tasks, two waves.** Story 1 is the whole gate — `asf/debug_toggles.py`, the three defaults
and the shape check in `asf/conventions.py`, `diff()` promoted in `asf/customer_content.py`,
`is_doc()` split out of `lane.landing_class`, and the refusal chained onto both of the marker
gate's existing seams. Story 2 is `debug_toggles.findings` and the one `debug toggles` row in
`asf/doctor.py`. Story 2's function lives **inside the module Story 1 creates**, so it cannot go
first and the two cannot go together: `after: Task 1`.

1. **Task 1 — a branch that adds a switched-off check is refused, at both seams.**
   `asf/debug_toggles.py`, `asf/conventions.py`, `asf/customer_content.py`, `asf/harvest/lane.py`,
   `tests/test_debug_toggles.py`. `after: none`.
2. **Task 2 — `asf doctor` says what the gate checks on this product.**
   `asf/debug_toggles.py`, `asf/doctor.py`, `tests/test_doctor.py`. `after: Task 1`.

Wave order: **1** → **2**. The two `writes:` sets intersect on `asf/debug_toggles.py` and that is
accepted rather than worked around (PD7): the `after:` edge already serialises them, so the queue
costs nothing it was not going to cost.

**Read PD2 before writing a single line of Task 2.** It is the one finding that changes what a card
must do: §3.5's assertion shape indexes the wrong element of the doctor's row tuple, and written as
the spec writes it the test cannot see its own row. **PD3 before touching `lane_refusal`** — it has
four callers, not the two P2 counts. **PD4 before writing any fixture into
`tests/test_debug_toggles.py`**: the waiver is what makes this branch landable by the gate it adds,
and the order the coder writes the two halves in decides whether the branch can be pushed at all.
