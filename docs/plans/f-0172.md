# Plan F-0172 — one retirement rule first, then the word that says it

Epic: E-0004 — Control plane — the factory's dashboard, served from the record. Spec:
`docs/specs/f-0172.md` (approved). Branch: `plan/F-0172`.

Two Tasks, one per Story — and **not** in the spec's order, and **not** order-free.

1. **the rule** — `index_reader.live` and `retired_done` drop and set aside on `removed:` *or*
   `moved_to:`, behind a fence test pinning the local predicate against
   `asf.record.core.is_retired`. From here a moved Feature is out of every table and is handed no
   feeder row, and the launch hazard the card reports is closed.
2. **the word** — `retired_stage` in `asf.record.core`, written onto a retired Feature's card by
   `ingest` as a stage-only write, and derived into the index entry by `build_index_data`. From here
   `index.json` — the artifact the customer install was reading — says `removed` /
   `moved → <target>` instead of the ladder word the card was left on.

They run **1 → 2**, one at a time. Their `writes:` are disjoint, so the queue would happily run them
together; the `after:` is a decision, and PD3 is the measurement behind it. **Landing Task 2 first,
or alone, is strictly worse than head**: on a `lane: direct` Feature the retirement label is *not*
inert — it converts a non-launching `APPROVED → LAND` wait row into a launching
`DIRECT → BUILD, would launch`, which is a full build session started on a Feature whose work moved
to another record. That is the spec's own C1 hazard, in the arm C1 did not measure.

**Read PD3 before writing a line of either Task.** It is the one place this plan overrules the spec
(C9, "the two Stories are order-free … a half-landed card is never worse than head" — measurably
false). PD5, PD6, PD7 and PD8 are the four places where copying a spec snippet or an acceptance
sentence literally gives a coder something that does not compile, writes into the wrong class, or
asserts against a module that does not import what it needs.

       """True for an index entry the record retires: ``removed:`` or ``moved_to:``.

       The same rule as :func:`asf.record.core.is_retired`, spelled again here because this module
       is stdlib-only by contract and imports nothing from ``asf.record``. The two are pinned equal

       retired (:func:`retired` — ``removed:`` or ``moved_to:``), with the retired ones that are

       out = Items((k, v) for k, v in raw.items() if isinstance(v, dict) and not retired(v))
       kept = dict(getattr(raw, 'retired_done', {}))
       kept.update((k, v) for k, v in raw.items()
