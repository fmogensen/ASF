# Plan F-0332 — the registrations, then the ceiling with its gate, then the pin

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0332.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0332`.

Three Tasks, and the cut is forced rather than chosen. Task 1 is the **two flag names**: the three
registrations a behaviour switch needs (`KNOWN_FLAGS`, `_PLANNED_FLAG_VALUES`, the example yaml),
which land first because `loops.settings` reads the flags through `conv.flag('…')` and
`tests/test_conventions_flags.py` walks `asf/` for exactly that call (S-99856). Task 2 is the
**ceiling and its gate in one diff**: `loops.TOTAL_CAP`, `TOTAL_KINDS`, `judge`'s third block,
`settings`' four keys, `replay`'s new parameter, the kind gate in `relaunch.loop_guard` and the one
keyword that carries the launch's kind from `step_wave.relaunch_assessment` — together, because
measured, §1 without §2 applies the ceiling to **every** job kind and the suite stays green while it
does (**PD3**). Task 3 is the **pin**: the new anonymised fixture and `TotalReplayPinTests`
(S-99859).

Wave order: **1 → 2 → 3**, three waves of one Task. Tasks 1 and 2 have disjoint `writes:` and are
still ordered, by `after:`, for the registration edge above. Task 3 writes `tests/test_loops.py`,
which Task 2 also writes, so the record would queue it behind Task 2 whatever this plan said; its
`after: Task 2` states the real edge as well, which is `loops.replay`'s new `total_cap` parameter.
There is no parallelism to buy here and the plan does not pretend otherwise (**PD10**).

The plan carries the spec's scope and no more. Two rows below correct it: its §1a undercounts the
shipped assertions this change reddens — six, not three, measured (**PD2**) — and it does not name
the ordering hazard its own D8 creates (**PD3**). Neither widens the work; both are about what the
coder will find on the first run.
