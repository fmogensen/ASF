# Plan F-0100 — the savings pass, and the two measurements it stands on

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0100.md` (approved). Branch: `plan/F-0100`.

Eight Tasks, cut along the spec's own §2 sections by the thing each one writes: the two new streams
(Task 1), the ledger the detached gate leaves behind (Task 2), the conventions and the doctor row
every reading is measured against (Task 3), the join that answers the card's first question
(Task 4), the six signals over it (Task 5), the filer the proposals go through (Task 6), the
scorecard that renders them (Task 7) and the command and the daily part that run the whole pass
(Task 8).

The spec's two halves are the plan's two halves. Tasks 1–4 build **measurements** — a landing, a
gate run, and minutes and dollars per landed change — and none of them proposes anything. Tasks 5–8
build the **pass**: the six detectors, the card, the page and the cadence. Nothing in the second
half can be tested before the first half exists, which is why the chain is as long as it is.

The spec was written against `2c39ede`; the base has moved 21 commits and `asf/harvest/harvest.py`
has gained 233 lines in them. PD4, PD5 and PD6 are where §2.2's gate design meets the harvest as it
now stands, and PD11 is where §2.5's approval class meets a test that closes the catalogue. Each
says what changed, what the spec asks for, and which Task closes the gap. No scope is added: every
one of them is a section of the spec, met where it actually lands.

Wave order: **1 ‖ 3 → 2 ‖ 4 → 5 → 6 ‖ 7 → 8**.

§3.7's rule that the whole suite stays green binds every Task, so every Gate below runs
`python3 tools/run_tests.py` beside its own fence.

### Task 1: the `landings` and `gates` streams, and the two keys the tick already writes
stories: S-17400, S-17401, S-17410
writes: asf/metrics/metrics.py, asf/init.py, tests/test_metrics.py, tests/test_tick.py
after: none

§2.1, §2.2's stream half, §4's stream folders, and the `SCHEMAS['ticks']` line of §1.1. Two streams
beside `ci`, `sessions` and `ticks`, their validators, their natural keys, the two readers that
fill them, and the backfill that calls both. Nothing writes the gate ledger yet — Task 2 does; this
Task reads one where it finds one, which is what makes `gates_from_ledger` testable on its own.

**Files**

- `asf/metrics/metrics.py` — `STREAMS` (`:37`) gains `'landings'` and `'gates'`, so `metrics append`
  takes them too (its `choices` is `STREAMS`); `SCHEMAS` (`:112`) gains §2.1's and §2.2's two dicts
  as fenced, and `SCHEMAS['ticks']` gains `'steps': (('list',), [])` and
  `'product': (('str', 'null'), None)` (P6); `validate` (`:179`) gains a `landings` arm and a
  `gates` arm **before** the `if stream != 'ticks'` guard (`:231`, P7); `natural_key` (`:259`) gains
  both; `landings_from_registry` and `gates_from_ledger` — new, beside `sessions_from_registry`
  (`:1008`); `cmd_backfill` (`:1110`) calls both and prints their counts.
- `asf/init.py` — `STREAM_FOLDERS` (`:28`) gains `'metrics/landings'` and `'metrics/gates'` (P8).
- `tests/test_metrics.py` — `LandingStreamTests` and `GateStreamTests` (new, §3.1); `Schema:139`
  gains the two ticks keys.
- `tests/test_tick.py` — `TickLineTests` (new, PD16).

**Steps**

1. The two schemas, byte for byte as §2.1 and §2.2 fence them, and the two names into `STREAMS`.
2. `SCHEMAS['ticks']` gains `steps` and `product` — the two keys `tick_line` (`tick.py:412`) already
   writes raw. Nothing in `tick.py` changes; the point is that what it writes would now survive
   `metrics append ticks`.
3. `validate`'s landings arm: `kind` falls back to `session_kind`'s mapping and must be in `KINDS`;
   `item` matched exactly as a `sessions` event's is; `ts` required and never `TS` — a landing's
   time is never "now". Its gates arm: `conclusion` in `('success', 'failure')`, `seconds >= 0`,
   every entry of `branches` a string.
4. `natural_key`: `landings` → `(ev['job'], ev['sha'])`; `gates` → `(ev['ts'], tuple(ev['branches']))`.
5. `landings_from_registry(product, items=None, known=(), state_path=None, repo=None)` per §2.1:

   `harvest.SUPERSEDED` and whose `(job, sha)` is not in `known`; `ts` from
   `git show -s --format=%cI <sha>` in `repo or product.repo_dir` (PD10), through
   `iso(parse_ts(...))`; no date → skipped and counted as unconfirmed, never invented (§1.2).
   `kind` from `session_kind(record)`, `branch` from the record, `item` as `session_event` takes it.
   Returns the events and the unconfirmed count.
6. `gates_from_ledger(product, known=())`: reads `<state dir>/gates.jsonl` line by line, one event
   per line, `signature` truncated to 120, a line whose natural key is already in the stream
   skipped. A missing ledger is no lines, not an error — this Task lands before its writer.
7. `cmd_backfill`: both readers after the sessions loop, each folder `os.makedirs`'d before its
   first append (§4), then `landings appended: n` and, when any were skipped,
   `landings unconfirmed: n (sha not in the trunk — retried next tick)`.
8. `STREAM_FOLDERS`, so `asf init` lays the two folders down and `asf check` stops reporting them
   missing (P8) — `tests/test_backlog.py:281` walks that tuple and covers the new entries for free.
9. `LandingStreamTests` and `GateStreamTests` per §3.1, over a fixture registry and a fixture repo
   whose commits have known committer dates. `TickLineTests`: `tick_line(ctx, ran)` put through
   `validate('ticks', …)` — no unknown key, no missing required one, `steps` a list of the step
   dicts and `product` the product's name.

### Task 2: every landing gate writes one timed, named ledger line
stories: S-17401
writes: asf/harvest/harvest.py, tests/test_harvest.py
after: Task 1

§2.2's ledger half. The landing gate runs in a detached process with no record clone and no `ctx`
(P9), so what it learns leaves through an append-only file in the state dir — the same directory
and the same shape `mark_harvested` already appends to. Two call sites become three functions'
worth of threading, because `confirmed_group` now sits where §2.2 expected `land_combined` (PD5).

**Files**

- `asf/harvest/harvest.py` — `record_gate(state_dir, branches, sha, ok, seconds, line)` — new,
  beside `mark_harvested` (`:206`), with PD6 in its docstring; `land_ff`'s `product_gate` call
  (`:1004`) timed and recorded; `gate_groups(…, ledger=None)` (`:1065`) recording its own call and
  passing `ledger` down both recursive calls; `confirmed_group(…, ledger=None)` (`:1096`) passing it
  through (PD5); `land_combined` (`:1124`) passing
  `lambda branches, sha, ok, seconds, line: record_gate(state_dir, branches, sha, ok, seconds, line)`.
- `tests/test_harvest.py` — `GateLedgerTests` (new, §3.2).

**Steps**

1. `record_gate` appends one JSON line to `os.path.join(state_dir, 'gates.jsonl')`, sorted keys, in
   §2.2's fenced shape: `at` (UTC ISO, the moment the gate **started**), `seconds`, `branches`,
   `sha`, `ok`, `line`. A write that fails is printed and never raised — a gate must not be lost
   because a ledger could not be appended to, exactly as `write_tick_line` already reasons.
2. `land_ff`: `time.monotonic()` around its `product_gate` call, `sha` from `git rev-parse HEAD` in
   the throwaway worktree, one line per call — so the `not_ff` retry writes its own (PD7).
   `state_dir` is already a parameter.
3. `gate_groups` takes `ledger=None` and calls it after its own `product_gate` returns, with the
   stacked branches, the head, `ok`, the seconds and the first failing line. `None` writes nothing,
   which is what every existing caller and every existing test passes. Both recursive calls pass
   `ledger` on, so a bisect writes one line per group it gated, and a targeted re-run one of its own
   (PD5).
4. `confirmed_group` takes `ledger=None` and passes it into every `gate_groups` call of its
   confirmation loop; `land_combined` builds the lambda from its own `state_dir`.
5. `red_on_trunk` is left alone, and `record_gate`'s docstring says why (PD6).
6. `GateLedgerTests` per §3.2: a per-branch landing appends one line with the branch, the sha and
   `ok: true`; a combined gate over three branches appends one line naming all three; a red combined
   gate that bisects appends one line per group it gated, each carrying the first failing line;
   `gate_groups` with no `ledger` writes nothing; and the file the harvest wrote is read back by
   `gates_from_ledger` into a valid `gates` event (the two halves of S-17401 meeting).

### Task 4: the join — minutes and dollars per landed change, per kind
stories: S-17402
writes: asf/savings/__init__.py, asf/savings/landed.py, tests/test_savings.py
after: Task 1

§2.3, and the one thing the card says the record cannot yet answer. Pure functions over three event
lists: no I/O, no product, no record — which is what lets the whole measurement be proven over
hand-built events before a single real landing exists.

**Files**

- `asf/savings/__init__.py` — **new**, empty but for the package docstring: what the pass is, and
  that it measures and proposes and acts on nothing (§1.2).
- `asf/savings/landed.py` — **new**: `Landed`, `landed_changes(landings, sessions, gates)`,
  `per_kind(rows)`.
- `tests/test_savings.py` — **new**: `LandedTests` (§3.3).

**Steps**

1. `Landed`, the frozen dataclass §2.3 fences, field for field.
2. `landed_changes`: one `Landed` per landing event; its sessions are every `sessions` event with
   the same `branch` and `ts <= landing.ts` — the lane's own rounds, the worker's and its
   corrections', reviews' and fixes'. A landing with no branch falls back to the events with the
   same `item` and kind.
3. `minutes`, `usd` and `in_tokens` are the sums over those, `None`-safe: `usd` stays `None` when no
   session carried one and is never `0.0`. `rounds` is `max(round or 1)`, `sessions` their count.
4. `gate_minutes` is `Σ (g.seconds / 60) / len(g.branches)` over every `gates` event whose
   `branches` contains the lane branch and whose `ts <= landing.ts` — a combined gate's minutes
   split over what it gated, the rule `compute_costs` already applies to a CI run's minutes
   (`metrics.py:356`). `gates` is how many such events there were.
5. `per_kind(rows)` → `{kind: {'landings', 'minutes', 'usd', 'gate_minutes', 'rounds',
   'in_tokens'}}` plus an `all` row; medians through `statistics.median` over the non-`None` values
   and `rounds` a mean (P16 is the shape, `asf/views/tokens.py:49`). A cell with nothing in it is
   `None` — printed as `—` by its callers, never `0`.
6. `LandedTests` per §3.3: a lane of four sessions and one landing sums to one row with `rounds` 2
   and the four sessions' minutes; a session that ended *after* the landing is not counted; a
   combined gate over two branches charges each half its minutes; `usd` stays `None` when no session
   carried one; `per_kind` medians per kind and the `all` row; a kind with one landing has a median
   and raises no mean error.

### Task 5: the six signals, each with its reading, window, expected move and measurement
stories: S-17405
writes: asf/savings/signals.py, tests/test_savings.py
after: Task 3, Task 4

§2.4. One detector per row of the card's table, all six returning the same `Signal`, all six reading
the same window, and each carrying the number that would close it. Nothing here files anything —
`signals()` returns every class, over its threshold or not, and the caller decides.

**Files**

- `asf/savings/signals.py` — **new**: `Signal`, `signals(streams, conv, day)`, one detector per
  class, and `DETECTORS` — the `{class: detector}` dict §2.8's re-read looks a card up in.
- `tests/test_savings.py` — `SignalTests` (new, §3.4).

**Steps**

1. `Signal` as §2.4 fences it: `cls`, `subject`, `reading`, `threshold`, `window_days`,
   `expected_pct`, `title`, `sentence`, `evidence`, and the `over` property (`reading > threshold`,
   so a reading exactly at its threshold is not over).
2. `expected_pct = round(100 * (reading - threshold) / reading)` — computed, never chosen (§1.4).
3. The six detectors of §2.4's table, each reading `savings_for(conv, …)` for its threshold and the
   window `savings_for(conv, 'window_days')` ending at `day`:
   `brief_tokens` (per brief kind, median `in_tokens` over all sessions' median, `preamble_ratio`);
   `gate_minutes` (median `gate_minutes` per landed change, `gate_minutes_max`);
   `rounds` (per kind, mean rounds per landed change, the kind that rounds most, `rounds_per_landing`);
   `step_duration` (per tick step, today's median `seconds` over the window's, `step_duration_ratio`);
   `model_spend` (per kind, median `usd` per landed change over the median of the per-kind medians,
   `spend_ratio`); `failure_class` (per `gates.signature`, occurrences in the window,
