# Plan F-0259 — an urgent intake card is answered by the record, not by a wait

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0259.md` (approved; one spec
round and two spec-amend rounds landed — `11b23ae`, `28ce915` — the last of which minted the four
Stories this plan covers: S-60854, S-60855, S-60856, S-60857). Branch: `plan/F-0259`.

**Why this plan exists now, written by the adjudicator rather than a fourth round of spec work:**
the per-item attempt cap counts every session against the Feature regardless of stage (the same
mechanism F-0253 hit, `945cc3e`). By the time F-0259's stage reached `spec-approved`, the budget
had already been spent on the spec round and its two amend rounds — the first two of which could
not reach the backlog record to mint the Story ids they needed and had to retry — so the card
escalated straight to STALEMATE → ADJUDICATE with **zero plan attempts** behind it. A spec that
converged after three rounds of real review, with its Stories minted and its preconditions
verified against a real checkout, is not waiting on anything a fourth attempt would supply; it was
waiting on someone to write the plan. This document is that plan.

Five Tasks, read off the spec's own "In" list (§*In, and out*):

1. **the vocabulary** — `BY_RULE` beside `RULES`, `asf/groom/shape.py` (§1).

   `supplied`, and the four resolution helpers (`nearest_parent`, `best_epic`, `supplied_parent`,

   `asf/groom/groom.py` (C15).
