# Plan F-0102 — Deliveries: one plan, one agent, one gate for several small items

Epic: E-0002 — ASF 0.2 — self-improvement. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0102.md` (approved). Branch: `plan/F-0102`.

Eight Tasks, cut along the spec's own `## The design` headings — one Task per module the spec
names, because that is also the footprint boundary. The spec adds a *rule* (`asf/groom/delivery.py`),
a *verb* (`asf/groom/groom.py`), a *lane* (`asf/feeder/rows.py`), two *briefs* (`asf/briefs`), a
*lane refusal* (`asf/harvest`), a *stage* (`asf/record/ingest.py`), a *validation*
(`asf/record/check.py`) and a *table* (`asf/metrics/metrics.py`). No two of those write the same
file, so seven of the eight Tasks are footprint-disjoint and start at once; only Task 2 waits,
because it imports the module Task 1 creates.

Wave order: **1 ‖ 3 ‖ 4 ‖ 5 ‖ 6 ‖ 7 ‖ 8 → 2**.

Nothing in this plan is reachable in production until Task 2 lands: `delivers:` is written by
exactly one thing, the groom's `deliver` branch of `apply_proposal`, and every other Task reads
that field. That is what makes the parallel wave safe (PD11), and it is also why Task 2 is last.

The spec's `## The whole suite and the gates` binds every Task: each Gate below runs its own
fence, then `python3 tools/run_tests.py`, `bash tools/check_generic.sh` and
`bash tools/check_conventions.sh`.

### Task 1: the selection rule, the budget and the three ceilings
stories: S-17450
writes: asf/groom/delivery.py, asf/conventions.py, docs/products.example.yaml, tests/test_delivery.py, tests/test_conventions.py
after: none

The spec's `### asf/groom/delivery.py (new)` and `### asf/conventions.py and docs/products.example.yaml`.
Pure functions over `canonical` and three integers: which items are deliverable, how they group,
what fits a budget, the proposal lines that come out, and — for Task 2 — `deliveries` and
`releasable`. It imports `asf.feeder.footprint` and `asf.groom.shape`, never the other way round.

**Files**

- `asf/groom/delivery.py` — **new**. Module docstring naming F-0102 and the import direction.
  `TOKENS_PER_GLOB = 2000`, `CHARS_PER_TOKEN = 4` (PD13), and
  `Member = namedtuple('Member', 'id type title writes rank tokens')`. The seven functions of the
  spec's block: `is_small`, `deliverable_items`, `estimate_tokens`, `group`, `budget_cut`,
  `delivery_proposals`, `deliveries`, `releasable`.
- `asf/conventions.py` — three module-level defaults beside `DEFAULT_BATCH_MAX_GLOBS` (`:74`),
  each with its one-line doc comment: `DEFAULT_DELIVERY_MAX_ITEMS = 4`,
  `DEFAULT_DELIVERY_MAX_GLOBS = 6`, `DEFAULT_DELIVERY_MAX_TOKENS = 60000`; and three fields on
  `Conventions` beside `batch_max_globs` (`:150`): `delivery_max_items: int`,
  `delivery_max_globs: int`, `delivery_max_tokens: int`.
- `docs/products.example.yaml` — three commented-out lines at their defaults under
  `conventions:`, after `batch_max_globs` (`:49`), under one comment naming
  `asf.groom.delivery` and saying that `delivery_max_items: 0` turns deliveries off.
- `tests/test_delivery.py` — **new**: `SmallTest`, `EligibleTest`, `GroupTest`, `BudgetTest`,
  `ReleasableTest`, built on plain `canonical` dicts (`{id: {'meta': {...}}}`), no repo.
- `tests/test_conventions.py` — `DeliveryCeilingTests`: the three defaults, the three names in
  `Conventions.field_names()`, and a yaml `delivery_max_items: 0` reaching the field (PD14).

**Steps**

1. `asf/conventions.py`: the three defaults and the three fields. Run
   `python3 -m asf.conventions --forbidden` and confirm the generated pattern list is unchanged —
   integers are not path-shaped (PD13).
2. `docs/products.example.yaml`: the three commented lines and their comment.
3. `is_small(rec, batch_max_globs)` — D5, in the spec's order: `size: small` on the card wins;
   else a Bug is small when `severity` is `S2` or `S3` (and never when `S1`); else a Feature or a
   Story is small when it has no child in `canonical` (PD12) and `len(writes) <= batch_max_globs`.
4. `deliverable_items(canonical, batch_max_globs)` — every clause of the spec's bullet list, in
   that order, returning `[Member]` sorted `(rank, id)` with `rank` from `shape._rank` (PD12) and
   `tokens` from `estimate_tokens`.
5. `estimate_tokens(rec, writes)` — D8: the card's Description, Acceptance and Fix text divided by
   `CHARS_PER_TOKEN`. The glob allowance is *not* added here; `budget_cut` adds
   `TOKENS_PER_GLOB * len(union)` once over the union, as D8 writes it.
6. `group(members, area_depth)` — an edge when `footprint.overlaps(a.writes, b.writes)` is truthy
   or `set(shape.areas(a.writes, depth)) & set(shape.areas(b.writes, depth))` is non-empty
   (`shape.areas` returns `{area: [globs]}`, so a set over it is the area names); connected
   components, each in `(rank, id)` order, components of size 1 dropped.
7. `budget_cut(component, max_items, max_globs, max_tokens)` — walk in `(rank, id)` order keeping
   a member while all three ceilings hold; a member that would breach one is **skipped, not
   stopped on**, and a later smaller one is not pulled in ahead of it — so the walk keeps its
   order and simply drops what does not fit. Fewer than two fit → `None`. `max_items <= 0` →
   `None`, always (D9).
8. `delivery_proposals(canonical, conv, declined=None)` → `[shape.Proposal('deliver', ids, (), why)]`,
   the `why` in the spec's exact shape (`<n> small items in <area> (<k> overlap, <m> share the
   area); <g> globs, ~<t>k tokens: one plan and one coder instead of …`), dropping any whose
   `shape.proposal_key('deliver', ids)` appears in a named card's `reshape_declined`.
9. `deliveries(canonical)` → `{lead: [members]}` from `delivers:`; `releasable(canonical)` → D11/D12
   exactly as the spec's paragraph writes it, including the lead-is-the-open-one case that ends
   the delivery.
10. `tests/test_delivery.py` and `tests/test_conventions.py`.

**Gate**

```sh

python3 -m unittest tests.test_conventions -v
python3 tools/run_tests.py
bash tools/check_generic.sh
bash tools/check_conventions.sh
```
