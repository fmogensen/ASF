# Plan F-0279 — the brief stops asking for the beat, the refusal stops being a question, and the parks come back

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0279.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0279`.

Three Tasks, one per Story and one per section of the spec's `## The design`. Task 1 is the
**brief**: the local lane stops being handed a precondition its sandbox may refuse, and the one
loop block that remains — the Actions lane's — says in its own block that a refusal is
survivable. Task 2 is the **reading**: `report.needs_input` stops counting a heartbeat-start
refusal as a question, a new `report.declared_question` keeps the raw reading the relaunch cap's
mark depends on, and health's park is never reached. Task 3 is the **release**: the parks already
written come back on the next tick, with their own reason and no `asf unpark`.

Wave order: **(1, 2) → 3**.

The cut is the spec's own §1–§3, one section per Task, and the seam is the one the spec draws.
Task 1 and Task 2 share no file and no dependency: the brief's text and the report's reading are
two different ends of the same bug, and either is useful without the other. Task 3 waits on
Task 2 for two reasons that are both real rather than bookkeeping — `release_answered` only
reaches its release once `needs_input` is already `None` for such a run, and the `why` line it
gains calls `report.heartbeat_refused`, which does not exist in `report` until Task 2 moves it
there (**PD11**).

What the plan does **not** do is split Task 2. The spec's §2 touches three modules, and measuring
it here showed the three cannot land apart: narrowing `report.needs_input` on its own turns an
existing green test red (**PD3**). That measurement, and one more about *how* the narrowing must
be written (**PD4**), are the two things this plan adds to the spec — neither widens it.

## Decisions

Twelve rows. Two are measured findings that change how Task 2 must be built and are the reason
it is one Task (**PD3**, **PD4**). One records a red the trunk already carries in the fence the
spec names, so a coder can tell their own breakage from it (**PD5**), and one corrects a test
class the spec names and the tree does not have (**PD12**). The rest re-measure a
precondition against today's line numbers, or record a consequence the spec's acceptance lines do
not pin (**PD6**, **PD9**, **PD10**). None widens the card: each is the spec's own In, Out and
Choices carried out on the tree as it stands at `080cdf5a6`.
