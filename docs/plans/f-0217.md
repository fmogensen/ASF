# Plan F-0217 — the publish is a ledger fact, and every hold site hands the guard the trunk

Epic: E-0004 — Control plane — the factory's dashboard, served from the record. Spec:
`docs/specs/f-0217.md` (approved). Branch: `plan/F-0217`.

**Two Tasks.**

1. **The record and its reader** (S-37950, S-37951) — `lifecycle.published_sha`, health's three
   resume points writing `published_head`, and `same_head_loop`'s third escape reading it. These
   two Stories are one mechanism and are landed together (PD1).
2. **Every hold site hands the guard the trunk** (S-37952) — the five call-site edits and the AST
   invariant that refuses a sixth site without `main=`. `after: Task 1`.

Wave order: **1** → Task 1, `after: none`, starts at once. **2** → Task 2, `after: Task 1`. The two
`writes:` sets intersect on `asf/workers/health.py` and `tests/test_lifecycle.py`, so Task 2 would
queue behind Task 1 whatever it declared; `after: Task 1` states the order rather than leaving it
to the queue (PD1).

**Read PD2 before writing the comparison in `same_head_loop`** — it is the one line in this card
that can silently disarm the guard on *every* publish, and the spec's own rejected option (c) is
how. **Read PD3 before choosing where `before = ev.remote_sha` goes** (two of the three resume
points rebind `ev` between the gather and the yield), **PD4 before building a pre-push hook for the
refusal test**, and **PD5 before building a git fixture for `SameHeadLoopGuard`** — it needs none.

### Task 1: a factory publish is recorded on the run it belongs to, and the loop guard counts it
stories: S-37950, S-37951
writes: asf/workers/lifecycle.py, asf/workers/health.py, tests/test_lifecycle.py, tests/test_workers.py
after: none

The spec's `## The design` §1 and §2 in full, and nothing else. After this lands, a health pass
that publishes a session's commits writes the sha it put on `origin/<branch>` onto that run's
ledger line, and `same_head_loop` reads it as what it is: a branch that moved. The F-0003 park is
not written — with no worktree left to read, and no caller needing to have remembered an argument.

Three things are load-bearing, each with a precondition behind it. The sha comes from
**`ev.remote_sha` through `published_sha`, never from the publish line** (PD2) — the line's sha is
nine characters and the comparison is against a forty-character `launch_head`, so parsing it would
make every publish look like progress and disarm the guard outright. `before` is read on the
statement **immediately above each `yield`** (PD3), because two of the three resume points rebind
`ev` through `file_review` in between. And the guard's test is a **comparison, not a presence
test** (C4): a publish that pushed the very head the launches were read on moved nothing.

Read **PD2** before writing the comparison, **PD3** before placing the three `before` captures,
**PD5** before building any git fixture for `SameHeadLoopGuard`, and **PD1** if the two Stories on
this line look like they should have been two Tasks.

**Files**

- `asf/workers/lifecycle.py` — `published_sha(before, after, line)` is **added** after `publish`
  ends at `:1792` and before the `REBASE_CONFLICT_RE` comment at `:1795`, exactly as §1 writes it,
  docstring included. In `same_head_loop`, §2's two lines are **inserted** directly after
  `sha = heads.pop()` (`:2380`) and directly before `if head and head != sha` (`:2381`), and the
  docstring (`:2356-2366`) gains §2's third paragraph in the voice of the other two. `RUN_FIELDS`

4. **The ordinary end-of-run pass** (`:717-733`) — `before = ev.remote_sha` directly above the
