# Plan F-0307 — stability first: the freeze, the declaration that escapes it, the line that reports it

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0307.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0307`.

Two Tasks. Task 1 is the **declaration**: `stability` joins `SETTABLE['feature']`,
`SETTABLE['epic']` and `BOOL_FIELDS` in `asf/record/new.py`, which buys `asf set`'s
`true`/`false` refusal and `asf check`'s type validation from machinery that already exists
(spec P17). Task 2 is the **gate and its report**: `stability_first` and its four helpers in
`asf/feeder/rows.py`, wired into `plan_rows` ahead of `build_cap`; `Row.s1` and `gate()`'s count
off the demand pass in `asf/feeder/tiers.py`; the new `tests/test_s1_feature_gate.py`; and the 13
cases of P21 rewritten in place.

Wave order: **1 → 2**.

The cut is forced, not chosen, and PD1 shows the arithmetic. The spec's own design is four files
whose halves cannot land apart — "without `Row.s1` and `gate()`'s count, the `S1 gate:` line reads
`None` while the gate holds every Feature in the product" — and the 13 cases of P21 each assert the
behaviour this card reverses, so they must move in the same commit or the trunk goes red. What *is*
separable is the card field: it has its own acceptance bullet, its own test modules, and a
footprint disjoint from the feeder's. Task 2 waits on it because one of Task 2's own acceptance
cases asserts that `asf new --set stability=yes` is refused — Task 1's surface, read not written.

The defect is reproduced at this plan's base, so the card is not taken on the spec's word (PD3):
with a live session on the S1 Bug and 11 seats, `plan_rows` still returns **10 `CARD → SPEC` rows
reading `would launch`**, and `tiers.gate_line` reads **`None`**. P8 and P11, measured here.
