# Plan F-0317 — one constant first, the three readers together, the brief in parallel

Epic: E-0004 — Control plane — the factory's dashboard, served from the record. Spec:
`docs/specs/f-0317.md` (approved). Branch: `plan/F-0317`.

Three Tasks. Task 1 is the **constant and its predicate** (spec §1): `REPLAN_SUFFIX` minted in one
place and `is_replan_branch` reading it back, which lands green with nothing reading it yet
(S-102505). Task 2 is the **three readers in one diff** (§2, §3, §4): `landed_doc`'s early return,
the correction row's `brief_kind`, and the lane's send-back note — together, because measured, §2
without §3 briefs a `plan` session onto the replan branch and §3's own acceptance class cannot pass
without §2 (**PD2**), so they are not separable in either order (S-102506, S-102507). Task 3 is the
**replan brief** (§5): the kind list, the Feature twin of the numbered Story renderer, the task
line's story ids, the template sentence and the regenerated golden (S-102508).

Wave order: **(1, 3) → 2**, two waves. Tasks 1 and 3 have disjoint `writes:` and no edge between
them, so they run together in wave 1. Task 2 waits on Task 1 by `after:` — it calls
`is_replan_branch` — and it would queue behind it anyway, since both write `asf/feeder/rows.py` and
`tests/test_feeder.py` (**PD11**). Task 3 shares no path with either and is on nobody's `after:`:
its own landing changes every replan brief for the better and changes no row, so it is safe in
front of the fix as well as behind it.

The plan carries the spec's scope and no more. Four rows below correct it on a point of fact: its
§2/§3 cannot be cut apart the way its Stories are numbered (**PD2**), two of its fences do not
reach the behaviour their Story's acceptance lines name (**PD4**, **PD5**), and six of its line
anchors have drifted off this branch's base while every content reading holds (**PD3**). None of the
four widens the work.
