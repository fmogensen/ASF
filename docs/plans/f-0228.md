# Plan F-0228 — a refused publish is remembered by the pair it was refused at, and every refusal leaves a hold

Epic: E-0002 — ASF 0.2 — self-improvement. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0228.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0228`.

**One Task, carrying all three Stories.** The spec says why in its own words — *"So the skip and
the hold are one change, and the card asks for both"* — and the footprint says it again: every
split of this card puts two Tasks in the same three files. The skip lives in `republish_steps`; the
hold lives eleven lines below it in the same function; the fact both read lives in `Evidence`; the
predicates they share live in `health.py` beside `refusal_text`. Two Tasks whose `writes:`
intersect are run one after the other anyway, so a split buys no wall clock and costs the one thing
that matters: a build in which the skip has landed and the hold has not is the silent dead end the
spec's own preamble says must never exist — commits in a worktree, no push, no hold, no session,
nothing printed. See **PD10**.

**Read PD2 before writing the `tests/test_workers.py` block.** The spec's acceptance snippet for
that file asserts a value the product it specifies will not write, and its stated reason is false:
the run's branch in that fixture is *not* on origin. It was measured here, not reasoned about. The
corrected assertion is in PD2 and in the Task's Acceptance.

Then read **PD3** (`publish_gap` is shared with the live end-of-run path, so D4's re-gather reaches
it too — and what that does and does not change), **PD5** (the pair is two tokens and stays two:
what leaving `origin/<main>` out trades, and what bounds it) and **PD6** (the ladder reads the text
after `refused: ` for its class and the whole line for a rebase conflict — that asymmetry is the
live path's own and must be copied, not unified).

### Task 1: the pair a refusal is remembered by, the skip it earns, and the hold that keeps it from being a dead end
stories: S-37450, S-37451, S-37452
writes: asf/workers/lifecycle.py, asf/workers/health.py, tests/test_health_republish_retry.py, tests/test_workers.py
after: none

The spec's design whole — its §1, §2 and §3, and all four of its acceptance blocks. When this
lands, a publish the product's pre-push hook refused is remembered by the `(local head, origin
tip)` pair it was refused at and is not made again while both halves read the same and the worktree
is clean; either half moving forgets it and the publish is attempted on that tick; a transport blip
is never remembered; and every newly recorded refusal that no pending correction already carries
leaves one hold, routed by the refusal's own class, carrying the refusal's own reason.

Read **PD2** first — the spec's `tests/test_workers.py` assertion is wrong and the Task is judged on
getting that block right — then **PD3** (why `publish_gap`'s one-line change is safe for the live
path the spec puts Out), **PD4** (why only one of `publish_gap`'s two refusal exits is re-gathered),
**PD5** (two tokens, never three), **PD6** (`why` for the class, `line` for the rebase test), **PD7**
(the `UNPUSHED` arm keeps its precedence) and **PD8** (the guard goes after `gather`).
