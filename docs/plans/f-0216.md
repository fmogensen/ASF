# Plan F-0216 — the exception list, the default, and the bypass the open lane took away

Epic: E-0003 — ASF 0.3 — autonomous delivery. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0216.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0216`.

**Three Tasks, three waves, run 1 → 2 → 3.** They serialise by construction, not by choice: Tasks 1
and 2 both write `asf/workers/cloud.py` and `tests/test_cloud.py`; Tasks 2 and 3 both write
`asf/workers/wave.py`, `asf/workers/spawn.py` and `asf/tick/step_wave.py`. The order is also the
order the card reads in: the floor has to exist before the default can be flipped onto it (a
cloud-first default over today's allow-list would send a `groom` row to a runner that throws its
answers away), and the S1 bypass only becomes reachable once the lane is open often enough to
matter.

**Read PD1, PD4 and PD6 before writing a line of any Task.** Three things in the spec do not survive
contact with this checkout. PD1: C5 does not move "four existing assertions" — it breaks **13 tests
in `tests/test_cloud.py`**, and one fixture line fixes 12 of them; a coder who edits the 13 by hand
ships a suite that no longer tests overflow at all. PD4: the S1 bypass tests are **not** in
`tests/test_host_pressure.py` where the spec sends them — they are in `tests/test_tick_steps.py`,
and **three of the spec's five already exist there**, so Task 3 adds one wave-step test, not five.
PD6: `local_cause`'s own fenced acceptance test contradicts its own call site, and the fix is one
normalising line.

Every measurement below was taken in this worktree at `20114aab6` — ten commits past the spec's
`7fbbfdba9` — and each says how it was taken. PD14 is the re-read of every precondition.
