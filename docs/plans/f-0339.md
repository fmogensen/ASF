# Plan F-0339 — one Task, because the two classes share six of seven paths and two fences

Epic: E-0002 — ASF 0.2 — self-improvement. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0339.md` (approved, landed at
`3bd5a6a0e`). Branch: `plan/F-0339`, cut from `origin/main` at `99b0b812c`.

**One Task, one wave.** The spec builds two session-question classes in the kernel's resolvers —
`pushed` (pure, read off facts the host already holds) and `trunk-base` (one refs-only probe) — and
the two are not separable: measured, their diffs share **six of the seven files** the change
touches, and two of the Feature's four Stories (`S-125107`, `S-125108`) carry fences that span both
classes, so no cut leaves two Tasks that can each pass their own acceptance (**PD1**).

Wave order: **1**. One wave, one Task. Nothing waits on anything and nothing runs beside it.

The plan carries the spec's scope and no more. Thirteen rows below correct or complete it, and the
corrections are facts, not widenings. The two that change what a coder writes first: the footprint
is **seven** files, not §4's five, because each knob needs a `Config` field and the line in
`ports.py` that fills it (**PD2**, measured); and `REWRITTEN_RE`'s first alternative never matches
the one real question text of its class, because the trunk word comes *before* the verb there
(**PD3**, measured). Two more bound what the Feature can claim: of the twelve records §1.1 counts
behind the two classes, **three** carry a question text at all — the other nine were answered off
the card, so no fixture can quote them (**PD4**); and `ON_ORIGIN`, the third `pushed` answer, is
reachable end-to-end only from a report that is *not* `done`, because a `done` report whose push
landed never becomes a Stuck — `decide.done_and_pushed` already moves that work on (**PD7**).
