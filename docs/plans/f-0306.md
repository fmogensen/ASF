# Plan F-0306 — rehearse the release: the snapshot, the runner, the acts, the edge

Epic: E-0001 — ASF 0.1 — a working factory. Spec: `docs/specs/f-0306.md` (approved). Branch:
`plan/F-0306`.

Seven Tasks. Task 1 is the **snapshot**: the builder half of `asf/rehearsal.py` (`build`,
`manifest_holds`, the filler), `asf rehearse --build` so the Story's own command exists, and the
committed `tests/rehearsal/` the builder writes from the live record. Task 2 is the **runner**:
`lay`, `rehearse`, the `ACTS` tuple, the `manifest` act and the `ids` act, plus the rest of the
command's flags. Tasks 3 and 4 are the two remaining acts — `record` and `tags` — one each. Task 5
is the **criterion** in both gates, independent of every other Task. Task 6 is the **edge**: the
`rehearsal` job and the two `needs:` lines. Task 7 is the one line that makes the edge bite, and it
lands only on a trunk where `asf rehearse` is already green (**PD13**).

The cut is the spec's own §1–§5, one section per Task, with the four acts split act-by-act rather
than shipped together: each act is a separate end-to-end exercise with its own red, its own test
module and its own fenced acceptance, and a single Task carrying three of them could not be
reviewed for any one of them. The `hooks` act of §2 is **not** on any Task — it has no Story card
(**PD11**), and the spec forbids a Task claiming it.

Wave order: **(1, 5) → 2 → (3, 4, 6) → 7**.

Task 1 and Task 5 are genuinely parallel: Task 1 writes `asf/rehearsal.py`, `asf/cli.py`,
`asf/surface.py`, `tests/test_surface.py`, `tests/test_rehearsal_snapshot.py` and
`tests/rehearsal/**`; Task 5 writes `asf/release.py`, `asf/release_preview.py`,
`docs/products.example.yaml` and `tests/test_release_rehearsal_criterion.py`. Disjoint on both
sides. Tasks 3 and 4 each intersect Task 2 and each other on `asf/rehearsal.py`, so the three of
them are queued one after another whatever their `after:` says — that is accepted, not worked
around (**PD14**): the spec names one module, and splitting it into three would buy parallelism at
the price of the seam the spec does not draw. Task 6 is disjoint from 3 and 4 and runs beside them.
